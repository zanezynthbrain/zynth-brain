# ZYNTH Service Package Architecture

## Commercial Positioning

ZYNTH should not sell a generic list of social posts, ads, events, and videos. The commercial offer should be built around the client decision that needs to change: **clarify, launch, grow, convert, become culturally relevant, create an experience, or enter a market**. The package creates a clear starting point; the full proposal then expands into the complete creative, operational, financial, and measurement system required for that decision.

The package architecture is deliberately modular. It prevents under-scoping but does not constrain agent creativity: every package can gain an approved specialist module for research, creators, media, 3D, video, events, technology, sponsorship, retail, public relations, or dual-market execution.

## Package Logic and Full-Proposal Requirements

### 01. The Clarity Sprint

The Clarity Sprint is the recommended entry product for ambiguous requests. It turns a raw idea, lead, or problem into a paid decision product rather than providing free consulting through a pitch. The output includes a business challenge statement, known/unknown evidence, audience and competitive hypotheses, three possible growth/creative routes, a budget/effort map, risk, priority actions, and a recommendation for the next package.

### 02. Brand Foundation

Brand Foundation gives clients the strategy and creative system needed before they buy tactics. It includes brand/audience diagnosis, positioning options, messaging architecture, creative direction, visual/voice system, foundational templates, governance, and a launch or adoption plan. A full rebrand needs a separate production and implementation scope.

### 03. Growth Engine

Growth Engine is an outcome-led retainer, not a fixed post count. It includes a defined monthly capacity plus a quarterly growth review. It should be sold only when the client agrees to shared decision rights, data access, approval turnaround, account ownership, and measurement definitions. The retainer may use a portfolio of social, paid media, SEO/GEO, email, CRM, conversion, retail, content, community, and sales-enablement modules based on the actual growth constraint.

### 04. Launch Room

Launch Room starts from the launch decision and turns it into a full 360 system. It includes a distinctive campaign title and concept, audience insight, launch sequence, brand/product truth, key visual/creative direction, message hierarchy, channel roles, product/retail/ecommerce readiness, sales and partner enablement, content/creator/PR/media plan, launch moment, tracking, risk, timeline, and complete itemised budget. It is never merely a “launch key visual.”

### 05. Social Culture Studio

Social Culture Studio turns social from a calendar into a platform. It creates the brand’s social role, audience communities, repeatable content worlds, creator/UGC logic, reactive/response guardrails, production rhythm, rights, amplification, and learning system. It can include TikTok challenges or commerce only after a mechanics, moderation, rights, prize, inventory, platform, and safety review.

### 06. Experience & Event Lab

Experience & Event Lab is built for real-world impact. Every event proposal contains business objective, audience, creative title/concept, guest journey, format/programme, spatial/stage/technical approach, content, staffing/RACI, vendor and permit plan, bottom-up budget, sponsor/ticketing model if applicable, safety/risk, KPI/ROI, and post-event continuation. No event concept is client-ready until it could be run responsibly on the day.

### 07. Content and Motion Studio

Content and Motion Studio produces client-ready creative assets with appropriate pre-production, rights, source-file, technical, and delivery discipline. Depending on scope, it can include campaign visuals, copy, photography, film, social video, animation, AI-assisted image/video, or Blender/3D spatial design. It must deliver a production brief, approved treatment, technical specification, revision limits, QC checklist, and final asset/source-file register.

### 08. Regional Growth Bridge

Regional Growth Bridge is the premium Myanmar/Singapore capability. It avoids the false assumption that one market’s messaging, media, pricing, community, regulations, or production method transfers directly to the other. Its deliverable is a cross-market decision plan—what must remain consistent, what must localise, what partner capability is needed, which market launches first, and how success will be measured.

## Commercial Rules That Protect ZYNTH and the Client

| Rule | ZYNTH policy |
| --- | --- |
| Professional thinking | Discovery and strategy are paid, with the depth of work matched to the decision value. |
| Scope discipline | Every package has explicit outputs, capacity, included revisions, client inputs, and a written change-order process. |
| Price transparency | Agency professional fees, production, paid media, supplier costs, taxes, and contingency are distinct lines. |
| Margin protection | ZYNTH prices from cost plus a founder-approved contribution margin. Discounts change scope, timing, term, or payment—not the hidden quality of delivery. |
| Currency control | Myanmar long retainers have a documented review/FX mechanism. Regional/MNC costs use agreed currency rules and dated conversion basis. |
| Payment discipline | Project work begins after a deposit; retainers are paid in advance or by documented milestones. |
| Rights and data | Client owns its accounts and approved final assets according to the contract; creator, stock, music, AI, source-file, and data rights are recorded explicitly. |
| Performance integrity | ZYNTH commits to disciplined execution, experimentation, transparency, and learning—not unsupported sales or reach guarantees. |

## Business-Development Plan

### Target Segments

ZYNTH should prioritise sectors that benefit from a combination of market intelligence, creative, experiential execution, and measurable demand: financial services/fintech, FMCG/beverage, healthcare, hospitality/real estate, retail/ecommerce, B2B/industrial/logistics, technology/education, and culture/entertainment. The first account list should be tiered by strategic fit, accessible decision maker, live trigger, potential lifetime value, ZYNTH proof relevance, and ability to pay for complete work.

### The ZYNTH New-Business Flywheel

| Stage | ZYNTH action | Founder decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Detect | Agents identify sector moments, launches, festivals, product/category signals, account changes, and public opportunities | Which 3–5 opportunities merit deeper research? |
| Diagnose | Build a one-page account/sector brief, evidence log, buying trigger, and probable constraint | Does this deserve a Clarity Sprint or proprietary concept? |
| Create value first | Produce a specific insight, a short opportunity memo, or one tailored creative territory | Approve any external outreach or use of client name/logo |
| Convert | Offer paid discovery, workshop, or Clarity Sprint—not unpaid comprehensive consulting | Approve commercial terms and named proposal owner |
| Mobilise | Produce full client-grade proposal after discovery or confirmed brief | Approve final release, budget, and contract route |
| Learn | Capture win/loss reason, scope/margin result, creative performance, and next sector play | Scale, revise, or retire the package hypothesis |

### First Ninety Days

In the first 30 days, ZYNTH should build 40 target account cards across the priority sectors, create eight sector opportunity briefs, finalise the offer sheets and proposal standard, prepare three client-safe proof pieces, and run weekly founder pipeline reviews. In days 31–60, the objective is to run discovery conversations and paid workshops, generate two full proposals from validated briefs, and establish a partner/RFQ map. In days 61–90, ZYNTH should review conversion, price resistance, sales-cycle delay, margin, channel response, and package adoption before changing the offer architecture.
