# ZYNTH-MM-LAU-001 — The Reveal Spine
## Spatial Technical Pack V01

**Status:** Internal concept / client-reviewable after technical validation  
**Units:** Millimetres unless otherwise stated  
**Design assumption:** Indoor product-launch environment; product geometry, venue plan and approved brand inputs not supplied

## Spatial Intent

The environment guides guests through orientation, transformation, proof and invitation before delivering a camera-ready hero reveal. The linear spine of light is the navigational and emotional device. The product position remains a neutral placeholder until verified dimensions, handling requirements and approved identity are supplied.

## Guest Flow

Guests should understand the entrance within seconds and retain an unobstructed path toward the final reveal. The transformation zone may create a pause but must not form a queue obstruction. A separate exit and staff route should be defined. Media and creator capture positions should be identified before final product placement.

## Product and Reveal Assumptions

The current render contains a neutral placeholder and does not confirm the final product’s size, weight, heat, security, handling, material, finish, packaging or power requirement. Any product that is fragile, high-value, temperature-sensitive or technically active requires a dedicated product-handling and security review.

## Sightlines and Cameras

Required views are entry, transformation, proof, reveal axis, audience and plan/orthographic. The reveal axis must remain clear of staff and crowd obstruction during the cue. The product should be framed at a camera height that works for press, social capture and audience eye level without making an unsupported claim through lighting or scale.

## Accessibility and Inclusion

The journey should remain step-free or have a documented accessible alternative. Information surfaces must be readable and available in the required languages. If the transformation zone uses sound, provide a low-sensory or captioned route. The final reveal must be visible from an accessible viewing position.

## AV, Lighting and Safety Assumptions

The concept assumes controlled lighting, a timed reveal cue, optional projection/LED, audio and a protected product plinth. Electrical load, heat, cable routes, backup mode, crowd barriers, fire/egress and venue restrictions are unverified. A low-technology fallback should be planned: physical aperture, manual light cue and approved printed/physical proof surface.

## Materials and Finish Direction

Dark matte scenic surfaces, translucent fins, warm timber detail, low-glare product plinth and warm-to-cool lighting are proposed. Final finishes must be selected against product reflection, photography, fire rating, handling, cleaning, transport and reuse requirements.
