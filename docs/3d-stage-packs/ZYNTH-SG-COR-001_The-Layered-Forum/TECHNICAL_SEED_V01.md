# ZYNTH-SG-COR-001 — The-Layered-Forum — Singapore hybrid leadership forum
## Spatial Technical Seed V01

**Status:** Internal Concept; site and engineering data not supplied  
**Units:** Millimetres unless otherwise stated  

## Technical Intent

This seed records the first feasibility questions for the concept. It is not a construction drawing, structural sign-off, vendor RFQ or production-ready specification. Dimensions, materials, power, AV, rigging, fire/egress and accessibility must be verified against the named venue and programme.

Broadcast exposure, latency, platform compatibility, captioning, privacy, remote-speaker reliability, audio, lighting and backup modes.
