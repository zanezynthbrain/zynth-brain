# ZYNTH-MM-COR-001 — The Living Forum
## Spatial Technical Pack V01

**Status:** Internal concept / client-reviewable after technical validation  
**Units:** Millimetres unless otherwise stated  
**Design assumption:** Indoor corporate conference venue; venue plan and production brief not supplied

## Spatial Intent

The stage is a flexible forum that supports keynote, panel, awards and audience-participation modes. Its central platform, layered content wall and overhead frame should create one coherent camera and audience composition while preserving speaker access, backstage operations and safe guest movement.

## Stage Modes

Keynote mode uses the central platform and the evidence wall. Panel mode adds low-profile seating without blocking captions. Awards mode opens a clear centreline and strengthens the reveal lighting. Town-hall mode can reduce furniture and extend audience-facing light into the room. Each mode requires a run-of-show and cue sheet.

## Sightlines and Cameras

Required views are hero wide from centre rear, speaker camera, panel camera, audience perspective and plan/orthographic. The content wall must remain readable from the defined seating arc. Side portals must not block a speaker’s face or create distracting visual bands. Camera height, lens, target and screen brightness must be reviewed by the production and broadcast teams.

## Accessibility and Inclusion

Stage access should be step-free where possible or include a compliant ramp/lift route. Captioning, translation and high-contrast content must be planned as part of the design rather than added after the visual is approved. Seating, aisle and stage clearances require venue and accessibility review.

## AV, Lighting and Rigging Assumptions

The concept assumes a rear LED, projection or mixed display system, distributed stage lighting, audio reinforcement, captioning/translation feed and optional camera capture. Electrical load, cable routes, rigging, screen brightness, acoustic treatment and backup power are unverified. Any overhead frame must be checked by a qualified rigging/venue supplier.

## Materials and Finish Direction

The current concept uses dark scenic surfaces, translucent vertical fins, warm timber or matte stage finish, low-glare content surfaces and warm/cyan light. Final material choices must consider camera reflection, fire rating, transport, reusability, cleaning and venue restrictions.

## Operational Dependencies

The producer and show caller need a mode-by-mode run-of-show. The technical director needs screen, lighting, audio, camera and power information. The stage manager needs backstage, speaker and award-object flow. The venue needs structural, fire, egress and accessibility review. These dependencies are not confirmed by the concept render.
