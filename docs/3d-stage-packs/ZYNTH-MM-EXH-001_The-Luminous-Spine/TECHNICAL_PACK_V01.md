# ZYNTH-MM-EXH-001 — The Luminous Spine
## Spatial Technical Pack V01

**Status:** Internal concept / client-reviewable after technical validation  
**Units:** Millimetres unless otherwise stated  
**Design assumption:** Modular indoor trade-show booth; actual venue and hall rules not supplied

## Spatial Intent

The booth is organised as a visible-to-qualified-conversation funnel: aisle beacon, orientation threshold, proof window, live demo island, meeting pods and concealed service wall. The concept is not a construction drawing and does not confirm structural, fire, electrical, rigging or venue compliance.

## Guest Flow

The preferred route is open entry from the aisle, rapid orientation within 10 seconds, optional proof viewing, demo dwell and qualified meeting. No wall or furniture should create a dead end. A second route must remain available for visitors who want to exit without entering the meeting zone.

## Sightlines and Cameras

The minimum view set is an aisle-facing hero view, a visitor-eye-level demo view, a meeting-pod privacy view and a plan/orthographic view. The demo screen must be visible from the aisle without blocking the host or product. Meeting pods should not be visually exposed to the full aisle, but staff must retain operational sightline.

## Accessibility and Inclusion

The threshold must remain step-free. The 1500 mm circulation assumption is provisional and requires accessibility review against the venue and local requirements. Demo surfaces, registration and meeting positions should include an accessible approach. If audio content is used, captions and a readable alternate route are required.

## Power, AV and Rigging Assumptions

The concept assumes distributed power to the service wall, demo island and display/light spine. Final electrical load, cable routes, protective covers and venue supply must be calculated by the technical supplier. The luminous spine may be floor-supported or suspended; the current design does not determine which route is permitted. LED, projection or transparent-display proof windows remain alternatives pending venue and content review.

## Materials and Finish Direction

Primary materials are dark powder-coated metal, translucent polycarbonate or acrylic fins, warm timber veneer, low-glare display surfaces and replaceable light channels. Fire rating, recycled content, cleaning, transport, reuse and end-of-life requirements must be confirmed with the fabricator.

## Required Vendor / Engineer Questions

The eventual RFQ must request venue plan and hall height, loading and install windows, permitted hanging loads, power availability, fire and material rules, booth build restrictions, screen/AV requirements, storage, cleaning, dismantling and comparative fabrication costs. A qualified engineer or venue-approved supplier must review any structure, rigging, electrical or fire-sensitive element.
