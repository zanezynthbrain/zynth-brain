# ZYNTH PR & Communications Operating System

Public relations for ZYNTH and its clients across Myanmar + Singapore — **earned
reputation, media relationships, and crisis-readiness**. PR is a trust business: slower
than ads, but it buys credibility ads can't. AI drafts and manages; the MD approves
anything that goes public.

---

## 1. What this department owns
- **Media relations:** press releases, media lists, journalist/editor relationships, pitches.
- **Earned coverage:** securing features, interviews, and mentions.
- **Corporate comms:** announcements, thought leadership, executive positioning, awards.
- **Reputation & crisis:** monitoring, holding statements, issue management.
- **PR events:** press conferences, launches, media hosting (with the Events system).

## 2. What it produces (real deliverables, filed)
- A **press release** (ready to send) and its tailored **media list**.
- A **pitch** per target outlet (angle written for that editor, not a blast).
- A **PR plan** for a launch/campaign (angles, timeline, targets, assets).
- **Thought-leadership** pieces + executive bylines.
- A **crisis playbook** and, when needed, a holding statement within the hour.
- A monthly **coverage report** (pickups, reach, sentiment, earned-media value).

## 3. The PR workflow (angle → pitch → coverage)
1. **Find the angle** — what's genuinely newsworthy (a first, a number, a human story,
   a trend the client can own). No angle → no pitch; manufacture a moment instead.
2. **Build the assets** — press release (inverted pyramid), quote, fact sheet, images.
3. **Target** — the *right* journalists/outlets for the angle (MM: Myanmar Times/Frontier/
   Irrawaddy-type business & tech desks, industry pages; SG: business/tech/marketing
   trade press). Pitch each individually.
4. **Pitch & follow up** — personalised subject + 3-line pitch + the hook; follow up once.
5. **Amplify** — pickups feed the social/content engine; log earned-media value.
6. **Report** — pickups, reach, sentiment, share of voice vs competitors.

## 4. The press-release SOP (structure that gets used)
Headline (specific, active) → dateline → strong lead (who/what/why-it-matters in one
sentence) → supporting paragraphs (facts, then colour) → a real quote → boilerplate ("About
[client]") → contact. Bilingual where the outlet needs it (Burmese Unicode/Pyidaungsu +
English). One page. No adjectives doing a fact's job.

## 5. Crisis / issues SOP (calm, fast, honest)
1. **Assess** — what happened, who's affected, how far it's spread (monitor first).
2. **Hold** — a holding statement within the hour: acknowledge, state what you're doing,
   commit to an update. Never speculate, never lie, never go silent.
3. **Escalate to the MD immediately** — crisis response is always an owner-decision.
4. **Resolve** — factual update, correction if needed, and a quiet fix of the root cause.
5. **Learn** — log it to the self-improvement loop so the trigger can't recur.

## 6. Metrics that matter
Quality pickups (tier of outlet × relevance) · total reach · sentiment · share of voice ·
earned-media value (coverage valued vs equivalent ad spend) · relationships built.

## 7. Tools & skills it works through
`zynth-copywriter` (releases, bylines) · `zynth-account-manager` (client comms) ·
`zynth-competitor-analyst` + `zynth-market-researcher` (angles, share of voice) ·
`zynth-event-manager` / Event system (press events). Never fabricate a quote, a
journalist contact, or coverage — unknown = "to source", real relationships only.

## 8. What escalates to the MD (only these)
Any crisis/issue (always) · any statement or release before it goes public · an on-record
executive comment. Everything else — drafts, media lists, pitches, reports — is produced
and filed.
