# ZYNTH HR Operating System

**For a founder with zero HR experience.** This is the whole people function as plain
steps and ready-to-use templates, for Myanmar + Singapore. Follow it and ZYNTH hires,
onboards, pays, and keeps people properly. The HR agent builds on this file and never
contradicts it.

Right now ZYNTH is AI-first with no human employees. This system is built **before**
you need it, so the day the first person joins, everything is ready.

---

## 1. The org today, and the first hires
- **Today:** you (MD) + the AI departments. That's a real operating structure.
- **First human hires, in likely order** (hire only when the work is repeatable and
  the revenue supports it):
  1. **Account/Project Manager** (Yangon) — owns client delivery & timelines. Usually
     the first hire, because it frees you and protects client relationships.
  2. **Junior designer / content producer** — executes creative volume.
  3. **BD/Sales** — when the pipeline is bigger than you can close.
- Hire when a role would clearly pay for itself within ~2–3 months of revenue it
  enables. Not before.

## 2. Hiring SOP (the steps, in order)
1. **Write the JD** (template §3). Be specific about outcomes, not just tasks.
2. **Set the salary band** from the market table (§7). Decide the range before you
   interview so you don't overpay under pressure.
3. **Source:** referrals first (best hires in MM/SG come via network), then JobNet /
   Facebook groups (MM) or MyCareersFuture / LinkedIn (SG).
4. **Screen:** a 15-min call — do they get the work, are they reliable, do they fit.
5. **Test:** one small paid or realistic task. Never hire on interview alone.
6. **Interview:** 30–45 min, structured (same questions for every candidate).
7. **Offer:** send the offer letter (§4). Agree start date.
8. **Contract:** signed employment agreement (§5) before day one.
9. **Onboard:** run the checklist (§6).

## 5. Employment agreement — the clauses you must include
Use a local lawyer to finalise, but every ZYNTH contract covers:
- Parties, role, start date, reporting line.
- Compensation, pay date, probation (3 months), working hours/location.
- **Leave** (per §8), public holidays (MM or SG calendar).
- **Confidentiality** (client data, ZYNTH methods — important for an AI-run agency).
- **IP ownership** — all work produced belongs to ZYNTH/its clients.
- Notice period (e.g. 1 month after probation), grounds for termination.
- Governing law (Myanmar or Singapore per where they're employed).
> `[Get one MM template and one SG template lawyer-checked once; reuse forever.]`

## 6. Onboarding checklist (day 1 → week 2)
**Before day 1:** contract signed · workspace/tools/logins ready · added to Telegram
+ Drive · first-week plan written.
**Day 1:** welcome + how ZYNTH works (AI-run, you + agents) · brand & voice
(`brand.py` / knowledge) · who to ask for what · first small real task.
**Week 1:** shadow a live project · read the relevant SOPs/skills · daily 5-min check-in.
**Week 2:** owns a small deliverable end-to-end · feedback both ways.
**End of probation (month 3):** honest review vs the JD's 90-day success measures →
confirm, extend, or part ways.

## 8. Leave policy (adopt as-is, adjust numbers to taste)
- **Annual leave:** 14 days/year (accrues monthly; unused caps at 1.25× annual).
- **Sick leave:** MM ~ up to 30 days/yr with medical cert per local norms; SG per MOM
  (up to 14 outpatient / 60 hospitalisation after 6 months). `[confirm current]`
- **Public holidays:** per the employee's country calendar.
- **Unpaid leave:** by agreement.
- **How to request:** message the MD/AM at least 3 working days ahead; logged in the
  HR sheet. The HR agent tracks balances.

## 9. Payroll link (finance owns the money; HR owns the record)
For each person keep: full name, role, start date, monthly rate, pay date, bank/KBZPay/
Wave details, statutory contributions, leave balance. Payroll pays from this; it must
always match Finance's "Money Out → payroll" log. (See `FINANCE_operating_system.md`.)

## 10. Performance — light but real
- **Weekly:** 5-min check-in per person (what shipped, what's blocked).
- **Monthly:** 20-min 1:1 — one thing going well, one to improve, any support needed.
- **Quarterly:** review vs the role's outcomes; adjust pay/scope if earned.
- Keep it simple and honest. Small teams die from unspoken problems, not from missing
  a formal appraisal form.

## 11. Culture (what keeps good people at an AI-run agency)
- **Clarity:** everyone knows what they own and how success is measured.
- **Craft:** ZYNTH's standard is sharp, intelligent work — protect it, celebrate it.
- **Speed with sanity:** fast for clients (48h proposals), but no chaos — the SOPs and
  AI carry the load so humans aren't burned out.
- **AI + human, not AI vs human:** people do judgement, relationships, and taste; the
  agents do volume and structure. Say this out loud so no one fears the AI.

## 12. Contractors & freelancers (most of your production)
- Use a simple **contractor agreement** (scope, fee, deadline, IP to ZYNTH, payment
  terms) — not an employment contract.
- Pay per deliverable, on the same 50%/50% or on-delivery logic; log in Finance.
- Keep a rated list of go-to freelancers per skill (links to the vendor knowledge:
  `06_vendors_myanmar.md`, `07_vendors_singapore.md`).

## 13. What the HR agent escalates to you (and nothing else)
- A **hire decision** (yes/no on a candidate, after it's done the screening + test).
- A **pay change** or a **termination**.
- A **people problem** that needs the owner.
Everything else — JDs, offer letters, contracts, onboarding, leave tracking, the
handbook — it writes and files, ready to use. You decide; you don't draft.
