# ZYNTH Research & Analysis Operating System

The intelligence function — the department that makes every other department **right**.
It supplies the market truth behind strategy, the competitor intel behind BD, the
audience insight behind creative, and the performance read behind decisions. Its
standard: **evidence over assertion, and every number sourced or tagged.**

---

## 1. What this department owns
- **Market research:** category size, trends, dynamics, opportunity (MM + SG).
- **Competitor intelligence:** named-rival deep dives, positioning, share of voice.
- **Audience insight:** segments, psychographics, media behaviour, the sharp insight.
- **Data & performance analysis:** campaign/business numbers → what they mean → what to do.
- **The knowledge base:** keeping ZYNTH's facts current and feeding every agent.

## 2. What it produces (real deliverables, filed)
- A **market brief** (landscape, trends, opportunity, sources) for a pitch or a sector.
- A **competitor audit** (3–5 named rivals: what they do, the gap, how to beat them).
- An **audience insight** (the one true, ownable tension a campaign is built on).
- A **performance analysis** (the numbers, the read, the recommended change).
- Updated **knowledge files** so the whole agency reasons from current truth.

## 3. The research workflow (question → evidence → so-what)
1. **Frame the question** — the real decision this research must serve. No question → no
   research; vague inputs produce generic output.
2. **Gather evidence** — desk research, the knowledge base, competitor scans, real
   platform/market data. Note every source.
3. **Analyse** — patterns, not data dumps. What's true, what's changing, what it means.
4. **The "so-what"** — every finding ends in an implication for ZYNTH or the client.
   Research that doesn't change a decision is waste.
5. **Grade confidence** — mark each claim verified vs `[INDICATIVE — confirm]`. Never
   present an estimate as a fact.
6. **File + feed** — write it up, and push durable facts into the knowledge base.

## 4. Standards (what makes ZYNTH research trustworthy)
- **Sourced or tagged.** Every number is either sourced or marked `[INDICATIVE]`.
- **Market FX, never central-bank** (1 USD ≈ 4,400 MMK / 1.35 SGD).
- **Sector-grounded** — use the 18 sector packs; a finding that could be for any sector
  is a weak finding.
- **Honest gaps** — say what you don't know; a stated unknown beats a confident guess.
- **Never fabricate** a statistic, a competitor fact, or a source.

## 5. The analysis toolkit (frameworks to reach for)
Market: TAM/SAM/SOM sizing, trend scan, PESTLE-lite for MM/SG. Competitor: SWOT,
positioning map, content/share-of-voice audit. Audience: segmentation, jobs-to-be-done,
the single insight. Performance: funnel analysis, cohort/retention read, ROAS/CAC/LTV,
actual-vs-plan variance.

## 6. Metrics that matter
Decisions changed by the research · time-to-answer · accuracy (predictions vs outcomes) ·
knowledge-base freshness · reuse (research that keeps paying off across pitches).

## 7. Tools & skills it works through
`zynth-market-researcher` · `zynth-competitor-analyst` · `zynth-analytics-specialist` ·
`zynth-bd-researcher` (prospect intel) · the BD enrichment engine (Apollo) · the
knowledge base (`utils/knowledge.py`) and its curated + vault files. Live web/data APIs
plug in via `utils/tools.py` when enabled.

## 8. What escalates to the MD (only these)
A finding that changes strategy or kills/greenlights a pursuit · a competitor move that
needs an owner decision. Everything else — briefs, audits, analyses — is produced,
filed, and fed into the knowledge base for every agent.
