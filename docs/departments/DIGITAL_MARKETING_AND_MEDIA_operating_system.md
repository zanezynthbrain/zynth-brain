# ZYNTH Digital Marketing & Media Production Operating System

The engine room. This department turns strategy into **content that ships and ads that
perform**, and produces the **media** (video, design, photo) behind it. The
`zynth-master-campaign-planner` skill sets the strategy; this system runs the
day-to-day production and performance. AI does the work; the MD approves spend and sends.

Financial law applies: 35% margin floor · 40% target · 15–20% management fee on managed
ad spend · market FX (1 USD ≈ 4,400 MMK / 1.35 SGD).

---

## 1. What this department owns
- **Organic:** content strategy, calendars, copy, community management.
- **Paid media:** Meta / TikTok / Google / LinkedIn campaigns, targeting, optimisation.
- **Media production:** video (concept → shoot → edit → grade), design/key visuals, photo.
- **Performance:** tracking, reporting, and the test-and-scale loop.

## 2. What it produces (real deliverables, filed — never a task list)
- A monthly **content calendar** (date · channel · content · format · owner · goal).
- Finished **copy** and **creative** ready to publish.
- A **paid-media plan** with targeting, budget split, flighting, and forecast results.
- **Media assets** (videos, key visuals, cut-downs) to platform specs.
- A monthly **performance report** with the numbers and the next move.

## 3. The production workflow (idea → live → optimise)
1. **Plan** — pull the month's pillars from the campaign plan; build the calendar.
2. **Produce** — write copy, brief/shoot/edit video (see the media pipeline below),
   design the key visuals. Every asset to spec (feed 1080×1350, Reels/TikTok 1080×1920,
   YouTube 4K −14 LUFS).
3. **Approve** — ZYNTH drafts → MD approves in one pass → publish. (The only human gate.)
4. **Launch paid** — set campaigns to the objective's budget split; real CPM/CPC bands.
5. **Optimise** — the test-and-scale loop: A/B the hook + audience, kill losers fast,
   pour budget into winners; report weekly.
6. **Report** — monthly: reach, engagement, leads/sales, ROAS vs benchmark, and the
   one change that will lift next month.

## 4. The media production pipeline (producer-led)
ZYNTH owns concept, script, direction, and the client relationship; shoots are executed
by outsourced crews at 1–3 camera scale.
- **Pre:** concept → script (EN + Myanmar Unicode/Pyidaungsu) → storyboard → shot list.
- **Production:** book crew/gear (Shwe Sin Oo, Wyne, Mandalay Productions, CMB Films —
  confirm contacts/rates by RFQ), shoot to the shot list.
- **Post:** edit → colour grade (DaVinci: NR → primaries → CST → look → polish) → sound
  (−14 LUFS social) → motion/VFX → platform cut-downs.
- **Craft depth** lives in the `zynth-creative-video-director` skill.
- **Compliance:** 2025 Motion Picture Law lead time for internet films; Myanmar text in
  Unicode (never Zawgyi); respectful portrayal; drone rules.

## 5. Paid-media SOP (the money discipline)
- Budget by objective (from the campaign commercial model): awareness ~45% paid,
  lead-gen ~50%, sales ~55%. Weight to the primary KPI, don't spread evenly.
- Forecast before spending: impressions = budget ÷ CPM × 1000; clicks = impressions ×
  CTR; conversions = clicks × CVR. State every assumption.
- Real bands: FB/IG-MM CPM $0.5–2 · TikTok-MM $0.4–1.5 · FB/IG-SG $6–12 · Google
  Search-SG CPC $1–5 · LinkedIn-SG CPM $15–30.
- ZYNTH management fee 15–20% of managed spend. Never run client money without it.

## 6. Metrics that matter
Reach & recall (awareness) · CPL & lead quality (lead-gen) · ROAS & CAC (sales) ·
engagement rate · content velocity (published/week) · production turnaround (brief→asset).
