# Full Proposal Exemplar — **Your Day, Tuned In**

**Prepared for:** Illustrative client, LUMA Mobile  
**Sector:** Consumer technology  
**Markets:** Myanmar primary; Singapore adaptation route  
**Proposal status:** Illustrative internal exemplar — not for external release, supplier booking, media spend, or performance guarantee  
**Prepared by:** ZYNTH  
**Proposal decision:** Approve a 10-week integrated launch, subject to product, claims, rights, inventory, budget, and market confirmation.

## 1. One-Line Ask

LUMA engages ZYNTH to launch its LUMA Air wireless-earbud range in Yangon, with a Singapore-ready adaptation, through an integrated creative, retail, creator, digital, and experiential system that creates qualified product consideration and first-wave conversion readiness.

## 2. Executive Summary

LUMA Air should not enter the market as “another earbud with features.” It should enter as the product that gives people a more intentional relationship with their day. The recommended platform, **Your Day, Tuned In**, dramatises the moments when people want to choose their own atmosphere: the commute, the work block, the reset, and the evening return.

ZYNTH recommends a ten-week phased launch: signal, reveal, try, and prove. The work combines a hero creative system, vertical social content, creator proof, retail/product experience, paid-media test plan, PR angle, and a compact launch experience called the **LUMA Listening Room**. The aim is not vanity reach; it is a clear product story, measurable high-intent behaviour, retail/ecommerce readiness, and a repeatable learning system for the next wave.

The indicative Myanmar launch investment is **MMK 220–380 million**, excluding taxes and subject to production/media/partner RFQs. A Singapore adaptation is planned separately at **SGD 85–155K**, reflecting market-specific production, creator, media, retail, and compliance requirements.

## 3. The Client Challenge

Wireless audio is a crowded category. Specification-led messaging creates comparison but rarely preference. LUMA needs a launch story that makes the product useful and emotionally relevant while remaining faithful to substantiated product facts. The launch must align product availability, retail staff, ecommerce, media, creators, customer-service answers, and marketing assets so attention does not land on an unprepared purchase journey.

## 4. Audience and Market Opportunity

The primary audience is urban, mobile-first adults aged approximately 18–35 who use audio as a personal transition tool across work, study, commuting, fitness, and downtime. This is a working hypothesis to validate through LUMA’s sales, audience, retail, and category evidence. The Myanmar plan prioritises Facebook/TikTok/retail behaviour after final channel confirmation; Singapore may require a different paid/creator/retail sequence and consumer-law review.

| Audience tension | Opportunity for LUMA | Evidence required before release |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The day feels noisy, fragmented, and externally controlled | Position personal listening as a small act of control | Product audio claims, audience research, category review |
| Buyers distrust over-produced technology advertising | Use real routines and simple proof rather than spec dumping | Creator credibility, product demonstration approvals |
| Product trials are difficult online | Give audiences a tactile, sharable Listening Room or retail trial | Venue/retail feasibility, hygiene, staff, inventory |

| **Your Day, Tuned In** | LUMA makes ordinary transitions feel chosen | Human, flexible, demonstrable, useful across markets | Recommended: requires precise product-proof discipline |

## 6. Recommended Concept — **Your Day, Tuned In**

The campaign follows people through ordinary moments that are usually treated as dead time. When LUMA Air is activated, the scene does not become unrealistic; it becomes intentionally framed. A crowded commute becomes a moment to arrive mentally. A repetitive work block becomes a focus ritual. A walk home becomes an evening reset. The product enables the change without claiming to transform the whole world.

**Signature moment:** The LUMA Listening Room, a four-part, walk-through public experience built around **Focus, Move, Reset, and After Hours**. Each space offers a brief sensory setting and a product trial connected to an approved use case. The final zone provides a clear retail/ecommerce next action.

**Tagline:** *Your Day, Tuned In.*  
**Myanmar adaptation:** A bilingual version is developed only after language and cultural review.  
**Tone:** Intentional, contemporary, tactile, calm confidence—not technical aggression.

## 12. Commercial Terms

The proposed professional-fee schedule is 50% at project commencement, 30% at final creative/production approval, and 20% at final delivery. Third-party commitments require written client approval and prepaid funding where suppliers require it. The scope includes two consolidated revision rounds per agreed deliverable. Additional revisions, new formats, new markets, new shoot/build days, or changes after approval use a written change order.

LUMA retains ownership of its accounts and approved final assets according to paid usage terms. Talent, creator, music, stock, AI, 3D/source-file, venue, and technology rights are recorded separately. No paid media, creator content, product claim, event build, or public communication is released without written approval.

## 14. Key Risks and Mitigation

| Risk | Mitigation |
| --- | --- |
| Product facts/claims are incomplete | Claims matrix approved by product and legal owner before creative release |
| Product stock or retailer readiness falls behind | Inventory/retailer checkpoint before paid-media or trial activation |
| Creator rights or audience fit are weak | Written brief, audience check, disclosure and usage-rights record |
| Venue/build/permit delay | Early feasibility, vendor RFQ, indoor retail fallback, remote digital reveal option |
| Product experience is unsafe or unhygienic | Demonstration protocol, staff training, cleaning/handover and insurer/venue approval |
| Performance below plan | Pre-agreed test matrix, weekly reallocation and creative/landing-page repair process |
| Myanmar/Singapore assumptions differ | Separate local adaptation and cost gate before dual-market release |
