# Regional Creative Benchmarks — SG / TH / VN vs Yangon

## What "regional standard" actually means to a client
- **Design coherence:** one visual system from invite → stage → lanyard →
  post-event report. Not "a nice backdrop."
- **Show-calling:** a called show (cues rehearsed, timed to the minute).
  SG galas run on a cue sheet; many Yangon events still run on "MC vibes."
  Adopting real show-calling is the cheapest visible quality jump.
- **Content on screens:** motion graphics designed for the LED's exact pixel
  map — not a stretched 16:9 PowerPoint.
- **Data discipline:** registration/badging with live counts (SG norm),
  post-event report with numbers within 72h.

## Where each market leads
- **Singapore:** compliance & safety culture, hybrid/stream quality,
  sustainability reporting, sponsor ROI decks.
- **Thailand:** spectacle & theatrical staging, best-value scenic builds,
  festival production maturity.
- **Vietnam:** speed + digital-first activations, aggressive social
  amplification around events.
- **Yangon opportunity:** international-standard *process* (cue sheets,
  RFQs, safety) on local costs — that's ZYNTH's regional-level pitch.

## Benchmarks to hold ourselves to
- Proposal → client within 72h of brief. Rehearsal: minimum one full
  technical run for anything with a programme. Post-event report: 72h,
  numbers first. Safety: crowd ratio plan for anything >500 pax.
