# Budgeting Norms — how ZYNTH builds and defends a budget

## Currency law
All client-facing MMK figures use the **market rate** (see /fx), never the
CBM reference rate. Quote costs at the **sell** side. State the rate and
date in the budget ("@ USD/MMK 4,400, 13 Jul 2026") — protects both sides
when the kyat moves.

## Standard cost structure (any event)
1. Venue & permits
2. F&B (per pax × guaranteed minimum — know the attrition clause)
3. Production: staging, LED/AV, sound, lights, power
4. Creative & content: design, motion graphics, print
5. Talent & programme: MC, artists, speakers
6. Staffing: crew, ushers, security, show-caller
7. Logistics: transport, storage, accommodation
8. **Contingency: 10% — non-negotiable line, never silently absorbed**
9. ZYNTH fee — protect the **35% gross margin floor (R1)**; below = MD approval only
10. **50% deposit before work (R2) on every quote**

## Rules of thumb (sanity checks, not quotes)
- Hotel ballroom corporate dinner (Yangon, premium): venue+F&B ≈
  USD 40–90/pax equivalent; production adds 30–60% on top.
- LED wall indoor (P3.9): rough MMK 500k–1.5M per sqm-day range at market
  rate — always RFQ, never quote from memory.
- Overtime: crew rates jump 1.5–2× after load-out windows; a 2am strike
  can erase the margin of a beautiful event.
- Weather: outdoor without wet-weather plan = not a plan. Marquee/backup
  venue is a real budget line, not a note.

## Defending the budget to a client
Line-item transparency on categories, package the ZYNTH fee inside
production management (never a naked "agency fee 35%"), and always present
GOOD / BETTER / BEST (three tiers) — clients buy the middle.
