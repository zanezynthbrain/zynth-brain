# Event Types — formats, success metrics, budget shape

| Type | Core purpose | Typical Yangon scale | Success metric | Budget gravity |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Corporate summit / conference | Thought leadership, B2B pipeline | 150–800 pax, hotel ballroom / YCC | qualified attendees, sponsor renewal | venue+AV 40%, F&B 30% |
| Product launch | Press + market moment | 80–300 pax | media pickups, orders in 30 days | staging/reveal 35%, press 15% |
| Brand activation / roadshow | Trial + footfall | mall/street, 1000s passing | samples, leads, cost-per-engagement | staffing 30%, build 30% |
| Gala / awards night | Relationship capital | 200–600 pax, banquet | table sales, VIP satisfaction | F&B 40%, entertainment 20% |
| Concert / live show | Ticket or sponsor revenue | 1k–20k (Myanmar Event Park / One Entertainment Park) | ticket sales, safety record | artist 30-50%, production 30% |
| Exhibition / trade show | Lead volume | booths at YCC / Rose Garden | leads per booth, exhibitor renewal | space+build 50% |
| Hybrid / livestream | Reach beyond room | any + online | concurrent viewers, replay views | AV/stream +15-25% on base |

## The three questions every brief must answer
1. **Who must feel what** when they walk out? (design driver)
2. **What does the client count** 30 days later? (KPI driver)
3. **What is immovable** — date, VIP, venue, budget? (constraint driver)

## Format-mixing warning
Clients ask for "a launch that is also a gala with a concert." Each added
format adds a production track (run-of-show, crew, rehearsal). Quote it as
such or the margin dies in overtime.
