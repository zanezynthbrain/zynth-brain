# ZYNTH 3D Stage & Exhibition Portfolio — Wave 1

## Portfolio Position

This first wave converts the approved library architecture into eight distinct, commercially usable spatial concepts for Myanmar and Singapore. They are internal spec concepts. A client name, logo, venue, product, sponsor, dimensions, construction method, final copy and budget remain assumptions until verified. Each concept is designed to become a complete proposal and a Blender-ready spatial package.

## 1. ZYNTH-MM-COR-001 — The Living Forum

**Event family:** Corporate leadership, conference, keynote, town hall and awards.

**Strategic opportunity:** Corporate audiences often receive a static stage that separates leadership from the people in the room. The Living Forum makes the stage feel like a shared operating system: a layered architectural frame that changes with the narrative, allowing keynote, panel, ceremony and audience interaction to feel like one coherent event.

**Spatial mechanism:** A central circular or oval “forum” platform sits inside a layered vertical frame. The rear LED/content plane is broken into three architectural bands: context, evidence and action. Side portals hold live-captioning, translation or data moments. The stage can be configured for keynote, panel and awards mode.

**Signature moment:** The rear frame opens through light and content to reveal the central speaker, making the transition from “what is happening” to “what we do next” visible.

**Required outputs:** Hero wide render, audience sightline, keynote camera view, panel mode, plan/zone diagram, technical assumption sheet, event-run-of-show logic and full proposal.

**Commercial route:** Corporate Conference or Brand Foundation/Launch Room package. Myanmar planning band: MMK 85–180M excluding venue, talent, travel and production pass-through. Singapore planning band: SGD 85–180K excluding venue, production pass-through and specialist technical requirements.

## 2. ZYNTH-MM-LAU-001 — The Reveal Spine

**Event family:** FMCG, consumer, automotive, healthcare, pharmaceutical and property product launches.

**Strategic opportunity:** Product launches need a memorable reveal without making the product feel disconnected from the audience. The Reveal Spine turns the product journey into a physical narrative: problem, transformation, proof and invitation.

**Spatial mechanism:** A linear spine runs from entry to stage, built from translucent fins, light seams and controlled sightline breaks. Guests encounter the product at the final axis, where a hero plinth and reveal screen create a camera-ready moment.

**Signature moment:** A light line travels through the spine as the reveal cue, with the product appearing only after the audience has walked through the story.

**Required outputs:** Entry perspective, hero reveal frame, product-plinth detail, audience view, plan, material palette, lighting sequence, content storyboard and launch proposal.

**Commercial route:** Launch Room package. Myanmar planning band: MMK 60–145M excluding product fabrication, venue, media and talent. Singapore planning band: SGD 70–160K excluding product fabrication, venue and media.

## 3. ZYNTH-MM-EXH-001 — The Luminous Spine

**Event family:** Exhibition booth, trade show, B2B industrial expo, investment forum and government pavilion.

**Strategic opportunity:** Most booths compete through branding volume. The Luminous Spine creates a visible, reusable structure that guides visitors from recognition to demonstration to qualified conversation.

**Spatial mechanism:** A luminous overhead or side spine becomes the long-distance beacon. Under it, three zones work as one funnel: fast orientation, live demo theatre and quiet meeting pods. The back-of-house is hidden in a compact service wall.

**Signature moment:** The product or capability demo appears through a semi-transparent “proof window,” so passersby see activity without disrupting the meeting zone.

**Required outputs:** Aisle-facing hero render, internal visitor view, demo-theatre view, plan with flow arrows, accessibility route, storage/service note, modularity options and trade-show proposal.

**Commercial route:** Experience & Event Lab or Regional Growth Bridge package. Myanmar planning band: MMK 35–95M for concept and build direction, excluding fabrication and venue fees. Singapore planning band: SGD 45–130K for concept and build direction, excluding fabrication and venue fees.

## 4. ZYNTH-MM-PUB-001 — City Pulse Pavilion

**Event family:** Public activation, mall activation, roadshow, city installation and brand experience.

**Strategic opportunity:** A public experience must be understandable in seconds, inviting without pressure and shareable without becoming a selfie-only object. City Pulse Pavilion gives visitors a simple participation loop that leaves a visible trace.

**Spatial mechanism:** A modular pavilion contains an open threshold, a central participatory object and a story wall that updates over the event. The exterior is readable from a distance; the interior rewards dwell time.

**Signature moment:** Every participant adds a small light or colour signal to the pavilion, gradually turning the structure into a collective city pulse.

**Required outputs:** Day/night renders, visitor journey, participation interaction, crowd-flow plan, modular kit-of-parts, activation operations note and campaign proposal.

**Commercial route:** Social Culture Studio or Experience & Event Lab package. Myanmar planning band: MMK 45–125M excluding site, fabrication, permits, staffing and media. Singapore planning band: SGD 60–180K excluding site, fabrication, permits and media.

## 5. ZYNTH-MM-HOS-001 — Monsoon Arrival

**Event family:** Hotel launch, resort experience, hospitality opening, tourism showcase and destination event.

**Strategic opportunity:** Hospitality audiences respond to atmosphere, pace and sensory memory. Monsoon Arrival uses rain, shelter, reflection and warm thresholds to turn arrival into the first story of the destination.

**Spatial mechanism:** A sequence of canopy, reflection pool or reflective floor, scent/light threshold and open sunset stage creates an arrival-to-dwell journey. The stage can host welcome remarks, performance or culinary presentation.

**Signature moment:** Rain-like light travels across the canopy while the destination reveal opens in warm dawn tones.

**Required outputs:** Arrival render, stage-atmosphere render, guest journey, plan, lighting cue sheet, material and weather assumptions, hospitality proposal and content moments.

**Commercial route:** Experience & Event Lab or Launch Room package. Myanmar planning band: MMK 55–160M excluding venue, hotel operations, performers and fabrication. Singapore planning band: SGD 80–220K excluding venue, hotel operations and specialist production.

## 6. ZYNTH-MM-HCR-001 — Clear Care Forum

**Event family:** Healthcare education, pharmaceutical public-information event, NGO programme and community forum.

**Strategic opportunity:** Health environments need trust, clarity, privacy and accessibility before spectacle. Clear Care Forum creates a calm stage and information journey that can support expert talk, approved-information display, consultation routing and community participation.

**Spatial mechanism:** A low, open stage faces a clear information wall. Side zones separate registration, education, private consultation and accessibility support. Content is composed as verified, readable information—not decorative AI-generated text.

**Signature moment:** A “clear information” sequence moves across the wall in simple layers, showing the audience where to find approved guidance without claiming diagnosis or treatment.

**Required outputs:** Hero environment, information-wall detail, consultation/privacy view, plan with accessibility route, content hierarchy, compliance assumptions, technical pack and proposal.

**Commercial route:** Clarity Sprint plus Experience & Event Lab. Myanmar planning band: MMK 35–100M excluding clinical/regulatory review, venue, fabrication and medical staff. Singapore planning band: SGD 55–150K excluding regulatory review, venue and specialist production.

## 7. ZYNTH-SG-EXH-001 — Proof-to-Pipeline Lab

**Event family:** Singapore B2B, industrial technology, engineering, manufacturing and innovation exhibition.

**Strategic opportunity:** B2B exhibition visitors need to understand capability quickly and qualify themselves into a valuable conversation. Proof-to-Pipeline Lab makes evidence the visual attraction and keeps the meeting conversion path visible.

**Spatial mechanism:** A modular laboratory grid combines a large proof wall, a live demonstration island and graduated meeting pods. The visual language is precise, adaptable and reusable across trade shows.

**Signature moment:** A live data/production sequence travels from the proof wall into the demo island and then into the conversation pod, visualising how evidence becomes a business decision.

**Required outputs:** Aisle hero, demo view, meeting view, plan, modular kit, data-content storyboard, lead-capture logic, technical assumptions and regional-growth proposal.

**Commercial route:** Regional Growth Bridge or Experience & Event Lab package. Singapore planning band: SGD 90–260K excluding fabrication, venue, data integration and staffing. Myanmar adaptation band: MMK 70–190M excluding fabrication and venue.

## 8. ZYNTH-SG-COR-001 — The Layered Forum

**Event family:** Hybrid leadership conference, board strategy forum, livestream, internal summit and awards.

**Strategic opportunity:** Hybrid events often make remote audiences feel secondary. The Layered Forum is designed around broadcast composition from the beginning, with equal attention to the room, the camera and the remote screen.

**Spatial mechanism:** A camera-safe stage uses three depth layers: presenter, live interaction and visual evidence. A curved content plane supports a consistent remote frame, while practical light makes the venue feel alive without damaging exposure.

**Signature moment:** A real-time remote contribution becomes a physical light layer in the room, making participation visible without cluttering the stage.

**Required outputs:** Broadcast frame, audience frame, panel frame, camera/sightline plan, lighting plot assumptions, screen/content map, hybrid run-of-show and proposal.

**Commercial route:** Launch Room or Experience & Event Lab package. Singapore planning band: SGD 100–280K excluding venue, broadcast, platform and production pass-through. Myanmar planning band: MMK 80–220M excluding venue, broadcast and production pass-through.

## Common Proposal Contract

Each design will be expanded into the full ZYNTH proposal standard with a campaign/event title, executive summary, strategic context, audience, objective, concept, experience journey, spatial zones, stage/exhibition features, content and production requirements, staffing, timeline, budget, KPI/measurement, risks, assumptions, exclusions, approval gates and next decision. The proposal must distinguish concept render, indicative scope, vendor-ready input and final build documentation.

## Common 3D Deliverable Contract

Each design will produce a client-showable visual set and a production handoff specification: five renders, a plan/zone diagram, one material and lighting sheet, one technical feasibility sheet, one camera and sightline record, one source-file specification and an export matrix. Where Blender production is executed, the `.blend` file will use the ZYNTH collection hierarchy and stated units. Where only a visual preview is created, it will be labelled **Internal Concept** until source geometry and feasibility checks exist.

## Founder Decision

The first build wave should begin with three designs: **The Luminous Spine** for exhibition/trade, **The Living Forum** for corporate events and **The Reveal Spine** for product launches. These three provide the broadest commercial coverage and create a reusable spatial language across proposal, pitch and portfolio work. The remaining five should follow after the first three pass internal quality review.
