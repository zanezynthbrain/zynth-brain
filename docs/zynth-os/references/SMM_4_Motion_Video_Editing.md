#### **SMM.4. Motion & Video Editing**

1.  **Video Brief & Storyboard Development:**
    *   **Action:** Define video objectives, target audience, key messages, and visual style. Develop a detailed storyboard.
    *   **AI Integration:** AI can assist in generating initial storyboard concepts and script outlines based on the brief. AI can also analyze existing video content for effective storytelling elements.
    *   **Tool:** Storyboarding software, generative AI for scriptwriting.
2.  **AI-Assisted Footage Selection & Rough Cut:**
    *   **Action:** Select relevant footage and create a preliminary rough cut.
    *   **AI Integration:** AI can automatically tag and categorize footage, identify key moments, and generate initial rough cuts based on script or spoken dialogue. AI can also suggest music and sound effects.
    *   **Tool:** Media asset management (MAM) systems with AI tagging, AI video editing software.
3.  **Human Editing & Post-Production:**
    *   **Action:** Human editors refine the video, add graphics, special effects, color grading, and sound design.
    *   **AI Integration:** AI-powered tools for automated transcription, subtitle generation, object removal, and intelligent color correction. AI can also assist with motion tracking and stabilization.
    *   **Tool:** Video editing software (e.g., Adobe Premiere Pro, DaVinci Resolve) with AI plugins.
4.  **Video Optimization & Distribution:**
    *   **Action:** Optimize video for various platforms and ensure proper distribution.
    *   **AI Integration:** AI can analyze video performance metrics, suggest optimal video lengths, formats, and distribution channels for maximum reach and engagement.
    *   **Tool:** Video analytics platforms, AI content distribution tools.
