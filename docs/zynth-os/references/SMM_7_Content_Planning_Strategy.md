#### **SMM.7. Content Planning & Strategy (Integrated)**

1.  **Cross-Platform Content Audit:**
    *   **Action:** Review all existing content across social media, website, blog, and other digital channels.
    *   **AI Integration:** AI can analyze content performance, identify gaps, and suggest repurposing opportunities across different platforms.
    *   **Tool:** Content audit tools, AI content analytics.
2.  **Integrated Content Calendar Development:**
    *   **Action:** Create a unified content calendar that synchronizes content across all digital touchpoints.
    *   **AI Integration:** AI can optimize content distribution schedules for maximum impact across different platforms and audience segments.
    *   **Tool:** Integrated content management systems, AI scheduling tools.
3.  **Content Personalization & Journey Mapping:**
    *   **Action:** Tailor content to different audience segments and stages of the customer journey.
    *   **AI Integration:** AI-powered personalization engines deliver dynamic content based on user behavior and preferences, optimizing the customer journey.
    *   **Tool:** Marketing automation platforms, AI personalization engines.
4.  **Trend Forecasting & Innovation:**
    *   **Action:** Stay abreast of emerging content trends and innovative formats.
    *   **AI Integration:** AI can predict future content trends, analyze competitor strategies, and suggest innovative content formats to maintain a competitive edge.
    *   **Tool:** Trend analysis platforms, AI innovation tools.
