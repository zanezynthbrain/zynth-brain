#### **SMM.3. Graphic Design & Visual Content**

1.  **Visual Brief & Style Guide Adherence:**
    *   **Action:** Understand visual requirements, brand guidelines, and campaign aesthetics.
    *   **AI Integration:** AI can analyze brand assets to ensure strict adherence to style guides (colors, fonts, logos). Image recognition for brand consistency checks.
    *   **Tool:** Brand asset management systems, AI visual compliance tools.
2.  **AI-Assisted Asset Generation:**
    *   **Action:** Create static graphics, illustrations, and image composites.
    *   **AI Integration:** Generative AI (text-to-image) can produce initial visual concepts, background elements, and variations, speeding up the design process. AI can also assist with image manipulation and enhancement.
    *   **Tool:** AI image generators (e.g., Midjourney, DALL-E), graphic design software with AI plugins.
3.  **Human Design & Refinement:**
    *   **Action:** Human designers refine AI-generated assets, ensuring artistic quality, brand alignment, and visual impact.
    *   **AI Integration:** AI-powered tools for background removal, image upscaling, and color correction. Automated resizing for different platforms.
    *   **Tool:** Graphic design software (e.g., Adobe Photoshop, Illustrator), AI design assistants.
4.  **Visual Performance Analysis:**
    *   **Action:** Analyze the performance of visual content across platforms.
    *   **AI Integration:** AI can identify visual elements that drive higher engagement, click-through rates, and conversions, providing insights for future design decisions.
    *   **Tool:** Social media analytics, AI visual analytics platforms.
