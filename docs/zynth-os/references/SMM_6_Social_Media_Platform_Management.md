#### **SMM.6. Social Media Platform Management**

1.  **Platform Setup & Profile Optimization:**
    *   **Action:** Set up and optimize social media profiles across relevant platforms.
    *   **AI Integration:** AI can analyze profile completeness and suggest optimizations for discoverability and brand consistency.
    *   **Tool:** Social media management platforms.
2.  **Community Engagement & Moderation:**
    *   **Action:** Engage with followers, respond to comments, and moderate discussions.
    *   **AI Integration:** AI-powered chatbots can handle routine inquiries and provide instant responses. Sentiment analysis tools can flag urgent or negative comments for human intervention.
    *   **Tool:** Social media engagement tools, AI chatbots, sentiment analysis software.
3.  **Crisis Management & Reputation Monitoring:**
    *   **Action:** Monitor brand mentions and respond to potential crises.
    *   **AI Integration:** AI-powered social listening tools provide real-time alerts for brand mentions, sentiment shifts, and potential PR crises, enabling rapid response.
    *   **Tool:** Social listening platforms, crisis management software.
4.  **Influencer Identification & Collaboration:**
    *   **Action:** Identify relevant influencers and manage collaborations.
    *   **AI Integration:** AI can identify influencers whose audience demographics and interests align with the brand, and predict potential ROI from collaborations.
    *   **Tool:** Influencer marketing platforms with AI analytics.
