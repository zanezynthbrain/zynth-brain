#### **SMM.2. Copywriting & Content Creation**

1.  **Copy Brief & Tone of Voice Definition:**
    *   **Action:** Establish clear copywriting objectives, target audience, and brand tone of voice guidelines.
    *   **AI Integration:** AI can analyze existing brand copy to define tone of voice parameters and ensure consistency. NLP tools can identify effective keywords and phrases.
    *   **Tool:** Brand style guides, AI tone analysis tools.
2.  **AI-Assisted Draft Generation:**
    *   **Action:** Generate initial drafts of social media captions, blog posts, and website copy.
    *   **AI Integration:** Generative AI can produce multiple copy variations, headlines, and calls-to-action based on prompts, significantly accelerating the drafting process.
    *   **Tool:** Generative AI (e.g., Jasper, Copy.ai), content optimization tools.
3.  **Human Editing & Refinement:**
    *   **Action:** Human copywriters review, edit, and refine AI-generated drafts for creativity, brand authenticity, and strategic alignment.
    *   **AI Integration:** AI-powered grammar and style checkers assist in polishing content. Plagiarism detection tools ensure originality.
    *   **Tool:** Grammar checkers (e.g., Grammarly), content editing software.
4.  **A/B Testing & Optimization:**
    *   **Action:** Test different copy variations to identify top-performing content.
    *   **AI Integration:** AI can automate A/B testing processes, analyze results, and suggest optimal copy elements for maximum engagement and conversion.
    *   **Tool:** A/B testing platforms, AI content optimization tools.
