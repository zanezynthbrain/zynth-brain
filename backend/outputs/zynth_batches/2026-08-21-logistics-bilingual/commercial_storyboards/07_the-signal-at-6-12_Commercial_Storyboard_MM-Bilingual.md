## Film Brief

**Tension:** Teams may collect operational updates from many people but lack a shared language for which signal triggers which action.

**Objective:** Create a client-approved discovery-conversation CTA through a controlled creative treatment; no operational or performance result is promised.

**Creative territory:** A signal matters when someone knows the next step
