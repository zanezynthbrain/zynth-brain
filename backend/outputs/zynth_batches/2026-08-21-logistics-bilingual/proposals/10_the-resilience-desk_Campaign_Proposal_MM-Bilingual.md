# The Resilience Desk

**ID:** CMP-2026-LOGISTICS-10  
**Format:** account-based disruption decision sprint and CRM reactivation  

## Commercial Brief

**Tension:** Past customer conversations can stall when leaders face disruption, changing priorities or unclear next decisions.

**Objective:** Move from dormant conversation to an explicit re-scope, defer or discovery-decision meeting.

**Creative territory:** When the Route Changes, the Conversation Can Continue

## Conversion Experience

A three-option decision message routes consented contacts to a client-approved decision sprint; no service-continuity promise is made.
