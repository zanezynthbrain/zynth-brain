# Handover Hour

လွှဲပြောင်းချိန်ကို ယုံကြည်စေမယ့် Handover Hour

**ID:** CMP-2026-LOGISTICS-01  
**Format:** B2B handover-proof pop-up and appointment clinic  

## Commercial Brief

**Tension:** Customers can be told an item is moving, but cannot see who owns the next handover or what information will travel with it.

**Objective:** Move from generic logistics interest to a client-approved service-discovery appointment.

**Creative territory:** The Promise Moves With the Parcel

## Conversion Experience

A three-step handover card maps a hypothetical order journey and prompts a consented service-discovery booking; no live tracking or delivery claim is displayed.
