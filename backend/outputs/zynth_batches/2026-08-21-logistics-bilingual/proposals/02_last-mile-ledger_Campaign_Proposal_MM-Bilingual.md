# Last Mile Ledger

နောက်ဆုံးမိုင်ကို ရှင်းလင်းစေမယ့် Last Mile Ledger

**ID:** CMP-2026-LOGISTICS-02  
**Format:** SME route-visibility retail and merchant activation  

## Commercial Brief

**Tension:** Small merchants need a practical conversation about deliveries, returns and customer communication without being sold an unverified service promise.

**Objective:** Move from informal delivery frustration to a client-approved merchant consultation.

**Creative territory:** Every Stop Leaves a Clearer Story

## Conversion Experience

A paper 'last-mile ledger' lets merchants prioritise notification, handover or returns questions and book a consented consultation.
