# ZYNTH Logistics Two-Hour Batch — Portfolio Overview

> **Batch:** `ZYNTH-20260821-LOGISTICS-BILINGUAL`  |  **Industry:** Logistics and supply chain  |  **Status:** Research-grounded concepts; all client, commercial and production elements proposed/TBC.

## Ten Campaign / Activation Proposals

| ID | Campaign | Format | Conversion mechanism | Recommended planning envelope |
|---|---|---|---|---:|
| CMP-2026-LOGISTICS-01 | Handover Hour | B2B handover-proof pop-up and appointment clinic | A three-step handover card maps a hypothetical order journey and prompts a consented service-discovery booking; no live tracking or delivery claim is displayed. | MMK 21.8m |
| CMP-2026-LOGISTICS-02 | Last Mile Ledger | SME route-visibility retail and merchant activation | A paper 'last-mile ledger' lets merchants prioritise notification, handover or returns questions and book a consented consultation. | MMK 19.4m |
| CMP-2026-LOGISTICS-03 | The Cool Chain Question | controlled cold-chain tabletop masterclass | A mock, non-product tabletop scenario asks participants to identify ownership at each fictional handover and opt into a workshop brief. | MMK 25.7m |
| CMP-2026-LOGISTICS-04 | Route Before Rush | executive demand-planning forum | A facilitated 'decision window' board captures non-sensitive planning questions and offers an executive follow-up session. | MMK 32.2m |
| CMP-2026-LOGISTICS-05 | Return With Reason | reverse-logistics discovery workshop | A fictional return card sort turns participant priorities into a consented discovery-sprint request, not a policy or service claim. | MMK 23.1m |
| CMP-2026-LOGISTICS-06 | Dock to Decision | import-export buyer briefing and trade-preview salon | A 'known / confirm / decide' desk turns generic questions into an opt-in appointment for a client-validated briefing. | MMK 37.6m |
| CMP-2026-LOGISTICS-07 | Signal in Transit | mobile operations-visibility roadshow | A simulated signal wall asks visitors to route fictional exceptions to an action owner, then book a discovery session. | MMK 35.8m |
| CMP-2026-LOGISTICS-08 | Every Stop Counts | driver and dispatch appreciation learning programme | A facilitated 'one change at my stop' card produces anonymous/consented themes for a client review, not an employment promise. | MMK 17.9m |
| CMP-2026-LOGISTICS-09 | The 30-Minute Warehouse | warehouse-process open house and controlled challenge | A non-operational mock picking-and-handover challenge ends with a visitor question card and opted-in follow-up request. | MMK 39.8m |
| CMP-2026-LOGISTICS-10 | The Resilience Desk | account-based disruption decision sprint and CRM reactivation | A three-option decision message routes consented contacts to a client-approved decision sprint; no service-continuity promise is made. | MMK 14.9m |
