# ZYNTH Manual AI Council Packet — No API Required

> **Purpose:** Use this template with any AI web interface you already have permission to use. Do not ask each AI to generate an entire unrelated proposal. Give every contributor the same canonical brief and request one structured artifact. Copy the returned artifact into the **AI Council** tab of the ZYNTH Master Tracker.

## A. Canonical Brief — Copy This First

```text
ZYNTH CANONICAL BRIEF
Record ID:
Track: Campaign / Commercial
Industry and market:
Client/product context:
Audience:
Commercial objective:
Desired behaviour or viewer action:
Known constraints:
Budget band:
Required dates:
Confirmed facts/source links:
Facts/assumptions/TBC boundary:
Do not invent: partners, testimonials, venues, product performance, prices, permits, rights, or claims.
Required response language: English + Myanmar where feasible.
```

## B. Specialist Prompts

| Role | Paste this request after the canonical brief | Required tracker fields |
|---|---|---|
| Research scout | `Return a claim table with: claim, direct source URL, publisher, date, evidence excerpt, confidence, limitation, contradiction, and what must remain proposed/TBC. Do not make a client recommendation.` | Evidence Count, Source Links, Pros/Cons, Critic Result |
| Strategy challenger | `Return: commercial tension, audience logic, desired behaviour, conversion hypothesis, measurement definition, three critical assumptions and three failure risks. Challenge weak logic directly.` | Contribution Summary, Pros, Cons, Resolution |
| Creative director | `Return three distinct creative territories. For each: single-minded proposition, logline, story/experience idea, tone, CTA, what makes it non-generic, and production/rights risks. Do not claim external people or places are confirmed.` | Contribution Summary, Pros, Cons, Next Action |
| Commercial storyboard planner | `Create a detailed 12-frame storyboard table: frame/time, picture/action, dialogue/VO/SFX, on-screen text, camera/edit note, claim/rights note, production dependency. Include master-film and social-cutdown plan.` | Commercial Storyboard Status, Asset Link, Risks |
| Production reviewer | `Return a feasibility checklist: location, talent, crew, equipment, HSE, accessibility, rights, claims, data/privacy, schedule, budget pressure points and client approvals. Label every unverified item proposed/TBC.` | Pros, Cons, Critic Result, Next Gate |
| Critic / red team | `Compare the proposed route against this rubric: evidence quality, Myanmar relevance, commercial logic, distinctness, feasibility, budget discipline, claims/rights/privacy risk and measurement. Return: strengths, weaknesses, contradictions, missing evidence, score 1–5 and revision actions.` | Critic Result, Human Decision, Resolution |

## C. Human Resolution Memo — Record This After Review

| Field | What the human owner writes |
|---|---|
| Decision | Accept / Revise / Hold / Reject |
| Why | One plain-language rationale that outweighs the alternatives |
| Accepted points | What from each contributor is being used |
| Rejected points | What is not being used and why |
| TBC / risk owner | Who must verify it before the next gate |
| Next artifact | The single next deliverable required |
| Owner and due date | Named accountable person and target date |

## D. Usage Rules

1. The user controls which AI web interfaces are used. **No API, key, browser automation or paid connector is required.**
2. Do not paste client personal data, confidential pricing, unreleased plans, passwords, contracts or rights-sensitive assets into a general consumer AI workspace.
3. Treat an AI response as a **draft artifact**, not a fact. Every external claim still needs a source in the Research & Sources tab.
4. Do not ask models to negotiate with, contact, promise, publish, buy from, or represent the client.
5. Add only the concise structured output and the human resolution to the tracker. The portfolio should show decisions, not an unmanageable mass of chat transcripts.
