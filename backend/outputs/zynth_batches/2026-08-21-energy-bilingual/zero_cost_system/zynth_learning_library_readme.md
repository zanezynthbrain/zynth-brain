# ZYNTH Proposal Learning Library — Start Here

## Purpose

This archive turns proposal volume into reusable marketing judgment. It is **not** a dumping ground for files. Every campaign, activation, exhibition experience, commercial idea and detailed storyboard should eventually leave behind one short learning record linked to its stable `CMP` or `COM` ID.

## Use the Library in Three Modes

| Mode | Frequency | What to do | Output |
|---|---:|---|---|
| **Before selection** | Before choosing a new concept | Read comparable closed concepts and the relevant source pack. Ask: what tension, audience, conversion mechanism and execution condition actually mattered? | A two-sentence reusable hypothesis. |
| **During development** | At G2/G3/G4 | Compare the creative route against at least two previous records. Identify what is genuinely distinct and what risk/assumption is repeating. | A recorded differentiation and risk note. |
| **After closure** | Within five working days of G7 | Complete the Learning & Guide questions, classify outcomes and link the note to the original artifact. | A `Proven`, `Promising`, `Unproven` or `Do not reuse` record. |

## Weekly 30-Minute Review

1. Open the live **ZYNTH Master Tracker → Learning & Guide** tab.
2. Select one campaign/activation record and one standalone commercial/storyboard record.
3. Review the evidence, decision log, creative proposition, conversion logic, budget scenario and current artifact link.
4. Answer the ten guide questions in plain language. Do not rewrite the proposal; document what the team learned.
5. Update the related GitHub issue if a system improvement is required.
6. Place an approved/de-identified learning note in this folder and link it back to the Master Tracker.

## Files That May Be Stored Here

| Allowed | Do not store without written approval |
|---|---|
| Approved final proposals, treatments, storyboards, public source packs, de-identified learning notes, approved research summaries | Personal data, client passwords, confidential rates, contracts, unannounced plans, rights-sensitive source media, unreleased product specifications, consented participant details |

## Optional Study Assistant

You may use Gemini Notebook / NotebookLM for **public, approved or de-identified** learning material. Upload a focused source pack, then ask it to compare concepts, identify repeated mechanics, extract evidence or explain a proposal in simple language. Do not treat AI summaries as facts without checking source citations. If material must stay wholly local, use a local Markdown/Obsidian folder instead.

## Naming Standard

`YYYYMMDD-INDUSTRY-CMP-or-COM-ID-Short-Title-v#`

Examples:

- `20260821-ENERGY-CMP-01-Continuity-Clinic-v1`
- `20260821-ENERGY-COM-01-Continuity-Commercial-Storyboard-v1`
- `20260821-ENERGY-Learnings-Week-01-v1`

The stable `CMP` or `COM` ID does not change. Increment only the version when a material artifact changes.
