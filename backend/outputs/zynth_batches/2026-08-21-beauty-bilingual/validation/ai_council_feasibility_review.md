## Mandatory Controls Before External Action

| Risk area | Required control |
|---|---|
| Product, pack, ingredient, label or claim | Written factual source of truth, brand/legal/regulatory sign-off and version-controlled asset register. |
| Product sampling, texture demonstration or handling | Client-approved hygiene, access, safety, product-fact and incident plan; no use implied by a concept alone. |
| Social, TikTok, paid media or commerce | Client account access, policy/market eligibility, approved audience/targeting, tracking, budget, CTA, destination and release decision. |
| Creator, influencer, UGC or testimonial | Contract/rights, disclosure, approved content brief, privacy/consent, moderation and takedown/escalation plan. No fabricated testimonial or performance result. |
| Clinical, procedure, medical or health-adjacent content | Specialist review before any creative development, content release or paid distribution. This batch does not clear such content. |
| Production | Final script, cast, location, music, product/pack, subtitles, claims, safety, privacy, end card and version plan approved at G5. |
| Data and follow-up | Client-approved consent notice, stated purpose, retention/access policy, contact method and escalation owner. |
