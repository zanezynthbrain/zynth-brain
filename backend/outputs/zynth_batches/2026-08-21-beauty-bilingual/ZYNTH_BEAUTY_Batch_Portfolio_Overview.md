## Operating Note

Campaign and commercial tracks are intentionally independent. Social media and TikTok preparation is a mandatory workstream inside each campaign proposal; it does not replace the separate COM concept and 12-frame storyboard. No paid social/TikTok launch, creator outreach, product sampling, UGC reuse, data capture, platform targeting, clinic/procedure or product claim activity is authorised until the relevant client, legal/regulatory, platform, privacy, rights, safety and operations gates are approved.
