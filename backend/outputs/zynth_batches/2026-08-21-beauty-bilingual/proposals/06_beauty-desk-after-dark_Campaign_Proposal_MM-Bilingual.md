# Beauty Desk After Dark

ညဘက် Beauty Desk

**ID:** CMP-2026-BEAUTY-06  
**Format:** appointment-led evening consultation salon with WhatsApp/social hand-raise preparation  

## Commercial Brief

**Tension:** After-work customers can need a quieter way to ask routine questions, without the brand implying a clinical or therapeutic consultation.

**Objective:** Create a client-approved, non-medical beauty conversation that turns informed attention into a consented next step; not a product, efficacy, safety, price, availability or revenue promise.

**Creative territory:** A calm answer after a full day

## Conversion Experience

A protected “ask a routine question” form or messaging hand-raise requests a client-approved appointment.

Invite → client-approved learning prompt → proposed/TBC beauty interaction → consent check → approved information, booking or retailer-assistance route → measurement/learning log.

## Social Media & TikTok Preparation

**Channel role:** quiet consultation

**Organic preparation:** Evening reminder posts, consent language, service-scope FAQ and controlled message response map.

**TikTok-specific preparation:** Calm evening desk scenes and question prompts; no treatment/diagnosis narrative, staff/venue TBC.

**Measurement preparation:** Qualified consented hand-raises; booked client-approved routine conversations; response SLA compliance.
