# Shade Story Studio

**ID:** CMP-2026-BEAUTY-03  
**Format:** shade-discovery studio, creator briefing and vertical-video capture plan  

## Commercial Brief

**Tension:** Makeup choice can become performative when shoppers feel they must copy a trend instead of describing a preferred look.

**Objective:** Create a client-approved, non-medical beauty conversation that turns informed attention into a consented next step; not a product, efficacy, safety, price, availability or revenue promise.

**Creative territory:** Your face, your own vocabulary

## Conversion Experience

A “look-language” card turns a visitor’s selected mood into a consented appointment or approved product-information request.

Invite → client-approved learning prompt → proposed/TBC beauty interaction → consent check → approved information, booking or retailer-assistance route → measurement/learning log.

## Social Media & TikTok Preparation

**Channel role:** self-expression and shade vocabulary

**Organic preparation:** Moodboard posts, client-approved pack/visual mock-ups and creator briefing kit.

**TikTok-specific preparation:** Vertical transitions built from “mood → language → choice” rather than real shade matching; creator/talent, range and rights TBC.

**Measurement preparation:** Creator-brief completion; content-save rate; consented studio booking or information request.
