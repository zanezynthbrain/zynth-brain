# Pack to Purpose

Pack ကနေ Purpose ဆီ

**ID:** CMP-2026-BEAUTY-09  
**Format:** pack-story retail trail and product-information social carousel preparation  

## Commercial Brief

**Tension:** Packaging can become only an aesthetic signal when users cannot easily find client-approved use, disposal, batch, label or care information.

**Objective:** Create a client-approved, non-medical beauty conversation that turns informed attention into a consented next step; not a product, efficacy, safety, price, availability or revenue promise.

**Creative territory:** The pack can start a clearer conversation

## Conversion Experience

A pack-question trail leads to a client-approved information hub or retailer-assistance request.

Invite → client-approved learning prompt → proposed/TBC beauty interaction → consent check → approved information, booking or retailer-assistance route → measurement/learning log.

## Social Media & TikTok Preparation

**Channel role:** pack literacy

**Organic preparation:** Bilingual pack-reading carousel, retailer help routing and product-fact approval checklist.

**TikTok-specific preparation:** Pack-detail videos explain “read / confirm / ask” through mock packs; real labels and claims require approval.

**Measurement preparation:** Carousel saves; approved information-hub visits; retailer-assistance requests.

### Required Setup

- Client-approved brand/product/claims matrix
- Account access and platform eligibility confirmed by client

- Privacy notice, consent route and moderation/escalation owner
- Client-approved landing, DM, booking or retailer-information destination
- Paid-media eligibility, audience, budget and tracking configuration TBC
