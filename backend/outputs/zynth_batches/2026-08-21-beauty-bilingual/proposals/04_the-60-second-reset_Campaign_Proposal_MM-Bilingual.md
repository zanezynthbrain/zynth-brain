# The 60-Second Reset

စက္ကန့် ၆၀ Reset

**ID:** CMP-2026-BEAUTY-04  
**Format:** short-form routine challenge and counter-side reset station  

## Commercial Brief

**Tension:** Short-form beauty content can pressure people to seek an instant result rather than understand a routine’s permitted use.

**Objective:** Create a client-approved, non-medical beauty conversation that turns informed attention into a consented next step; not a product, efficacy, safety, price, availability or revenue promise.

**Creative territory:** A pause before the scroll

## Conversion Experience

A one-minute non-product reset prompt routes participants to approved education content or an opt-in reminder.

Invite → client-approved learning prompt → proposed/TBC beauty interaction → consent check → approved information, booking or retailer-assistance route → measurement/learning log.

## Social Media & TikTok Preparation

**Channel role:** slow beauty reset

**Organic preparation:** Short captions, story polls and a moderation playbook for routine questions.

**TikTok-specific preparation:** Six 60-second reset variants: desk, commute, evening, weekend, weather, gentle pause; no result/benefit claim.

**Measurement preparation:** Video completion; approved poll participation; opt-in education reminders.
