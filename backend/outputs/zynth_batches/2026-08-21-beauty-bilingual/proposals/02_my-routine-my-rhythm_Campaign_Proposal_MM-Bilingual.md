# My Routine, My Rhythm

ကိုယ့် Routine ကိုယ့် Rhythm

**ID:** CMP-2026-BEAUTY-02  
**Format:** routine-building retail clinic and seven-day social participation preparation  

## Commercial Brief

**Tension:** A product shelf can feel like a list of promises rather than a calm, client-verified routine conversation.

**Objective:** Create a client-approved, non-medical beauty conversation that turns informed attention into a consented next step; not a product, efficacy, safety, price, availability or revenue promise.

**Creative territory:** Small rituals, clearly chosen

## Conversion Experience

A fictional routine board helps visitors select a learning card and consent to a client-approved follow-up.

Invite → client-approved learning prompt → proposed/TBC beauty interaction → consent check → approved information, booking or retailer-assistance route → measurement/learning log.

## Social Media & TikTok Preparation

**Channel role:** routine education

**Organic preparation:** Seven daily routine prompts, static carousel recap, community reminder and client-approved link hub.

**TikTok-specific preparation:** Seven 15–30 second “day in the routine” prompts; creator/content talent TBC; no before/after, diagnosis or routine-fit assertion.

**Measurement preparation:** Completed learning cards; opt-in reminder rate; approved appointment/retailer-information requests.
