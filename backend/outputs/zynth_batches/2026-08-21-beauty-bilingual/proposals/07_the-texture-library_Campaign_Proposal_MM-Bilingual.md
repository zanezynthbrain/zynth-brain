# The Texture Library

Texture ကို စမ်းသပ်မယ့် Library

**ID:** CMP-2026-BEAUTY-07  
**Format:** sensory design exhibition and creator macro-content preparation  

## Commercial Brief

**Tension:** Beauty texture is often shown visually, but customers may need a brand-approved, hygienic and non-medical way to explore it.

**Objective:** Create a client-approved, non-medical beauty conversation that turns informed attention into a consented next step; not a product, efficacy, safety, price, availability or revenue promise.

**Creative territory:** See the detail. Keep the boundary.

## Conversion Experience

A hygienic non-product texture language board opens an approved product-information or appointment path.

Invite → client-approved learning prompt → proposed/TBC beauty interaction → consent check → approved information, booking or retailer-assistance route → measurement/learning log.

## Social Media & TikTok Preparation

**Channel role:** texture literacy

**Organic preparation:** Macro-texture stills, hygiene process explainer and content usage-rights register.

**TikTok-specific preparation:** Macro visual hooks around fictional/non-product texture cards; real product, handling, product facts and claims TBC.

**Measurement preparation:** Macro-content completion; approved information requests; hygiene-question resolution.
