# The Next Routine

နောက်တစ်ကြိမ် Routine

**ID:** CMP-2026-BEAUTY-10  
**Format:** consented CRM re-engagement and TikTok/organic content testing sprint  

## Commercial Brief

**Tension:** Past customers may have a changing routine, but reactivation can become intrusive when consent, contact rights and product facts are unclear.

**Objective:** Create a client-approved, non-medical beauty conversation that turns informed attention into a consented next step; not a product, efficacy, safety, price, availability or revenue promise.

**Creative territory:** The next routine starts with permission

## Conversion Experience

A three-choice re-engagement message—learn, revisit, pause—routes to a client-approved information or booking flow.

Invite → client-approved learning prompt → proposed/TBC beauty interaction → consent check → approved information, booking or retailer-assistance route → measurement/learning log.

## Social Media & TikTok Preparation

**Channel role:** permission-based re-engagement

**Organic preparation:** Client-owned CRM message variants, privacy/consent check and editorial test register.

**TikTok-specific preparation:** Three message-led vertical variants: learn, revisit, pause; no profile inference, personal data exposure or price/availability claim.

**Measurement preparation:** Valid consent rate; opt-out processing; qualified re-engagement actions, not purchases.

### Required Setup

- Client-approved brand/product/claims matrix
- Account access and platform eligibility confirmed by client
- Creator/talent, music, UGC, visual and location rights register
- Privacy notice, consent route and moderation/escalation owner
- Client-approved landing, DM, booking or retailer-information destination
- Paid-media eligibility, audience, budget and tracking configuration TBC

## Budget (MMK, non-tax planning envelope)

Lean: MMK 10.8m | Recommended: MMK 16.9m | Flagship: MMK 26.5m

## Primary KPIs

- Client-approved reach / qualified exposure definition
- Content completion or save signal by approved platform analytics
- Consented information, booking or retailer-assistance requests
- Moderation/escalation response compliance
- No unapproved claim, rights, privacy or safety incident

## Required Approvals

- Client factual product and permitted-claims brief
- Myanmar FDA/regulatory and legal review as applicable
- Product label/pack, ingredient, price, availability and retailer facts
- Venue, hygiene/sampling/accessibility and operational safety plan where relevant
- Creator, talent, music, user-generated content and platform rights
- Social/TikTok account access, policy eligibility, moderation and data/privacy approval
