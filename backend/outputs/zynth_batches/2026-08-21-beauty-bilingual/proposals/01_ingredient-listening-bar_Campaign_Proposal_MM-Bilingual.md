# Ingredient Listening Bar

ပါဝင်ပစ္စည်းကို နားထောင်မယ့် Ingredient Listening Bar

**ID:** CMP-2026-BEAUTY-01  
**Format:** controlled ingredient-literacy pop-up and short-form Q&A series  

## Commercial Brief

**Tension:** Beauty shoppers may see ingredient language without a brand-approved way to ask what a product is, is not, and requires them to verify.

**Objective:** Create a client-approved, non-medical beauty conversation that turns informed attention into a consented next step; not a product, efficacy, safety, price, availability or revenue promise.

**Creative territory:** Care begins with a better question

## Conversion Experience

A non-product “ask before you choose” card captures a consented consultation or approved retailer-information request.

Invite → client-approved learning prompt → proposed/TBC beauty interaction → consent check → approved information, booking or retailer-assistance route → measurement/learning log.

## Social Media & TikTok Preparation

**Channel role:** question-led product literacy

**Organic preparation:** Burmese-first explainer cards; comment FAQ triage; approved “what we can/cannot say” content bank.

**TikTok-specific preparation:** 5 hook tests: ingredient question, myth/limit, label close-up, ask-the-expert prompt, retail-card reveal. No efficacy claim or product demonstration without approval.

**Measurement preparation:** Organic save/share/comment signals; approved FAQ completion; consented consultation requests.
