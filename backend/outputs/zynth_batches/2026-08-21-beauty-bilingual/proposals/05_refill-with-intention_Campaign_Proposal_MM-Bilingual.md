# Refill With Intention

ရည်ရွယ်ချက်နဲ့ Refill

**ID:** CMP-2026-BEAUTY-05  
**Format:** refill-literacy activation with a materials/aftercare social education series  

## Commercial Brief

**Tension:** Sustainability language can become vague if a beauty brand has not confirmed what packaging, refill, return or aftercare proposition is actually available.

**Objective:** Create a client-approved, non-medical beauty conversation that turns informed attention into a consented next step; not a product, efficacy, safety, price, availability or revenue promise.

**Creative territory:** Keep the ritual, question the waste

## Conversion Experience

A proposed/TBC return-and-care pledge asks participants to choose a learning path and a consented update channel.

Invite → client-approved learning prompt → proposed/TBC beauty interaction → consent check → approved information, booking or retailer-assistance route → measurement/learning log.

## Social Media & TikTok Preparation

**Channel role:** informed aftercare

**Organic preparation:** Care/disposal/refill education posts, customer-service response tree and proposed/TBC collection FAQ.

**TikTok-specific preparation:** Return/refill question hooks, pack-care close-ups and “what to verify first” clips; availability/terms remain TBC.

**Measurement preparation:** Approved information-hub visits; pledge opt-ins; client-validated collection enquiries.
