## Planning Implications

TikTok is included inside the **campaign preparation track**, not as a substitute for the separate commercial/storyboard track. Every beauty campaign must define an organic-content preparation route, a TikTok-specific short-form content route, a consent/rights approach for creators and participants, a protected CTA/landing or booking approach, client-owned measurement definitions, moderation/escalation and a brand/regulatory approval gate.

Beauty creative must avoid unverified efficacy, safety, dermatological, medical, whitening, acne-treatment, cure, permanent-result, ingredient, price, availability, review, user-testimonial and before/after claims. Proposed/TBC is mandatory for brand, product, retailer, marketplace, creator, talent, venue, formulation, claims evidence, substantiation, permits, rights and product-distribution details.

> Any paid social or TikTok activity may advance only after a named client owner confirms the actual product facts, permitted audience, claims substantiation, label/pack imagery, account access, pixel/measurement configuration, consent/privacy notices, creator contracts, media budget and platform-policy clearance.
