**Format:** 60-second neighbourhood food-trail social film  
**Linked campaign:** CMP-2026-FNB-007  

## Film Brief

**Tension:** Distinct local food businesses can be hard to discover without flattening them into a generic list.

**Objective:** Drive a voluntary route-passport start while foregrounding neighbourhood discovery and safe participation.

**Creative territory:** A city is read one small table at a time.
