**Format:** 60-second craft-documentary commercial  
**Linked campaign:** CMP-2026-FNB-005  

## Film Brief

**Tension:** Craft can be lost when a finished dish is seen without the decisions that made it.

**Objective:** Build interest in a client-approved open-kitchen, tasting-session or content waitlist.

**Creative territory:** What you watch being made changes what you taste.
