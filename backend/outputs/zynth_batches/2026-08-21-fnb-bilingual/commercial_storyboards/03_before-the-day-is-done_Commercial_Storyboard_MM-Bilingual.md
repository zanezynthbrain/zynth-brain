# Before the Day Is Done

**Format:** 45-second convenience-meal narrative commercial

## Film Brief

**Tension:** A busy person may assume convenience means a compromised table.

**Objective:** Reframe convenient meal preparation as a client-approved trial or demonstration invitation.

**Creative territory:** A small pause can still become a real table.
