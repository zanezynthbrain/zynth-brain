# Find Your Flavour

သင့်အရသာကို ရှာဖွေပါ

**ID:** COM-2026-FNB-002  
**Format:** 30-second kinetic retail-discovery commercial  
**Linked campaign:** CMP-2026-FNB-002  

## Film Brief

**Tension:** A crowded shelf can turn curiosity into default behaviour.

**Objective:** Make a flavour-choice route feel easy and drive an optional discovery-trail start.

**Creative territory:** Your flavour is a route, not a random choice.
