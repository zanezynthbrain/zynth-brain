# The Second Serving

ဒုတိယအကြိမ် စားသုံးမှု

**ID:** COM-2026-FNB-010  
**Format:** 35-second feedback-to-return product dialogue commercial  
**Linked campaign:** CMP-2026-FNB-010  

## Film Brief

**Tension:** A first trial does not automatically teach a brand why a customer did or did not return.

**Objective:** Invite existing client-approved customers to give structured feedback and choose a future client-approved update.

**Creative territory:** The second serving begins with listening.
