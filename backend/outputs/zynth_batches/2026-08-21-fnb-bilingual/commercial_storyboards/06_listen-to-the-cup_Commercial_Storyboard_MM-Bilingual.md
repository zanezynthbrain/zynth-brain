# Listen to the Cup

ခွက်ကို နားထောင်ပါ

**ID:** COM-2026-FNB-006  
**Format:** 30-second sensory coffee-and-tea sound film  
**Linked campaign:** CMP-2026-FNB-006  

## Film Brief

**Tension:** Coffee and tea language can feel exclusive before a person discovers their own sensory vocabulary.

**Objective:** Invite adult viewers to a client-approved sensory learning session or preference profile.

**Creative territory:** Taste begins before the first sip.
