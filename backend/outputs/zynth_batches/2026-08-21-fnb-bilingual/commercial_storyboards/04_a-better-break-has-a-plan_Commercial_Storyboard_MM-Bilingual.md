**Format:** 40-second B2B workplace service film  
**Linked campaign:** CMP-2026-FNB-004  

## Film Brief

**Tension:** Workplace food decisions can be made without seeing the people, timing and practical needs behind them.

**Objective:** Prompt workplace decision-makers to request a scoped meal or pantry conversation.

**Creative territory:** The break is part of how work works.
