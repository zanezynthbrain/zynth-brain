# One Bite, Then the Brief

**ID:** COM-2026-FNB-009  
**Format:** 45-second B2B food-trade preview film  
**Linked campaign:** CMP-2026-FNB-009  

## Film Brief

**Tension:** A promising food product can create interest without a respectful structure for the next business conversation.

**Objective:** Invite qualified buyers and producers to a client-approved review or category-conversation route.

**Creative territory:** A bite can start a better question.
