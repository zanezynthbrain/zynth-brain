## Batch-Level Strengths

The strongest feature is the deliberate separation of **campaign** and **commercial** tracks. `CMP` records manage activation/business mechanisms, while `COM` records manage film and storyboard development. This prevents a commercial treatment from being mistakenly treated as an activation plan, or a campaign mechanic from being mistaken for a shoot script. It also makes the ZYNTH Master Tracker capable of recording their different budgets, approvals, rights, assets and learning outcomes.

The second strength is controlled evidence use. The research register retains public context for food-show, competition, trade-preview, sensory-learning, calendar and packaging-review formats, while explicitly refusing to treat it as proof of any future opportunity. This is especially important for F&B work, where claims, food handling, packaging and consumer information need named client ownership.

## Priority Risks and Controls

| Risk | Why it matters | Mandatory control |
|---|---|---|
| Product, ingredient, allergy and storage facts | Incomplete consumer information could cause customer harm or misleading communication. | Client-approved factual product sheet; food-safety and legal sign-off before external use. |
| Food sampling or live preparation | Hygiene, holding, temperature, serving and waste controls cannot be assumed. | Written operational plan, venue agreement, accountable food-safety owner and on-site escalation route. |
| Brand, chef, venue, sponsor or retailer references | Public stories and past events do not establish permission or association. | Written agreement/approval and rights record before naming, filming or publishing. |
| Consumer-data capture | Consent and marketing follow-up must be purposeful, minimal and controlled. | Approved privacy notice, opt-in language, CRM owner, retention and deletion path. |
| Commercial production | Pack, talent, music, location, subtitles and claims need multiple clearances. | G5 production pack with script, rights matrix, legal card and release checklist. |
| Scenario interpretation | Planning figures could be misread as expected financial returns. | Mark every scenario as directional and replace it with client data before budgeting/commitment. |
