# Origin on the Table: Regional Ingredient Proof Pop-Up

မူရင်းကို စားပွဲပေါ်သို့ — ဒေသထွက်ပါဝင်ပစ္စည်း အတွေ့အကြုံပြပွဲ

**ID:** CMP-2026-FNB-001  
**Format:** Regional ingredient proof pop-up  

## Commercial Brief

**Tension:** Consumers may enjoy a dish but lack a clear, non-exaggerated reason to choose it again when multiple F&B options feel interchangeable.

**Objective:** Convert taste curiosity into an opted-in tasting follow-up, product trial or client-approved purchase route.

**Creative territory:** The ingredient has a place, a season and a hand behind it.

## Conversion Experience

A three-station tasting card unlocks a QR ingredient/story card and a voluntary next-step preference selection; any order, delivery or purchase destination is client-approved/TBC.

Discover one regional ingredient → observe controlled preparation → taste a small labelled portion → select a preference → opt into a follow-up only with consent.
