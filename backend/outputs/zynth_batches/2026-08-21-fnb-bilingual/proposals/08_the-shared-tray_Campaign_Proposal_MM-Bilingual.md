# The Shared Tray: Hosted-Table Pre-Order Activation

မျှဝေစားပွဲ — Hosting Pre-Order Activation

**ID:** CMP-2026-FNB-008  
**Format:** Seasonal hosting pop-up and group-order conversion  

## Commercial Brief

**Tension:** People planning a shared meal can lack confidence in what to order, how much to choose or how to coordinate a group without over-ordering.

**Objective:** Move hosting interest into a client-approved group-order consultation, menu-planning tool or pickup/delivery enquiry.

**Creative territory:** The table begins with the people you are thinking of.

## Conversion Experience

A table-size planner creates a non-binding hosting card and offers an optional client-approved pre-order or consultation request.

Choose a gathering moment → set a table size → explore menu/serving prompts → build a non-binding card → request the next approved service step.
