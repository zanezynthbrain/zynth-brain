# The Better Break: Workplace Meal Discovery Programme

ပိုကောင်းတဲ့ နားချိန် — အလုပ်ခွင် Meal Discovery Programme

**ID:** CMP-2026-FNB-004  
**Format:** B2B workplace tasting and procurement clinic  

## Commercial Brief

**Tension:** Office meal or pantry decisions can remain price-only conversations, leaving service fit, dietary information and ordering workflow unexplored.

**Objective:** Move a workplace decision-maker from generic interest to a consented procurement tasting or operational scoping meeting.

**Creative territory:** A break should work as hard as the team does.

## Conversion Experience

A short preference-and-operations card produces a named tasting/scoping appointment; no catering capacity or outcome is promised at the activation.

Choose a workplace use case → review practical service cards → taste approved portions → complete a procurement priority card → book a scoping conversation.
