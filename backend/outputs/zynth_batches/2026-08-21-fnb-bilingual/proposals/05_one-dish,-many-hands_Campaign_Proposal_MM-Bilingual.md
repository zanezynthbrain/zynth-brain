# One Dish, Many Hands: Open-Kitchen Craft Table

တစ်ပွဲတည်း၊ လက်များစွာ — Open-Kitchen Craft Table

**ID:** CMP-2026-FNB-005  
**Format:** Chef-led craft demonstration and preview  

## Commercial Brief

**Tension:** A food brand may have a credible making process, yet customers see only the finished dish and cannot distinguish craft from generic promotion.

**Objective:** Create a high-attention, client-approved waitlist, tasting-session or content-consent pathway around visible craft.

**Creative territory:** The making is part of the meal.

## Conversion Experience

A clearly timed demonstration ends with a question card, a controlled tasting sequence and an optional waitlist or session request.

Meet the dish story → observe selected preparation steps → ask a guided question → receive serving context → request a future client-approved seat or update.
