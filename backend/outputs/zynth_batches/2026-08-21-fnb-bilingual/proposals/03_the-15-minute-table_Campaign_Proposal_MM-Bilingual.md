# The 15-Minute Table: Convenience Meal Kit Experience

၁၅ မိနစ် စားပွဲ — အလျင်မြန်အစားအစာ Kit အတွေ့အကြုံ

**ID:** CMP-2026-FNB-003  
**Format:** Product-launch demonstration and reservation activation  

## Commercial Brief

**Tension:** Busy households may perceive convenient food as less satisfying or too difficult to fit into their own routines.

**Objective:** Convert a convenience concern into a client-approved trial-kit reservation or cooking-demo booking.

**Creative territory:** A real table can begin before the day has finished.

## Conversion Experience

Visitors select a real-life meal moment, watch a timed but non-performance-guaranteed demo, and reserve a limited client-approved trial kit or demonstration slot.

Choose a meal moment → view ingredient/step card → observe a controlled demo → compare serving choices → reserve a next step.
