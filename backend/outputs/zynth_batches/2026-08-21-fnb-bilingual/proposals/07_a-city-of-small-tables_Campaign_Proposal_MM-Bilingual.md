# A City of Small Tables: Neighbourhood Food Passport

စားပွဲငယ်များ၏ မြို့ — ရပ်ကွက်အစားအစာ Passport

**ID:** CMP-2026-FNB-007  
**Format:** Neighbourhood food-trail and local-business activation  

## Commercial Brief

**Tension:** Independent food businesses may have distinct offers but little shared mechanism to help people discover, compare and return responsibly.

**Objective:** Generate transparent discovery, client-approved opt-in and measurable return routes without promising sales or partnership outcomes.

**Creative territory:** A good city is remembered one small table at a time.

## Conversion Experience

A digital/print passport records client-approved visits or learning moments, then offers a voluntary preference capture and future route update.

Pick a route theme → receive rules/safety guidance → visit participating points → collect approved marks → reflect on a favourite → opt in to future routes.
