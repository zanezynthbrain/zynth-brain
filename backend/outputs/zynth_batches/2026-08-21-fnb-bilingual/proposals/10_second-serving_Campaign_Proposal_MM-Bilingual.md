# Second Serving: Feedback-to-Return Packaging Lab

ဒုတိယအကြိမ် စားသုံးမှု — Feedback-to-Return Packaging Lab

**ID:** CMP-2026-FNB-010  
**Format:** Product-feedback pop-up and CRM reactivation programme  

## Commercial Brief

**Tension:** A first trial may not become a repeat habit because brands often learn too little about the actual serving, storage, pack-opening or re-order friction.

**Objective:** Generate consented, structured feedback and a client-approved re-engagement route without claiming packaging, sustainability or compliance outcomes.

**Creative territory:** The next serving begins with what the first one taught us.

## Conversion Experience

A guided pack-use review captures a priority friction and lets the participant select a future update, trial or client-approved repeat-purchase prompt.

Review an approved use moment → identify one friction → rank practical needs → receive a transparent response path → opt in or decline follow-up.
