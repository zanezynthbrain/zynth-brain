# The Flavour Find: Guided Shelf-to-Sip Discovery Trail

အရသာရှာဖွေရေး — လမ်းညွှန်ထားသော Shelf-to-Sip Discovery Trail

**ID:** CMP-2026-FNB-002  
**Format:** Retail discovery trail  

## Commercial Brief

**Tension:** New beverages or snack variants can be overlooked when choice overload makes shoppers default to familiar products.

**Objective:** Help a shopper identify one personally relevant flavour route and capture a consented re-engagement preference.

**Creative territory:** Do not browse the shelf. Follow your flavour.

## Conversion Experience

A five-question flavour card issues a route token to two or three client-approved discovery points, ending in a voluntary preference capture or offer reveal.

Choose a flavour mood → receive a route token → explore short product stories → sample only where approved → choose a preferred next step.
