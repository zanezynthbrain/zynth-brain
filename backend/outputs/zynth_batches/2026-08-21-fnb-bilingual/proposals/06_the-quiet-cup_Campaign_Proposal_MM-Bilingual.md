**Format:** Sensory masterclass and membership pathway  

## Commercial Brief

**Tension:** Coffee and tea communication can become technical or status-led, making curious newcomers reluctant to ask questions or return.

**Objective:** Turn guided sensory participation into an optional club/session registration or client-approved product learning route.

**Creative territory:** Your taste already knows where to begin.

## Conversion Experience

A guided aroma/flavour scorecard produces a self-selected learning profile and optional registration for a future session, tasting pack or client-approved update.

Choose a sensory prompt → smell/observe → taste controlled samples → complete a scorecard → select a next learning step.
