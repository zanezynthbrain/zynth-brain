# The Buyer’s Bite: MSME Food Trade Preview

ဝယ်ယူသူအတွက် တစ်ကိုက် — MSME Food Trade Preview

**ID:** CMP-2026-FNB-009  
**Format:** Curated B2B trade preview and buyer-meeting clinic  

## Commercial Brief

**Tension:** Emerging food producers may generate interest yet lack a structured, realistic route to a buyer conversation, product review or next qualification step.

**Objective:** Turn selected product discovery into a client-approved buyer meeting, product-review request or category briefing—not an implied listing or endorsement.

**Creative territory:** One good bite should open a better conversation.

## Conversion Experience

A structured buyer card captures criteria and issues a named, consented follow-up request after a client-approved product interaction.

Review category brief → meet selected producer/story → conduct controlled product interaction → complete buyer criteria card → request a meeting or review.
