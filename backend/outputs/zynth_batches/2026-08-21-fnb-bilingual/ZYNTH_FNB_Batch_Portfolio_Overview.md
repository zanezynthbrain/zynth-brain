## Ten Campaign / Activation Proposals

| ID | Campaign | Format | Conversion mechanism | Recommended planning envelope |
|---|---|---|---|---:|

| CMP-2026-FNB-004 | The Better Break | B2B workplace tasting and procurement clinic | A short preference-and-operations card produces a named tasting/scoping appointment; no catering capacity or outcome is promised at the activation. | MMK 19.8m |
| CMP-2026-FNB-005 | One Dish, Many Hands | Chef-led craft demonstration and preview | A clearly timed demonstration ends with a question card, a controlled tasting sequence and an optional waitlist or session request. | MMK 31.2m |
| CMP-2026-FNB-006 | The Quiet Cup | Sensory masterclass and membership pathway | A guided aroma/flavour scorecard produces a self-selected learning profile and optional registration for a future session, tasting pack or client-approved update. | MMK 23.7m |
| CMP-2026-FNB-007 | A City of Small Tables | Neighbourhood food-trail and local-business activation | A digital/print passport records client-approved visits or learning moments, then offers a voluntary preference capture and future route update. | MMK 33.8m |
| CMP-2026-FNB-008 | The Shared Tray | Seasonal hosting pop-up and group-order conversion | A table-size planner creates a non-binding hosting card and offers an optional client-approved pre-order or consultation request. | MMK 25.4m |
| CMP-2026-FNB-009 | The Buyer’s Bite | Curated B2B trade preview and buyer-meeting clinic | A structured buyer card captures criteria and issues a named, consented follow-up request after a client-approved product interaction. | MMK 36.5m |
| CMP-2026-FNB-010 | Second Serving | Product-feedback pop-up and CRM reactivation programme | A guided pack-use review captures a priority friction and lets the participant select a future update, trial or client-approved repeat-purchase prompt. | MMK 18.4m |
