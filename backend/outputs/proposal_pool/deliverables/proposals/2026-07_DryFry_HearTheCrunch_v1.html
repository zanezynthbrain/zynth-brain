01
Executive Summary & Strategic Rationale

Own the crunch, own the category.

Yangon's fried-chicken market is busy and undifferentiated — international chains, local players and delivery brands all competing on price, combos and the same soft, oily bite. Dry Fry has a real product truth to win with: the dry-fry technique makes it shatteringly crispy, boldly spiced and not greasy. That is a sensory territory no competitor has claimed.

The ask: engage ZYNTH to launch Dry Fry with an integrated campaign and an opening event built entirely around the crunch — a sound-led social movement, food-creator amplification, and a first-week trial-and-footfall drive that turns a new outlet into the city's most talked-about bite.

The headline outcome

A packed opening week (2,500–4,000 covers), a #HearTheCrunch movement of 8,000–15,000 UGC posts, and a delivery + repeat-visit engine that keeps the seats full after the hype.

02
Market & Audience Intelligence

A crowded plate, one empty seat: the crunch.

Fried chicken is one of Myanmar's most competitive F&B categories — chains (KFC, Lotteria), fast-growing local brands, and a booming delivery layer (foodpanda). Discovery is Facebook- and increasingly TikTok-led; young diners chase what looks and sounds crave-worthy, and delivery has made trial frictionless.

The competitive set

Local fried-chicken brandsPrice, delivery, spiceSoft/greasy perception; compete on discount, not craft

The tension: everyone's fried chicken looks the same in a photo and goes soft on the ride home. Nobody owns crispy you can hear. Dry Fry's technique makes that ownable — and sound is the most scroll-stopping thing on TikTok.
