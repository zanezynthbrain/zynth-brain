01
Executive Summary & Strategic Rationale

The wallet war is won on meaning, not features.

Myanmar's payment market is not empty — it is crowded and utilitarian. KBZPay and Wave have won scale, and the rest are fighting a cashback-and-feature war that a challenger cannot out-spend. Shwe Pay does not need a bigger feature list. It needs to mean something.

The opportunity: a generation of urban Myanmar under 30 is building its own future — earning early, selling online, sending money home, refusing to wait for the old institutions. They have wallets, but none of them feels like theirs. Shwe (gold) is Myanmar's oldest symbol of stored value, trust and status. Shwe Pay can own the double meaning: the new gold standard — the standard for money, and the standard this generation sets for itself.

The ask: engage ZYNTH to design and deliver an integrated launch — a brand campaign, a signature cultural moment (Launch Night), and a performance engine — that turns Shwe Pay from “another wallet” into a movement, and converts that movement into installs, activated users and merchants.

The headline outcome

120,000–180,000 installs and 60,000–95,000 activated (first-transaction) users in a 12-week window, at a blended cost per activated user at or below MMK 650 — plus a cultural launch moment that competitors cannot buy with media weight.

02
Market & Audience Intelligence

A category winning on utility, wide open on identity.

Digital payment adoption among urban Myanmar youth has accelerated sharply, led by Facebook-first discovery and a fast-rising TikTok culture. Cash still dominates day-to-day, but the young, smartphone-first cohort is the tipping point — and it is up for grabs.

Strategic Positioning & The Idea

The New Gold Standard.

Positioning: Shwe Pay is the digital wallet for the generation building Myanmar's future — money that moves as fast as their ambition.

Single-minded proposition: Your ambition deserves the new gold standard.

Campaign line: Shwe Pay — The New Gold Standard.

Why this beats the obvious idea

The obvious move is a cashback/feature launch. Against KBZPay's scale, that is a war Shwe Pay loses — it competes on the incumbent's terms and rents users who leave for the next promo. “The New Gold Standard” competes on meaning and identity, which the incumbents are too big and too institutional to claim. It reframes a wallet as a statement about who you are — and turns users into a movement that spreads itself. One idea, carried from positioning to the launch stage to the KPI line.

Concept & Creative System

Matte black. Liquid gold. Kinetic pride.

Not old-temple gold — self-made gold: sharp, digital, in motion. A visual world of deep black and liquid-gold light, kinetic Burmese + English typography, and a recurring “gold thread” motif that links people, payments and ambition. Confident and warm — proud, never arrogant.

Four content pillars

  • The Standard — aspirational brand films featuring real young Myanmar makers, sellers and creators. The emotional core.
  • Gold Moves — fast, satisfying product content: how quick, how easy, how it feels to pay and get paid.
  • The Circle — UGC + creators answering one question: “What's your gold standard?” The movement engine.
  • Merchant Rise — young sellers growing because getting paid got easier.
