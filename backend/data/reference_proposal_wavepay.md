1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
WavePay has revolutionized digital payments in Myanmar. Now, as the financial landscape evolves, there is a strategic opportunity to capture the high-value segment: young professionals, entrepreneurs, and SME owners in Yangon. This proposal outlines a comprehensive, high-impact product launch event for WavePay's new premium digital banking feature.
ZYNTH proposes "The Next Wave: Elevating Your Financial Future," an exclusive, tech-forward launch event designed to position WavePay Premium not just as a tool, but as a lifestyle enabler for Myanmar's ambitious professionals.
Set in the prestigious Crystal Ballroom at LOTTE Hotel Yangon, this event will host 250 carefully curated guests, including VIPs, key media, strategic partners, and high-net-worth individuals. The evening will blend immersive technology, elegant hospitality, and a powerful narrative of financial empowerment. From a futuristic LED tunnel entrance to a dramatic product reveal and high-level networking, every touchpoint is engineered to generate buzz, drive immediate premium user acquisition, and solidify WavePay's market leadership.
With a proposed budget of 105,000,000 MMK, ZYNTH will deliver an end-to-end, turnkey solution encompassing creative direction, technical production, vendor management, and seamless execution.
2. EVENT CONCEPT & CREATIVE DIRECTION
Theme: "The Next Wave: Elevating Your Financial Future"
The narrative centers on progression, sophistication, and seamless control. We are moving from basic transactions to wealth management and premium lifestyle integration.
Visual Identity
Color Palette: Deep Navy Blue (trust/finance), Metallic Gold (premium/wealth), and Electric Cyan (technology/future).
Typography: Sleek, modern sans-serif fonts with clean lines.
Imagery: Abstract fluid dynamics (representing the "wave" and seamless flow of money), glowing digital nodes, and high-end lifestyle photography.
Key Experience Moments
The Digital Gateway (Arrival): Guests enter through a custom-built LED tunnel displaying flowing, abstract data streams that react to their movement, symbolizing stepping into the future of finance.
The Premium Lounge (Pre-Event): A sophisticated networking space featuring a bespoke cocktail bar, interactive touch-tables demonstrating the app's new features, and ambient electronic-jazz fusion music.
The Reveal (Main Show): A high-energy, synchronized light and sound sequence leading into a cinematic 3D projection mapping on the main stage, culminating in the CEO's keynote and the official unveiling of the premium features.
The SME Success Panel: A short, impactful discussion featuring top Yangon entrepreneurs discussing how digital finance accelerates their business, providing social proof.
Mood & Style References
The aesthetic is "Futuristic Elegance." It should feel like a high-end tech keynote (think Apple or Tesla) merged with an exclusive VIP gala. Lighting is moody and architectural, utilizing sharp lasers and soft ambient glows. Materials include brushed metal, dark acrylics, and high-resolution LED surfaces.

5. STAGE & PRODUCTION DESIGN
Stage Dimensions & Materials
Main Stage: 10m (W) x 5m (D) x 1m (H) with a central thrust extending 3m into the audience.
Materials: High-gloss black acrylic finish for a sleek, reflective surface. Integrated LED strips along the edges for dynamic lighting effects.
Backdrop: Seamless 10m (W) x 4m (H) LED wall as the primary visual element.
3D Design Brief (Blender)
Main Stage: Model the stage structure with integrated LED strips.
LED Tunnel Entrance: A 5m long, 3m high, 2.5m wide curved LED tunnel with dynamic content capabilities.
Premium Lounge: Design a modular lounge area with modern furniture, interactive touch-tables, and a branded bar.
Product Reveal Mechanism: Conceptualize a dramatic reveal moment for the WavePay Premium logo/interface on the main LED screen, possibly involving a holographic effect or a physical element rising from the stage.
Lighting Design
Fixture Types:
Moving Head Spotlights (e.g., Robe Pointe, Clay Paky Sharpy): 12 units for dynamic beam effects, aerials, and gobos. Positioned on front and side trusses.
LED Wash Lights (e.g., Chauvet Rogue R2 Wash): 16 units for stage color washes and ambient lighting. Positioned on overhead trusses and floor.
Profile Spotlights (e.g., ETC Source Four LED Series 2): 4 units for key lighting on speakers and performers, ensuring clear visibility.
Laser Projectors (e.g., Pangolin FB4-controlled RGB lasers): 2 units for futuristic beam shows and atmospheric effects.
Effects: Sharp, angular beams in electric cyan and deep blue. Slow, elegant color transitions during networking. High-energy, synchronized light show during product reveal and entertainment segments.
Sound System Specs
Main PA System: Line array system (e.g., L-Acoustics K2 or d&b audiotechnik J-Series) suitable for the ballroom size, ensuring even coverage and clarity for speech and music.
Monitors: 4-6 stage monitors for speakers and performers.
Microphones: High-quality wireless handheld microphones (e.g., Shure ULXD series) for speakers and panel, lapel mics for key presenters.
Mixing Console: Digital mixing console (e.g., Yamaha CL5 or Avid S6L) with experienced sound engineer.
LED/Video Content Plan
Pre-Event: Dynamic abstract animations, WavePay branding, countdown timer.
Arrival: Interactive LED tunnel content reacting to guest movement.
Opening: High-definition WavePay corporate video, animated logo reveals.
Keynote: Custom-designed presentation slides, embedded video clips, live camera feed of speaker.
Product Reveal: Cinematic 3D animation showcasing WavePay Premium features, UI walkthroughs, holographic effects.
Panel Discussion: Branded lower-thirds, speaker profiles, live camera feed.
Entertainment: Synchronized visual content complementing the dance performance.
Networking: Ambient visuals, social media wall integration.
Special Effects
Haze Machines: 2-4 units for atmospheric enhancement of lighting beams.
Projection Mapping: Custom 3D projection mapping onto the main stage and surrounding architectural elements during the product reveal, creating an immersive environment.
Confetti Cannons: Discreetly placed for a celebratory burst during the peak product reveal moment.
