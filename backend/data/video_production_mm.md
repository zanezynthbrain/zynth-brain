## Platform delivery specs
- **TikTok / Reels** — 9:16, 1080×1920, H.264/H.265; hook in first 3s
- **YouTube** — 16:9, up to 4K (3840×2160), −14 LUFS
- **Facebook** — 1:1 or 4:5 for ads, 1080p, H.264
- Broadcast/TVC (region PAL) — 25fps, −23 LUFS, station spec sheet

## Cultural & legal (Myanmar)
- **Censorship:** commercials and "internet-based films" technically require approval under the 2025 Motion Picture Law amendments — flag lead time for clearance.
- **Language:** Myanmar text in **Unicode (Pyidaungsu)**, never Zawgyi; give Burmese lines proper line-height; transcreate taglines, don't translate.
- **Etiquette:** respectful portrayal of elders, monks and religious sites is mandatory; check sensitivities before shoot.

## Post-production quick-reference (ZYNTH standard)
- **DaVinci Resolve node order:** Noise Reduction → Primaries (balance) → CST (Log→Rec.709) → Look/creative → final polish. Grade to scopes, protect skin.
- **Premiere:** ProRes 422 Proxy @ 1/4 for smooth 4K/8K; Dynamic Link to After Effects for VFX.
- **CapCut (social):** auto-captions + high-contrast text + rapid cuts in the first 3s; speed-ramp curves to hit audio beats. Licensed music for brand work.
- **Loudness:** −14 LUFS online, −23 broadcast; true-peak < −1 dBTP.
