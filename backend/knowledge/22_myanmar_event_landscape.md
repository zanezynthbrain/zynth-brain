## Cultural calendar → campaign windows (Gregorian · Myanmar month · opportunity)
- **Jan** Pyatho · Independence Day (Jan 4) → nationalistic campaigns, Q1 planning
- **Feb** Tabodwe · Htamane, Union Day (Feb 12) → community/brand building
- **Mar** Tabaung · Pagoda festivals → localized promos, cultural sponsorship
- **Apr** Tagu · **Thingyan (New Year)** → MAJOR: brand activations, pandals, water pavilions, launches
- **May** Kason → CSR, spiritual themes; school reopening
- **Jun** Nayon → educational campaigns, CSR
- **Jul** Waso · **Buddhist Lent starts** → CSR + corporate only; QUIET for major launches
- **Aug** Wagaung → community/goodwill; Thadingyut campaign prep, back-to-school
- **Sep** Tawthalin · Regatta → sports sponsorship, outdoor
- **Oct** Thadingyut · **Festival of Lights** → HIGH consumer spend, gift-giving, launches, retail
- **Nov** Tazaungdaing · **Balloon festival** → brand sponsorship, night markets, entertainment
- **Dec** Natdaw → year-end galas, corporate celebrations, Q1 planning

## Cultural sensitivity (integrate into every event)
Respect religion (avoid full-moon/Lent for non-CSR) · hierarchy & formality with VIPs · bilingual signage
(Burmese + English) · diverse F&B (Halal/veg, avoid pork at general events) · colour/number symbolism ·
avoid politically sensitive content · plan for Yangon traffic.
