# 25 — Marketing Campaign Playbook

> Source: Manus consultation (Jul 2026). Reusable campaign frameworks + benchmarks
> for the CMO/campaign work. Benchmarks are general guidelines — validate per client.

## Campaign planning SOP (10 steps)
1) Discovery & brief · 2) Market research (competitors, audience, platform data) ·
3) Strategy (positioning, messaging, channel mix, budget allocation) · 4) Creative
direction (key visuals, copy themes, pillars) · 5) Tactical plan (calendar, ad specs,
schedule) · 6) Budget breakdown (per channel/week + production + agency fee) · 7) KPI
framework · 8) Execution timeline (week-by-week) · 9) Measurement & reporting ·
10) Optimization (A/B tests, budget-reallocation triggers).

## Every campaign output includes (15 sections)
Overview · Audience profile · Competitive landscape (3–5 competitors) · Strategy (big
idea, positioning, messages, tone) · Channel strategy (+budget %) · Content plan
(pillars, calendar 2–4 wks) · Creative direction (visual/copy/hashtags/CTA) ·
Influencer/KOL strategy · Paid-media plan (targeting, expected results) · Detailed
budget (itemised, local + USD, % of total) · Timeline · KPIs (metric/target/tool) ·
ROI projection (+break-even) · Risk & mitigation · **ZYNTH fee & profit (15–20% mgmt fee).**

## Budget-allocation benchmarks (by objective)
- **Brand awareness:** 60% ToF (social/display/OOH) · 30% MoF (content/influencers) · 10% BoF (search).
- **Lead gen:** 20% ToF · 50% MoF (webinars/gated/email) · 30% BoF (search/retargeting).
- **E-commerce/sales:** 10% ToF · 30% MoF · 60% BoF (shopping/search/retargeting/email).

## KPI benchmarks (general) `[UNVERIFIED — Manus]`
- Social engagement rate 1–5% · CTR: search 2–5%, display 0.1–0.5%, social 0.5–2%
- CPL: B2B $50–200+, B2C $5–50 · ROAS (e-commerce) target 3–5×+.

## Content format specs
FB/IG: square 1080×1080, portrait 1080×1350, Stories/Reels 1080×1920 · TikTok 1080×1920, 15–60s ·
YouTube 1920×1080 (+Shorts 1080×1920) · LinkedIn 1200×627, professional tone.

## Channel-selection framework
Pick channels by: 1) where the audience is · 2) the objective · 3) affordable budget ·
4) dominant platforms in the market · 5) best content format for the message.
