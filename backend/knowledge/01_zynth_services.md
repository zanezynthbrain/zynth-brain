# ZYNTH Services & Business Rules

ZYNTH Asia (www.zynth.asia) — *The Intelligence of Creativity*. Digital
marketing & events agency operating in Singapore and Myanmar (Yangon).

## Service departments
1. **Creative** — commercial video production & SMM content: concepts, scripts, production pipelines (ZYNTH owns the idea/script/direction; production is outsourced to vetted vendors)
2. **Digital Marketing** — SEO, paid ads (Meta/TikTok/Google), social media marketing, 6-hourly market scans
3. **Event Management** — run-of-show, budgets, vendor scoring, full delivery (MM + SG vendor databases)
4. **Tech** — website & AI agent development, design, maintenance, uptime monitoring
5. **Business Development ("NOVA")** — prospect research, lead scoring 0–100, pitch prep, pipeline
6. **Operations** — exact-ROI financial reporting, daily/weekly executive summaries

## Financial governance (R1–R5 — these are LAW in every proposal/pitch)
- **R1:** Minimum **35% gross margin** on any quote — anything below gates for MD approval
- **R2:** Every proposal states a **50% deposit before work starts** — no exceptions
- **R3:** Maintain ≥ 3 months cash runway — Ops flags when below
- **R4:** Revenue mix target **80% retainer / 20% project**; flag when project revenue exceeds 40%
- **R5:** 20% of profit reinvested into the AI stack

## Approval gates (nothing below happens without human sign-off)
- Any prospect outreach or client-facing message/proposal
- Ad spend ≥ USD 1,000 · event budget ≥ USD 5,000 · pitch value ≥ USD 5,000
- Any content publication, production deploy, or client-facing push
- Any pitch below the 35% margin floor (MD dual approval)

## Lead qualification
Lead score 0–100 (market fit, budget signal, digital gap, growth signal,
accessibility — 20 pts each). Qualified at **≥ 60** → outreach angle +
pitch skeleton, recommending the retainer tier that fits their budget signals.

## Writing standards
Client-grade prose, no emoji in client documents, numbered sections.
Burmese-script copy always gets human translation review — never machine-translate.
