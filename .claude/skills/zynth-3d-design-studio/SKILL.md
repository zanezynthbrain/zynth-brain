# ZYNTH 3D & Design Studio

## ZYNTH Operating Contract

Follow the shared [ZYNTH Capability System Standard](../../../docs/ZYNTH_CAPABILITY_SYSTEM_STANDARD.md). In particular: classify the work band; separate verified facts from assumptions; create three distinct territories for material creative work; make output executable and measurable; pass the relevant quality gate; and preserve founder/project-owner approval before external release, spend, client contact, vendor commitment, or publication.


## Mission

Turn a commercial objective into a visual system that is **distinctive, on-brand, buildable, and deliverable**. Produce an internal concept package first; only create media or a production job after the linked project and founder approval state permit it.

This skill creates work across key visuals, campaign systems, product renders, packaging, social creative, posters, spatial concepts, exhibition booths, stage concepts, retail activations, and Blender-ready 3D scenes. It does not substitute an aesthetic render for a real brand system, technical drawing, RFQ, structural sign-off, cultural review, or client approval.

## Work Band and Authority

| Stage | May do automatically | Human decision required |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Explore | Research visual references, create three territories, draft prompts, visual briefs, moodboard logic, and internal concept frames. | None to draft. |
| Propose | Build client-reviewable rationale, indicative deliverables, production assumptions, and cost/RFQ inputs. | Founder/project owner before external presentation. |
| Execute | Create a single controlled preview or approved-template variation after a founder-approved linked project enters the creative queue. | Project owner for each new territory; founder for scope/risk changes. |
| Release | Send, publish, manufacture, fabricate, print, or run paid media. | Explicit human release approval. |

## Preflight

Capture the following before creating a client-ready package. Ask at most three sharp questions if two or more critical items are missing; otherwise proceed as an **internal hypothesis** and label assumptions.

| Required input | Examples / decision rule |
| --- | --- |
| Commercial objective | Launch, conversion, premiumisation, retail footfall, sponsorship visibility, internal culture, product explanation, event attendance. |
| Audience and context | Who must notice, feel, understand, or act; where, when, market, culture, category and accessibility needs. |
| Brand truth | Verified brand kit, logo permissions, palette/type system, mandatory claims, prohibited content, prior work and competitive context. |
| Deliverable and environment | Platform dimensions, print stock, packaging dieline, venue/site plan, screen ratio, site photos, product geometry, output format. |
| Constraint | Budget, lead time, approval path, fabrication limit, lighting/power/rigging, platform policy, rights, language and legal review. |
| Evidence state | Confirmed facts, supplied claims, unverified assumptions, references, source/date. |

## Core Method

1. **Frame the problem.** State audience, human tension, objective, one desired behaviour/belief, and why a visual/spatial solution is appropriate.
2. **Audit the brand and environment.** Read the verified brand kit; inspect the site/product/dimensions. Do not guess logos, product details, brand colours, venue measurements, technical limits, or cultural symbolism.
3. **Create three territories.** Vary the mechanism—not merely the colour. Each territory includes: insight, proposition, visual world, material/lighting/style language, signature moment, build/generation approach, risk, and production implication.
4. **Select the creative system.** Recommend one territory with a rationale. Define hierarchy, focal point, type and image relationship, palette, composition, motion/spatial behaviour, and a prompt/reference plan.
5. **Choose the production route.** Select one of the routes below. Record why it is the right fidelity/cost/speed choice.
6. **Build the package.** Create the requested artifact, specifications, assumptions, source-file requirement, and use cases. Use `references/production-package.md` and `templates/design-production-brief.md`.
7. **Quality gate.** Score, revise, and route for approval. Nothing external is released from this skill.

## Production Route Decision

| Route | Use when | Required proof before it can move forward |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Design composition** | Typography, logo lockups, claims, layouts, precise brand work, accessible information, print files. | Editable source and verified brand/legal copy. |
| **OpenArt/image model preview** | Internal concept frames, art direction exploration, backgrounds, visual texture, non-text hero imagery. | Prompt log, model/version if available, source/reference inputs, one human QC pass. Do not place final logos, readable text, Burmese type, legal claims, or unverified products in-model. |
| **Blender scene** | Stage, booth, retail, product, spatial or environment concept that needs camera/light/scale control. | `.blend` source, units/scale, scene hierarchy, preview renders, materials assumptions and export target. |
| **Template-auto image** | Pre-approved client/template, constrained copy overlay and a fixed visual system. | Founder-approved project, named template ID, output limit, queue approval, QC/release route. |
| **Physical fabrication / vendor RFQ** | Anything that will be built, printed, installed, or used at an event. | Drawings/specs, quantity/measurements, materials, site/venue constraints, safety/permits, comparative quote and owner approval. |

## 3D and Spatial Design Rules

For a stage, booth, exhibition, retail environment, or activation, do not stop at a hero render. Produce the spatial package described in `references/production-package.md` and confirm the following.

- Use a stated unit system, real-world scale, guest-flow assumptions, sightlines, access/egress, service/back-of-house needs, power/AV/rigging assumptions, and mobility/accessibility considerations.
- Separate **concept geometry** from build-critical geometry. A model is not a structural, electrical, fire-safety, or permit drawing.
- Name Blender collections and objects consistently; preserve a clean scene hierarchy; pack only approved/rights-cleared texture assets; include camera and lighting setup; save a versioned `.blend` source.
- Export only the formats requested by the delivery brief. `.glb`/`.fbx` exports require a scale/axis/texture check; renders require stated resolution, colour space, camera, and revision version.
- Every beautiful visual needs a buildability note: what is plausible, what needs a vendor/engineer, what is a placeholder, and what could change cost or timing.

## Image and Creative Direction Protocol

For any generated preview, write a prompt specification before generation:

| Prompt component | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| Objective and audience | What attention/feeling/action is the image meant to earn? |
| Visual mechanism | Composition, focal point, perspective, movement/energy, material/light, not only style adjectives. |
| Brand boundary | What verified brand assets will be applied later; what must not be generated. |
| Environment/product truth | Only verified physical characteristics; otherwise use a neutral placeholder. |
| Technical output | Aspect ratio, use, resolution target, version, source/ref image and variation count. |
| Exclusions | No readable text, logos, celebrity/public likeness, minor, unsafe/culturally sensitive symbols, infringing visual imitation, unsupported product/claim. |

The first output is a **creative preview**, not a final client deliverable. Final composition uses editable typography, verified logo assets, legal copy, and image QC.

## Mandatory Output Contract

The scale determines the package, but every output must include the following.

| Artifact | Minimum delivery |
| --- | --- |
| Visual concept | One-page opportunity/insight, three territories, selected direction, rationale, use cases, risks/assumptions, approval state. |
| Key visual / social system | Art direction, master composition, responsive/adaptation specifications, editable copy/logo layer plan, approved asset list, export matrix. |
| Product/packaging | Front/side/hero views, product/claim assumptions, dieline/engineering dependencies, material/finish direction, production disclaimer. |
| 3D/spatial concept | Narrative, plan/zones, dimension assumptions, camera/render set, technical/buildability notes, `.blend` source, preview exports, revision log. |
| Client package | Executive explanation, visual rationale, deliverables, timeline, scope/assumptions, measurement intent, commercial/RFQ inputs, decision required. |

## Quality Gate

Score each applicable dimension 1–5. Client-ready work requires 4+ in every mandatory dimension and a named reviewer.

| Dimension | Pass condition |
| --- | --- |
| Strategic relevance | The design advances one defined business objective for one defined audience. |
| Originality | The territory has a recognizable mechanism and does not imitate a named artist/brand or generic AI trope. |
| Brand and craft | Hierarchy, layout, colour, type, imagery, language and accessibility comply with verified brand standards. |
| Spatial/build realism | Scale, flow, materials, service, technology, cost/time implications and safety dependencies are stated. |
| Technical integrity | Correct source file, naming, resolution, format, version, asset rights and export checks. |
| Governance | Assumptions, cultural/rights risks, approval state and external-release restriction are visible. |

## Handoff and Learning

- Hand off to **Creative Director** for territory selection and creative QA, **Project Manager/Producer** for schedule and resource lock, **Event Manager/3D Production** for spatial build/RFQ, and **Account Manager** for client review.
- Store the chosen direction, rejected territories and why, prompt/reference log, source assets, QC score, client feedback, production variance and outcome evidence in the project folder.
- Promote a technique to a reusable template only after it has passed at least two approved uses or one verified high-value use without a rights, quality, or production failure.
