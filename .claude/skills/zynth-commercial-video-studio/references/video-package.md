# ZYNTH Commercial Video Package Reference

Read this when creating a complete video package, approving a shoot/generation plan, or reviewing a video before client release.

## 1. Treatment Standard

The treatment must answer the commercial question before the aesthetic question.

| Section | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| Objective and audience | What decision/behavior should move; who must care; where will they encounter it? |
| Proposition | One sentence describing the brand role or message. |
| Story engine | What changes, is revealed, is proven, is participated in, or is felt across the film? |
| Hook | The visual/verbal/audio opening built for the actual platform context. |
| Brand integration | How the product/service earns a role within the story; avoid a disconnected end card. |
| Craft direction | Camera, edit, art, performance, light, sound, grade, typography and motion logic. |
| Representation | Casting, culture, language, accessibility, inclusion, sensitive depiction and location/product considerations. |
| Production route | Live, AI preview, 3D, motion, footage or combination; why it is appropriate. |

## 4. Rights and Representation Checklist

| Area | Confirm before release |
| --- | --- |
| Talent and likeness | Written release, permitted territory/time/platform, parent/guardian process where a minor may be involved. |
| Product and location | Permission to depict; verified product/packaging/version; no unsupported endorsement. |
| Footage/image/model assets | Licence/source/term/territory/medium; attribution where required. |
| Music/voice/SFX | Licence, commissioned-work record or provider terms; rights for edit/cutdown/territory/platform. |
| AI generation | Provider/model version if known; source/reference rights; no deceptive claim that a real person/event/product was recorded. |
| Claims/legal | Confirmed substantiation and owner; required disclaimer/caption form. |
| Language | Verified EN/MM copy; subtitle/transcreation review owner; typeset rather than model-generated Burmese text. |
| Cultural/safety | Sensitivity review; no unsafe/stereotyped/religious/political implication without qualified approval. |

## 7. Final QC

- Validate the approved picture-lock version against the current brief and change log.
- Check copy, logo, product, claims, subtitles, names and lower-thirds against verified source—not the generated or edit draft.
- Watch with sound on and sound off; check the first three seconds, captions, CTA, final frame and platform crop.
- Check visual artifacts, continuity, colour, compression, black/silent frames, clipping, audio level, thumbnail, links and delivery permissions.
- Archive source/project files, approved master, alternate versions, licence/release evidence, script, board, QC record, client approval and final outcome notes.
