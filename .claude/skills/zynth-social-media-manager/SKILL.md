## Platform Management Guide

### Instagram
**Profile optimisation:**
- Bio: 150 chars max. Lead with value proposition, not description. Include one CTA + link.
- Profile photo: Logo — high contrast, readable at 110x110px
- Link in bio: Use link aggregator (Linktree/Beacons) for multiple CTAs
- Story Highlights: Organise by category — About, Services, Testimonials, FAQ, Behind the Scenes

**Posting best practices:**
- Feed: 3–4x per week minimum for algorithm favour
- Stories: Daily — mix of content (polls, questions, behind-scenes, reposts)
- Reels: 2–3x per week — highest organic reach on Instagram currently
- Best posting times (Singapore): 7–9am, 12–2pm, 7–10pm weekdays
- Caption structure: Hook → Value → CTA. First line must work without "more" tap.
- Hashtags: 5–10 relevant hashtags. Mix of niche (10K–500K posts) and broad (500K+). Place at end of caption or first comment.

**Engagement rules:**
- Reply to ALL comments within 24 hours
- Reply to DMs within 4 hours during business hours
- Like and respond to Story replies — they are high-value signals
- Engage with followers' content proactively (5–10 meaningful comments per day)

### Facebook
**Page optimisation:**
- Cover photo: 820x312px — use as billboard, not just logo
- About section: Complete every field — address, hours, website, description
- CTA button: Set to most relevant action (Message Us / Book Now / Learn More)
- Pinned post: Always have a pinned post — current offer, key announcement, or brand intro

**Posting best practices:**
- Frequency: 3–5x per week
- Native video outperforms linked video — always upload directly to Facebook
- Posts with questions or clear CTAs get more engagement
- Facebook algorithm rewards posts that generate comments > reactions
- Best posting times (Singapore): 1–3pm, 7–9pm
- Best posting times (Myanmar): 7–9pm (peak mobile usage)

**Myanmar Facebook specifics:**
- Facebook IS the internet for most users — treat the page as a website
- Facebook Live performs exceptionally well — use for launches, Q&As, demonstrations
- Messenger response time matters — fast responses boost page rating and trust
- Burmese script in posts significantly increases engagement for local audiences
- Groups: Consider a community group alongside the page for deeper engagement

---

### TikTok
**Profile optimisation:**
- Bio: Lead with what value viewers get, not what the brand does
- Link: Add website once eligible (1,000 followers)
- Pinned videos: Pin 3 best-performing or most representative videos

**Posting best practices:**
- Frequency: 3–5x per week minimum for algorithm traction
- Post natively — never repost Instagram Reels with watermarks
- Use TikTok's text, effects, and trending sounds — native content outperforms
- First 2–3 seconds are everything — hook must be immediate
- Best times (Singapore): 7–9am, 12–3pm, 7–11pm
- Use 3–5 relevant hashtags + 1–2 trending hashtags per post

**Content approach for TikTok:**
- Educate, entertain, or both — pure promotion rarely works organically
- Behind-the-scenes, process videos, and "how we do X" perform well for agencies
- Trend participation: If a trend fits the brand naturally — use it. Never force it.
- Comment engagement: Reply to comments with video replies — generates more views

---

### LinkedIn (for B2B clients)
**Profile/Page optimisation:**
- Banner: 1128x191px — clear value proposition
- About: First 2 lines visible before "see more" — make them count
- Featured section: Highlight best case study, media mention, or key service

**Posting best practices:**
- Frequency: 3–4x per week
- Text-only posts with strong hooks can outperform image posts
- Native documents (carousels/PDFs) get high reach — use for insights, lists, guides
- Tag people (not just companies) — individual notifications drive more engagement
- Best times (Singapore): Tuesday–Thursday, 8–10am, 12pm, 5–6pm

## Community Management Framework

### Response Time Standards:
| Channel | Comments | DMs / Messages |
|---|---|---|
| Instagram | Within 24 hours | Within 4 business hours |
| Facebook | Within 24 hours | Within 2 business hours |
| TikTok | Within 48 hours | Within 24 hours |
| LinkedIn | Within 48 hours | Within 24 hours |

### Response Tone Guide:
- **Positive comments:** Acknowledge + add value. Don't just say "Thank you!" — extend the conversation.
- **Questions:** Answer fully. If complex, offer to continue in DM.
- **Complaints:** Acknowledge publicly, resolve privately. Never argue. Never delete unless abusive.
- **Negative reviews:** Respond calmly, take ownership, offer a path to resolution. Always public.
- **Spam/Irrelevant:** Delete or hide. Do not engage.

### Crisis Response Protocol:
1. Flag to account manager immediately — do not respond without approval
2. Pause all scheduled posts while situation is assessed
3. Draft response for client approval before publishing
4. Monitor closely for 24–48 hours post-response
5. Document and debrief after resolution

## Influencer Management

### Influencer Tier Guide (Singapore):
| Tier | Followers | Best For | Typical Rate (SGD) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Nano | 1K–10K | Niche trust, high engagement | $50–$300/post |
| Micro | 10K–100K | Targeted reach, good ROI | $300–$2,000/post |
| Macro | 100K–500K | Broad reach, brand association | $2,000–$10,000/post |
| Mega | 500K+ | Mass awareness | $10,000+/post |

### Influencer Brief Checklist:
- [ ] Campaign objective and key message
- [ ] Mandatory inclusions (product, hashtag, disclosure #ad or #sponsored)
- [ ] Content format and platform
- [ ] Posting date and approval deadline
- [ ] Usage rights (can ZYNTH/client repurpose the content?)
- [ ] Exclusivity period (if applicable)
- [ ] KPIs: views, reach, engagement rate, link clicks

### Influencer Vetting Checklist:
- [ ] Engagement rate above 2% (micro), 1% (macro)
- [ ] Audience location matches target market
- [ ] No recent controversy or brand-unsafe content
- [ ] Authentic-looking follower growth (no sudden spikes)
- [ ] Has posted sponsored content before and disclosed properly

## Social Media Audit Framework

Use when taking over a new client's social media or reviewing performance.

Rate each area 1–5:
| Area | Question |
|---|---|
| Profile completeness | Are all profile fields optimised? |
| Posting consistency | Has the brand posted regularly in the last 90 days? |
| Content variety | Is there a healthy mix of formats and topics? |
| Engagement quality | Are comments and DMs being responded to? |
| Brand consistency | Does it look and sound like the same brand everywhere? |
| Audience growth | Is the follower count growing month on month? |
| Content performance | Are posts generating meaningful engagement (not just likes)? |

Output: Scores + top 3 priority fixes with specific recommended actions.
