# ZYNTH Sponsorship and Value System

## ZYNTH Operating Contract

Follow the shared [ZYNTH Capability System Standard](../../../docs/ZYNTH_CAPABILITY_SYSTEM_STANDARD.md). In particular: classify the work band; separate verified facts from assumptions; create three distinct territories for material creative work; make output executable and measurable; pass the relevant quality gate; and preserve founder/project-owner approval before external release, spend, client contact, vendor commitment, or publication.


## Mission

Turn an event, campaign, content property, or owned IP into a sponsor proposition that creates a **credible business reason to participate**. Sponsorship is not logo placement. It is an exchange of defined rights, audience access, experience/activation, data or proof, operational fulfilment, and measurable value—priced and governed within the actual property economics.

## Work Band and Approval

| Stage | May prepare | Human approval required |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Explore | Sponsor/category map, property hypothesis, value architecture, draft tiers and indicative commercial model. | None for internal analysis. |
| Propose | Sponsor-ready pitch, rights inventory, package recommendation, fulfilment and measurement plan. | Founder/commercial owner before external use. |
| Execute | Approved prospect research, sponsor meeting brief, approved fulfilment plan and proof tracker. | Named project owner and legal/commercial approval for any negotiated term. |
| Release | Outreach, inventory hold, contract, exclusivity, pricing, benefit promise, data sharing, logo use, event activation. | Explicit founder/authorised commercial and legal decision. |

## Qualification: Start with the Property, Not the Sponsor List

Do not build tiers until this information is known or visibly labelled as assumption.

| Property truth | Minimum question |
| --- | --- |
| Objective and ownership | What must the property achieve; who owns it; client-funded, sponsor-funded, ticketed or hybrid? |
| Audience | Who attends/participates/watches; verified size, quality, decision role, geography, behavior and consent/data status. |
| Moment and mechanism | What is the cultural/business moment, experience, content/activation mechanism, and why would an audience care? |
| Inventory | What rights actually exist: naming, stage, content, category, sampling, experience, hospitality, speaking, data, media, post-event content? |
| Constraints | Category conflicts, venue/platform rules, brand safety, privacy/consent, lead time, capacity, production, legal/sector restrictions. |
| Economics | Property cost, funding gap, revenue goal, margin/contingency logic, sales deadline, delivery cost of each benefit. |
| Measurement | What sponsor objective/signal can genuinely be measured; source, owner, consent and reporting date. |

If audience, inventory, economics, and ownership are not known, create only an **internal sponsorship hypothesis**. Do not issue a rate card.

## Core Method

1. **Diagnose the property.** Write the property’s audience promise and commercial reason to exist. Separate confirmed audience/inventory facts from hypotheses.
2. **Map sponsor jobs-to-be-done.** Classify desired sponsor outcomes: awareness, credibility, category association, product trial, lead quality, relationship hosting, recruitment, content, community trust, distribution, or sales enablement.
3. **Build the category map.** Identify category adjacency, audience relevance, conflict/exclusivity implications, timing, budget cycle, brand-safety fit and realistic decision-maker. Do not invent contacts or assert budget availability.
4. **Design the rights and activation system.** Inventory every benefit, its scarcity, delivery owner, delivery cost, evidence source, and sponsor value. Prioritise a small number of high-value, executable rights over many generic logos.
5. **Create three package architectures.** Vary the commercial mechanism: for example title/strategic partner, category platform with owned activation, or targeted hospitality/content/data partner. Explain why each matches a sponsor objective.
6. **Build indicative value and revenue logic.** Link benefits to outcome hypotheses and property P&L. Use comparative/market logic only where sourced; label all estimates. Sponsorship revenue must still protect ZYNTH/project margin and delivery capability.
7. **Prepare the pitch and negotiation guardrails.** Recommend one package, ask, give/get position, proof plan, approval path and next meeting decision. Use `templates/sponsorship-package.md`.
8. **Plan fulfilment and proof.** Before a sale, establish owner, timeline, assets, site/activation requirements, consent/data handling, evidence capture and post-event/reporting commitment.

## Rights Inventory Standard

Every sellable right requires all seven fields below.

| Field | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| Benefit/right | Specific, observable sponsor asset—not “visibility.” |
| Sponsor objective | The job it helps a sponsor do. |
| Audience/context | Who encounters it, at which moment and in what quality. |
| Scarcity and exclusivity | Quantity, category conflicts, duration/territory, approval limits. |
| Delivery owner/cost | Who produces it, budget/resource dependency and deadline. |
| Evidence | What proof can be delivered: photo, delivery log, attendance, scan, consented lead report, content/report. |
| Risk | Privacy, claims, venue/platform restriction, brand suitability, cannibalisation or non-delivery contingency. |

## Valuation and ROI Rules

- Do not manufacture a single “media value” number or equate raw impressions with sponsor ROI.
- Establish the sponsor’s decision/use case first. A B2B sponsor may value named target-account hospitality; a consumer brand may value product trial and attributable offer redemptions; an employer may value qualified recruitment conversations.
- Use an **indicative investment range** only where property economics, benefit scarcity, comparable evidence, delivery cost, and strategic value have been considered. State source, date and uncertainty.
- Every sponsor proposal has a measurement hypothesis, not a guarantee. List baseline/target only where credible; otherwise state the proxy, owner, source and decision it will inform.
- Treat personal data, leads, scans, attendee lists and content rights as governed assets. Collect/share only with lawful permission and agreed purpose; do not promise attendee data as a generic benefit.

## Mandatory Output Contract

A sponsor-ready package must contain:

1. Property executive summary: opportunity, audience truth, objective, dates/location/platform, funding model, decision requested.
2. Sponsor/category opportunity map: why selected categories fit; conflicts/exclusivity and evidence state.
3. Audience and experience: verified/future audience, journey and sponsor-relevant moments.
4. Rights and activation inventory: all rights using the seven-field standard above.
5. Three differentiated package architectures and recommended route.
6. Tier/partner table: benefits, activation, exclusivity, delivery owner, production requirement, evidence and indicative investment/assumptions.
7. Sponsor value narrative: business objective, expected mechanism, measurement hypothesis, proof-of-delivery plan; no unsupported ROI promise.
8. Commercial/P&L logic: revenue target, fulfilment cost, funding/margin/contingency assumptions, sales milestone and approval status.
9. Fulfilment plan: runbook, asset deadlines, rights approvals, event/site/data dependencies and contingency.
10. Negotiation and next step: discovery questions, give/get boundaries, decision maker, legal/commercial approvals and next action.

## Quality Gate

| Dimension | Pass condition |
| --- | --- |
| Property truth | Audience, inventory, ownership and constraints are verified or openly marked as assumptions. |
| Sponsor relevance | Each targeted sponsor/category has a clear business objective and fit—not a generic logo list. |
| Rights quality | Benefits are scarce, tangible, deliverable, owned, costed and evidenceable. |
| Commercial integrity | Indicative price/revenue is grounded in actual delivery economics; no margin or fulfilment blind spot. |
| Measurement and privacy | Sponsor value is a mechanism/hypothesis with a real evidence source and consent boundary. |
| Activation creativity | The proposal gives the sponsor a meaningful role in the audience experience rather than a passive placement. |
| Governance | Exclusivity, contracts, data, brand, venue/platform, rights and approval gates are flagged. |

Client or sponsor-facing output requires 4/5 or better in every applicable category.
