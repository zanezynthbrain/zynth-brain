#### **S.4. Sponsorship Package Creation & Negotiation**

1.  **Sponsorship Package Creation & Negotiation:**
    *   **Action:** Design tiered sponsorship packages with clear benefits and deliverables. Negotiate terms and conditions with potential sponsors.
    *   **AI Integration:** AI can analyze market rates for sponsorship, suggest optimal package structures, and provide negotiation insights based on historical data. AI can also assist in drafting contract clauses.
    *   **Deliverable:** Sponsorship Packages, Negotiation Strategy.
