Creative angles that land · KPIs / what "result" means · Sell vs sponsor.**

## 1. Banking & Finance / Fintech (KBZ, AYA, CB, Yoma, Wave, KPay, OnePay)
- **Audience:** mass retail + young professionals + SME owners + HNWIs. Trust is the
  currency; they buy confidence, security, and status (for premium tiers).
- **Channels:** Facebook (mass), in-app campaigns, KOL for youth wallets, PR for
  trust, events for premium/SME tiers. **Budget:** high, always-on; product/app
  campaigns 50–150M MMK; premium launch events 80–200M MMK.
- **Decision-makers:** Head of Marketing & Corporate Communications; Head of Digital
  Banking; wallet/app Marketing Lead. RFP for large, direct for activations.
- **Creative angles:** "financial empowerment / your future", premium-tier as a
  lifestyle badge, trust through real customer/SME stories, festival digital-gifting
  (Thadingyut). **KPIs:** app installs/actives, premium sign-ups, cost-per-acquisition,
  brand trust lift, earned media.
- **Sell vs sponsor:** SELL — banks fund their own campaigns. (They also SPONSOR
  third-party events → pitch them as sponsors of owned IP too.)

## 2. Telecommunications (MPT, Ooredoo, ATOM, Mytel, Frontiir)
- **Audience:** everyone, skewing youth + data-heavy. Fast-moving, price + experience.
- **Channels:** very heavy digital, app, gaming/esports, influencer, concerts,
  campus/roadshow. **Budget:** very high (biggest ad/event spenders); campaigns
  50–150M MMK; music-festival activations 200–600M MMK. ATOM (rebrand) = unusually
  open door.
- **Decision-makers:** Head of Brand; Events & Sponsorship Lead; Digital & Content Lead.
- **Creative angles:** youth culture, data-plan-as-enabler, UGC challenges, experiential
  concerts/campus. **KPIs:** SIM/plan activations, reach vs benchmark, challenge
  entries, cost-per-activation. **Sell vs sponsor:** SELL (they also sponsor heavily).

## 3. Real Estate & Property (Shwe Taung, Yoma Land, CDSG, Dagon, SPA)
- **Audience:** upgraders + investors (SG+MM), HNWIs. Aspirational; buy a life, not a floorplan.
- **Channels:** premium content/film, launch events, show-units, investor briefings,
  targeted digital + property portals. **Budget:** high; grand openings 150–500M MMK.
- **Decision-makers:** Sales & Marketing Director; Project CEO; Launch & Brand Lead. Direct/referral.
- **Creative angles:** cinematic CGI reveal, "find where you belong", lifestyle
  storytelling, investor ROI narrative. **KPIs:** qualified leads, bookings on the
  night, cost-per-booking. **Sell vs sponsor:** SELL.

## 4. F&B / Restaurants / Hospitality (chains, KFC, Lotteria, YKKO, hotels)
- **Audience:** urban foodies 18–34; families; staycationers. Impulse + social proof.
- **Channels:** Instagram/TikTok/Facebook, food KOLs, in-store/POS, grand openings.
  **Budget:** moderate–high; franchise opening 30–70M MMK; hotel opening 200–500M MMK.
- **Decision-makers:** Marketing Manager; Franchise Owner; Director of Sales & Marketing (hotels).
- **Creative angles:** "menu drop like a sneaker drop", food-styling reels, tasting
  events, seasonal/festival tie-ins. **KPIs:** footfall on drop week, new-item mix %,
  reach, bookings. **Sell vs sponsor:** SELL.

## 5. FMCG / Consumer Goods (Unilever, P&G, Loi Hein, CDSG brands)
- **Audience:** mass consumers, festival-driven. Habit + availability + brand love.
- **Channels:** TV/OOH for mass, heavy social, roadshows/sampling, trade marketing,
  KOLs. **Budget:** high, continuous; roadshow/activation 40–120M MMK.
- **Decision-makers:** Category Manager; Brand Manager. Mix of RFP + direct.
- **Creative angles:** culture-led activation ("pour culture"), festival hydration/
  celebration, sampling-to-social loops. **KPIs:** reach, sampling redemptions, UGC
  volume, sales lift. **Sell vs sponsor:** SELL (big brands also sponsor festivals).

## 6. Automotive (Toyota, Suzuki, Ford, SSS, German Motors)
- **Audience:** affluent 30–55; premium buyers. Status + experience + trust.
- **Channels:** cinematic launch film, showroom events, test-drive experiences, auto
  expos, targeted digital. **Budget:** high for launches; model launch 100–250M MMK.
- **Decision-makers:** Marketing Manager; Head of Sales & Marketing; Dealership Owner.
- **Creative angles:** "the drive" luxury experience, cinematic reveal, curated
  test-drive, lifestyle lounge. **KPIs:** test-drives booked, qualified leads, orders,
  cost-per-lead. **Sell vs sponsor:** SELL.

## 7. Technology / SaaS / Startups (WavePay, KPay, tech startups, regional SaaS)
- **Audience:** MM = consumers/users; SG = B2B decision-makers. Value + proof + speed.
- **Channels:** MM: app installs, KOL, launch events; SG: LinkedIn ABM, webinars,
  content, performance search. **Budget:** MM 30–150M MMK; SG SGD 20–80k+ (B2B ABM).
- **Decision-makers:** Marketing Director; Product Manager; Growth Lead; startup CEO.
- **Creative angles:** product-as-lifestyle, founder/user story, ABM personalisation
  (SG). **KPIs:** installs/actives (MM), MQL/SQL + pipeline (SG), CAC, ROAS.
  **Sell vs sponsor:** SELL.

## 8. Healthcare & Pharma (Pun Hlaing Siloam, Parami, AA Medical, Fame)
- **Audience:** families 28–55; patients; medical professionals (B2B). Trust + authority.
- **Channels:** trust-building content/film, doctor features, health-education carousels,
  community health events, medical seminars (B2B). **Budget:** moderate; content
  series 20–40M MMK/mo; hospital opening 150–400M MMK.
- **Decision-makers:** Head of Marketing & Communications; Hospital Administrator;
  Pharma Marketing Manager.
- **Creative angles:** "in good hands" real doctors + patient stories, calm authority,
  preventive-health CSR. **KPIs:** enquiries/appointments, sentiment, reach.
  **Sell vs sponsor:** SELL.

## 9. Education / EdTech (Strategy First, ILBC, universities, Wall Street English)
- **Audience:** students + parents (intake season). Aspiration + outcome + trust.
- **Channels:** performance social/search (enrolment funnel), open houses, testimonial
  content, campus events. **Budget:** moderate; enrolment funnel 20–45M MMK/mo.
- **Decision-makers:** Admissions Director; Marketing Manager. Direct.
- **Creative angles:** "your future, enrolled", student-outcome stories, funnel to a
  cost-per-enrolment target. **KPIs:** cost-per-lead, applications, enrolments.
  **Sell vs sponsor:** SELL.

## 10. Retail / Fashion / E-commerce (City Mart, Junction, Myanmar Plaza, brands)
- **Audience:** families + young shoppers; deal + experience + trend.
- **Channels:** seasonal social, mall events, loyalty content, influencer, e-commerce
  ads. **Budget:** moderate; mall seasonal event 50–100M MMK.
- **Decision-makers:** Marketing Manager; Head of Brand & Loyalty; Retail Director.
- **Creative angles:** tentpole seasonal festival ("season of belonging"), retail-media,
  drop culture. **KPIs:** footfall uplift, tenant/sales lift, social mentions, ROAS.
  **Sell vs sponsor:** SELL (mall festivals can be tenant/brand-sponsored — hybrid).

## 11. Government / NGO / Non-profit (UNDP, Save the Children, Red Cross, ministries)
- **Audience:** communities, donors, stakeholders. Purpose + credibility + impact.
- **Channels:** awareness campaigns, community events, PR, stakeholder workshops,
  fundraising galas. **Budget:** grant-driven; campaign launch 70–180M MMK; gala
  100–250M MMK. **Decision-makers:** Project Manager; Communications Director. RFP.
- **Creative angles:** human-impact storytelling, measurable-good narrative.
  **KPIs:** reach, behaviour change, funds raised, partnerships. **Sell vs sponsor:**
  SELL to the grant (and pair with corporate CSR sponsors).

## 12. Entertainment / Media
- **Audience:** youth + fans. Culture + participation + hype.
- **Channels:** social-first, KOL, live events, UGC/challenges, streaming tie-ins.
  **Budget:** varies; award show 120–300M MMK. **Decision-makers:** Marketing Lead;
  Producer. **Creative angles:** fandom, participation mechanics, cultural moments.
  **KPIs:** reach, engagement, attendance, earned media. **Sell vs sponsor:** SPONSOR-
  heavy (events funded by brand sponsors) — often the sponsorship model.

## 13. Construction / Industrial (Max Myanmar, Shwe Taung cement, B2B suppliers)
- **Audience:** B2B buyers, project owners, government. Credibility + capability + trust.
- **Channels:** trade shows/exhibitions, corporate content, B2B events, direct + PR.
  **Budget:** B2B trade booth 150–400M MMK. **Decision-makers:** BD Director; Project
  lead. **Creative angles:** capability proof, corporate brand film, case studies.
  **KPIs:** qualified B2B leads, meetings, tender invitations. **Sell vs sponsor:** SELL.

## 14. Agriculture / Agribusiness (CP Myanmar, MAPCO, Loi Hein agri)
- **Audience:** farmers/distributors (B2B) + consumers (food brands). Yield + trust +
  price. **Channels:** trade + community activation, radio (rural reach), Facebook,
  demo events. **Budget:** campaign 40–120M MMK. **Decision-makers:** Marketing/Trade
  Manager. **Creative angles:** yield/livelihood stories, product education, export
  brand storytelling. **KPIs:** trade uptake, awareness, sales. **Sell vs sponsor:** SELL.

## 15. Travel / Tourism / Hospitality group (Memories Group, hotels, airlines)
- **Audience:** local staycationers + regional travellers. Escape + experience + value.
- **Channels:** travel storytelling video, influencer stays, direct-booking offers,
  OTA + social. **Budget:** content + campaign 20–40M MMK/mo. **Decision-makers:**
  Director of Sales & Marketing; Brand Lead. **Creative angles:** "stay a while",
  cinematic destination film, direct-booking beats OTAs. **KPIs:** direct bookings,
  RevPAR lift, reach. **Sell vs sponsor:** SELL.

## 16. Insurance & Financial Services (Grand Guardian, IKBZ, CB Insurance)
- **Audience:** families + SME owners + agents. Trust + education + peace-of-mind.
- **Channels:** trust content, agent events, education campaigns, targeted digital.
  **Budget:** content + events 20–60M MMK. **Decision-makers:** Head of Marketing;
  Agency lead. **Creative angles:** protection stories, education-led, agent enablement.
  **KPIs:** enquiries, policies, agent recruitment. **Sell vs sponsor:** SELL.

## 17. Logistics / E-commerce enablement (Royal Express, Ninja Van)
- **Audience:** consumers + SME/e-commerce sellers (B2B). Reliability + speed + trust.
- **Channels:** brand + performance marketing, B2B content, partner events. **Budget:**
  campaign 30–80M MMK. **Decision-makers:** Marketing Lead; BD. **Creative angles:**
  reliability proof, seller-growth stories. **KPIs:** brand trust, seller sign-ups,
  shipment volume. **Sell vs sponsor:** SELL.

## 18. Beauty / Wellness / Fitness (salons, cosmetics, gyms, clinics)
- **Audience:** urban women + youth. Aspiration + transformation + community.
- **Channels:** Instagram/TikTok-first, beauty KOLs, before/after, in-store/class
  events. **Budget:** social + KOL 15–50M MMK. **Decision-makers:** Owner; Marketing
  Manager. **Creative angles:** transformation stories, ritual/self-care, UGC.
  **KPIs:** bookings, product sales, UGC, footfall. **Sell vs sponsor:** SELL.
