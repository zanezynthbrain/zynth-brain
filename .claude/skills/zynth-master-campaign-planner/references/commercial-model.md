# ZYNTH Commercial Model — pricing, budget, sourcing, profit, sponsorship & ROI

The money layer. Every plan's Sections 6, 9, 13, 14, 15 are built from this.
All figures are **market-rate**, real-world bands — tag anything you can't verify
with `[INDICATIVE — confirm by RFQ]`. Never use central-bank FX.

## Market FX (use these)
- **1 USD ≈ 4,400 MMK** (market rate) · **1 USD ≈ 1.35 SGD**.
- Quote MM plans in MMK with a USD column; SG plans in SGD with a USD column.
- Restate the FX line in the budget so the client sees the assumption.

## Financial law (hard rules — every plan obeys)
| Rule | Value | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Gross-margin floor | **35%** | Never deliver below this. If a plan can't clear it, re-scope/re-price or change model. |
| Gross-margin target | **40%** | What we aim for. |
| Deposit | **50%** | Taken before work starts. |
| Management fee | **15–20% of spend** | ZYNTH's fee on managed media/production. |
| Contingency | **10%** | On production line items. |

**Margin math:** `Gross margin % = (Client price − Direct cost) ÷ Client price`.
Direct cost = supplier + production + paid media at cost + freelance. ZYNTH fee and
margin sit on top. To hit 40% target: `Client price = Direct cost ÷ 0.60`.

## 1. How to price an engagement
Three pricing shapes — pick per client:
- **Project fee** (most pitches): bottom-up itemised budget (Step 9 of the method)
  + management fee + margin to hit ≥40%. Best when scope is defined.
- **Retainer** (always-on / content / community): monthly fee = (est. monthly hours
  × blended rate) + tooling + margin. Good for predictable recurring work.
- **Media + management %** (paid-heavy): cost of media pass-through + 15–20% mgmt
  fee. Used when the client funds ad spend and ZYNTH runs it.

## 4. Supplier & vendor sourcing (Section 12)
- Pull from **ZYNTH's supplier DB** (backend/knowledge video-production rates,
  event landscape) + the sector pack's named vendor types.
- Every line: category · vendor · deliverable · **contact** · indicative rate ·
  lead time · verified/indicative.
- **Never fabricate a contact.** Unknown vendor/contact = `to source` — the plan
  lists the category and the budget line, and flags it for RFQ.
- Always carry **≥2 quotes** for anything material; use the lower for the floor
  check, the realistic one for the client price.
- Production realities to price in: censorship/regulatory lead time (MM 2025 Motion
  Picture Law for video), permits for on-ground, translation/subtitling, font
  licensing (Myanmar Unicode/Pyidaungsu).

## 5. The profit model (what ZYNTH makes)
Show it transparently in Section 14:
- **Direct cost** (suppliers + production + media-at-cost + freelance).
- **ZYNTH management fee** (15–20% of managed spend).
- **Gross margin** (client price − direct cost), as % — must clear 35%, aim 40%.
- **Net after ZYNTH delivery cost** (your own team days at the day-rates above).
- If margin < floor: state it, then show the lever (drop a costly production, shift
  to a retainer, re-tier sponsors, or raise price with justified value).

## 6. Sell vs. sponsor — the model decision
Decide per campaign; justify from the sector's sponsorship dynamics (sector pack).
**Sell the campaign (client-funded)** when:
- The client has budget and owns the outcome (leads/sales/launch).
- The idea's value accrues to one brand.
- → Price as project/retainer; ZYNTH margin from the fee + markup.

**Sponsor-funded** when:
- The idea is a platform/event/content property multiple brands want to be near.
- The audience is valuable to adjacent non-competing brands.
- The client can't/won't fund it all but the property has pull.
- → Build **tiered sponsor packages**. ZYNTH margin from the package sell.

**Sponsor tier template:**
| Tier | Price (indicative) | What they get | Sponsor ROI |
|---|---|---|---|
| Title / Presenting | highest | Naming, logo lockup, on-stage, data | Reach + association + leads |
| Gold | mid | Logo, activation booth, content integration | Reach + sampling |
| Supporting | entry | Logo, social mentions, passes | Awareness |
Always show **each sponsor's own ROI** (audience reached ÷ their fee vs. their normal
CAC/CPM) — that's what makes a sponsor say yes.

## 7. ROI / value proof (Section 15)
Translate the plan into the client's money:
- **Awareness:** reach & recall vs. equivalent media cost (earned + owned value).
- **Lead-gen:** projected qualified leads × client's close rate × deal value vs.
  investment → pipeline value & CPL vs. their benchmark.
- **Sales/e-comm:** ROAS = projected revenue ÷ ad spend; blended CAC vs. LTV.
- **Launch:** trials/sign-ups × conversion-to-paid × value.
- Always show **break-even** (what the campaign must return to pay for itself) and
  make the headline benefit a single, unmissable number.

## 8. Sanity checks before you deliver
- Margin ≥ 35% (ideally 40%)? Contingency 10% in? Deposit 50% stated?
- Every creative promise has a budget line AND a supplier?
- FX = market rate, stated? Unverified numbers tagged `[INDICATIVE]`?
- The client's **result and benefit** expressed as one clear number?
- Sell-vs-sponsor decision made and justified, not dodged?
