# TD.5. Spatial Ergonomics & Traffic Flow

## 1. Traffic Flow Analysis

### 1.1. The "Golden Path"
In exhibition design, the AI should create a primary path that guides visitors through the booth.
- **Entry Points**: Minimum 2m wide to prevent bottlenecks.
- **Dwell Zones**: Areas where visitors stop (e.g., demo screens) should be placed away from the main entry.
- **Exit Points**: Should lead naturally towards the next engagement or the aisle.

### 1.2. Booth Zoning (The 60-30-10 Rule)
- **60% Open Space**: For movement and visibility.
- **30% Interaction Zone**: Counters, demo units, seating.
- **10% Private Zone**: Storage, technical racks, meeting rooms.

## 2. Human Ergonomics

- **Counter Height**: Standard 1000mm - 1100mm for standing interaction.
- **Table Height**: 750mm for seated meetings.
- **Reach Range**: Interactive elements should be between 800mm and 1400mm from the floor.
- **Clearance**: Minimum 900mm around furniture for wheelchair accessibility and comfort.

## 3. Sightline & Branding Placement

- **Primary Branding (The Hook)**: Placed at 3m+ height for long-distance visibility.
- **Secondary Branding (The Detail)**: Placed at eye level (1600mm) for visitors inside the booth.
- **Obstruction Check**: Do not place tall furniture or walls directly in front of the main entry sightline.

## 4. SOP: Ergonomic Layout Check
1.  **Map Aisle Access**: Identify which sides of the booth are open to the aisle.
2.  **Place "Anchors"**: Position the largest elements (backwalls, storage) first.
3.  **Define Paths**: Use 2m wide virtual "corridors" to ensure flow.
4.  **Place Furniture**: Ensure 900mm clearance around all chairs and tables.
5.  **Final Sightline Pass**: Rotate the 3D camera to eye level (1.6m) and walk through the scene to verify visibility of key branding.
