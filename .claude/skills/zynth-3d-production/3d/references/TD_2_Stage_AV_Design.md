# TD.2. Stage & AV Design Fundamentals

## 1. Stage Geometry & Sightlines

### 1.1. Standard Heights
- **Small Room (up to 100 pax)**: 300mm - 450mm.
- **Medium Ballroom (100-500 pax)**: 600mm - 900mm.
- **Large Arena (500+ pax)**: 1200mm - 1800mm.

### 1.2. Sightline Analysis (C-Value)
The "C-Value" represents the clearance between the eye of one row and the top of the head of the row in front.
- **Goal**: C = 60mm to 120mm.
- **AI Check**: When designing a tiered seating arrangement, ensure the stage height and screen placement satisfy the C-Value formula to prevent obstructed views.

## 2. Professional Lighting Setup

### 2.1. The 3-Point Lighting System
For a speaker or central product on stage:
1.  **Key Light**: 45° to the side and 45° up. Main source of light.
2.  **Fill Light**: 45° to the opposite side. Softens shadows.
3.  **Back Light (Hair Light)**: Placed behind the subject. Separates them from the background.

### 2.2. Stage Wash
- **Warm Wash**: 3200K (Tungsten feel).
- **Cool Wash**: 5600K (Daylight feel).
- **RGB/Color Wash**: Used for branding and atmosphere.

## 3. AV & Screen Integration

- **LED Walls**: Pixel pitch (e.g., P2.5, P3.9) determines viewing distance.
    *   *Rule of Thumb*: Viewing distance (m) = Pixel Pitch (mm) * 2.
- **Aspect Ratios**: Standard 16:9, Ultrawide 21:9, or custom creative ratios.
- **Placement**: Bottom of the screen should be at least 1200mm above the stage floor to be visible over the audience's heads.

## 4. SOP: Stage Design Workflow
1.  **Define Stage Deck**: Create the main platform (e.g., 12m x 4m).
2.  **Backdrop Design**: Place LED screens or scenic flats.
3.  **Lighting Rig**: Setup a front truss (FOH) and a back truss for hair lights and effects.
4.  **Speaker/Furniture Placement**: Lectern (left or right), lounge chairs (center for panels).
5.  **Technical Check**: Verify that the screen is visible from the furthest seat in the 3D scene.
