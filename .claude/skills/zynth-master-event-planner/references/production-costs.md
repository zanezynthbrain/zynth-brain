# ZYNTH Event Production Costs — rates & budget build

The money layer for events. Bands are **real market**, Yangon-anchored from ZYNTH's
knowledge base (`backend/knowledge/22_myanmar_event_landscape.md` +
`23_mm_video_production_rates.md`) — tagged `[UNVERIFIED — confirm by RFQ]` where
not yet quote-confirmed. Always carry ≥2 real quotes before a client number.

## Market FX
- **1 USD ≈ 4,400 MMK** (market) · **1 USD ≈ 1.35 SGD**. Never central-bank rate.
- Do NOT use the Manus "1 USD ≈ 5,000 MMK" estimate — MMK bands are the useful part,
  convert them at market FX.

## Financial law (events)
35% gross-margin **floor** · 40% target · 50% deposit · ZYNTH management fee
**15–20% of total event budget** · **10% contingency on production (non-optional)**.
Margin % = (Client price − Direct cost) ÷ Client price. To hit 40%: price = direct ÷ 0.60.

## Budget build (bottom-up)
1. Venue + F&B (headcount-driven) → 2. Production/AV/staging → 3. Talent/content/
   marketing → 4. Crew/staffing → 5. Logistics/permits/security → 6. **10%
   contingency** → 7. **ZYNTH management fee 15–20%** → 8. margin check (≥35%, aim 40%).
Every line: qty × unit × total in MMK + USD at market FX. Tag unverified `[INDICATIVE]`.

## Cultural / seasonal cost & timing notes
- Avoid full-moon / Buddhist Lent (Jul–Oct varies) for non-CSR major launches.
- Peak windows: **Thingyan (Apr)**, **Thadingyut (Oct)**, **Tazaungdaing (Nov)**,
  year-end galas (Dec) — book venues and vendors early; expect premium pricing.
- Bilingual signage (Burmese Unicode/Pyidaungsu + English); Halal/veg F&B; plan for
  Yangon traffic in the run-of-show; permits for outdoor + entertainment.
