# The ZYNTH Event Plan Standard — Output Contract

Every event plan contains ALL of these sections, in order, fully populated. Skipping
one is a stated decision, never a silent gap. Use real tables (not prose) for
anything with numbers, times, or line items. Target length: a complete plan,
typically 6,000–15,000+ words depending on scale.

---

## 0. Cover & one-line ask
- Client, sector, event name, format, date/window, venue/city, guest count,
  prepared-by (ZYNTH), date.
- **The one-line ask:** one sentence — *who engages/sponsors ZYNTH to run what event,
  for what total investment, delivering which single headline result.*

## 1. Executive Summary (1 page)
- The opportunity and why an event is the right vehicle.
- The event concept in 2–3 sentences.
- The headline outcome (result) and the investment.
- Why ZYNTH. Approvable from this page alone.

## 2. Objective, Audience & Guest Profile
- The ONE primary objective (launch / lead-gen / relationship / awareness /
  celebration / sales) and the KPI it maps to.
- Guest profile: who attends, how many, seniority/segment, why they come, what they
  must feel and do. Primary + secondary guest segments.

## 3. The Event Concept & Creative Theme
- **The concept in one line**, then the expansion. Distinctive and ownable.
- **Why this concept** and not the obvious one — territories considered/rejected.
- **The creative world:** theme, look & feel, colour, set/scenic language, motif,
  and the signature "moment" guests will remember and post.
- **Naming, key visual, and tagline** (bilingual where useful).

## 4. Guest Experience Journey (arrival → exit)
- The full arc: pre-event (invite/RSVP) → arrival/registration → welcome →
  programme peaks → networking/activation → close → post-event follow-up.
- Each touchpoint: what the guest sees/feels/does, and the ZYNTH craft that makes it
  land. Call out the **peak moment** the whole event is built around.

## 5. Format & Programme
- Format rationale (conference / gala / launch / roadshow / activation / hybrid).
- **Programme table:** segment · duration · content · speaker/talent · stage need.
- Content/agenda logic, speakers/hosts/MC direction, entertainment, and any
  interactive or experiential elements.

## 6. Production Design & Technical
- **Stage & scenic:** stage design, backdrop, set pieces, branding placements.
- **AV & technical:** screens/LED, sound, lighting, cameras/livestream, power.
- **Décor & spatial:** floor plan/zoning (registration, main, F&B, activation,
  green room), furniture, florals, signage/wayfinding.
- **F&B:** format (seated/canapé/buffet), menu direction, headcount, dietary.
- Format specs and quantities so a vendor could quote from this section.

## 7. Run-of-Show & Operations (how it runs on the day)
- **Detailed run-of-show table:** time · segment · action · who's responsible · cue.
  From crew call to load-out, minute-by-minute for the live window.
- **Crew & roles:** event director, stage manager, production manager, registration
  lead, hospitality, technical crew, security — who owns what (RACI in a line each).
- **Operating SOPs:** registration flow, VIP handling, speaker management, contingency
  triggers, comms/radio protocol, incident escalation.
- **Pre-event timeline** (week-by-week from kickoff to event day) with milestones.

## 8. Suppliers, Vendors & Production Partners
- Table: category · recommended vendor(s) · what they deliver · **contact** ·
  indicative rate · lead time · verified/indicative. Pull from ZYNTH's supplier DB
  and the sector pack; never invent a contact — unknown = "to source".
- Categories to cover: venue, AV/staging, lighting, F&B/catering, décor/florals,
  printing/signage, talent/MC/entertainment, photo/video, security, transport/logistics,
  furniture rental, permits.

## 9. Permits, Safety & Compliance
- Required permits/approvals (venue, municipal, entertainment, F&B, filming) and
  lead times for the market (MM/SG).
- Safety: crowd management, fire/emergency exits, first aid, insurance, weather plan
  (for outdoor). Any censorship/regulatory content approval lead time.

## 10. Marketing & Attendance Plan
- How guests are driven and RSVPs secured: invite strategy, channels, registration
  system, reminders, and (if public) ticketing/promotion.
- On-the-day content capture and amplification plan (social, PR, post-event recap).
- If the event supports a wider campaign, note the handoff to
  zynth-master-campaign-planner.

## 11. Detailed Budget (itemised)
- Full line-item table by category: item · description · qty · unit cost · total
  (local currency + USD) · % of total. Sub-totalled by workstream (venue, production,
  F&B, talent, marketing, ZYNTH fee).
- Market FX stated. **10% contingency line** (non-optional for events). Nothing
  rounded to hide assumptions.

## 12. Funding Model, Investment & ZYNTH Profit
- **The model decision:** sponsor-funded (tiered packages) / ticketed / client-funded
  / hybrid — with rationale (see `sponsorship-and-commercial.md`).
- If sponsor-funded: sponsor tiers, what each gets, target sponsor count, and **each
  sponsor's ROI**. If ticketed: price tiers, target sell-through, break-even attendance.
- Total investment (or sponsorship target), direct cost vs ZYNTH management fee
  (15–20%) vs margin — shown transparently. **Gross margin %** (clear the 35% floor;
  target 40%). Payment terms (50% deposit).

## 13. ROI Projection, Results & Client Value
- **The result the client gets:** projected outcomes vs the objective (leads,
  meetings, sales pipeline, attendance, reach, brand lift) with assumptions shown.
- Value math: what the outcome is worth vs the investment (pipeline value,
  cost-per-qualified-guest, earned media value). Make the benefit unmistakable.
- The value narrative in the client's language.

## 14. Measurement & KPIs
- Table: metric · target · benchmark · how measured · when reported.
- Pre/at/post-event metrics (registrations, attendance rate, engagement, leads
  captured, sponsor satisfaction, post-event conversion).

## 15. Risk Register & Contingency
- Table: risk · likelihood · impact · mitigation · **on-the-day contingency**.
- Cover: low attendance, weather, AV/power failure, speaker/talent no-show, permit
  delay, safety incident, over-run, supplier failure. Every live event needs a Plan B.

## 16. Terms & Why ZYNTH
- Payment, cancellation/postponement, scope-change, liability/insurance, force majeure.
- Client responsibilities (approvals, guest list, assets, access).
- Short, credible "Why ZYNTH" — dual-market event delivery, real market pricing,
  end-to-end production, speed.

## Self-check — the Critic rubric (score before delivering)
Score 1–10 each; do not deliver below 8, or below 8 on financial-law:
- **Commercial logic** — money and funding model make sense?
- **Market grounding** — real MM/SG venue/production numbers, market FX?
- **Concept distinctiveness** — could this only be this client's event?
- **Completeness** — every section full, real run-of-show and budget, no placeholders?
- **Operational realism** — could a stage manager run the day from this? Contingency real?
- **Financial-law compliance** — 35% floor held, 50% deposit, 10% contingency, tagged.
- **Value clarity** — is the client's result and benefit unmistakable?
