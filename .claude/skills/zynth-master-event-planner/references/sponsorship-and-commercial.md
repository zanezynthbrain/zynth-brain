# ZYNTH Event Commercial Model — funding, sponsorship, ticketing, profit & ROI

How an event gets funded and how ZYNTH makes money. Feeds Sections 12–13 of the
event output contract. All figures market-rate; tag unverified `[INDICATIVE]`.

## The funding decision — sponsor / ticket / client / hybrid
Decide per event; justify from the sector's sponsorship dynamics (sector pack) and
the guest value.

**Client-funded (sell the event)** when:
- One client owns the outcome (their launch, their VIP relationships, their leads).
- The audience value accrues to that single brand.
- → Price as a project: itemised budget + management fee + margin to ≥40%.

**Sponsor-funded** when:
- The event is a property multiple brands want to be near (a summit, a festival, an
  awards night, a community platform).
- The guest audience is valuable to adjacent, non-competing brands.
- The originating client can't/won't fund it all but the property has pull.
- → Sell **tiered sponsor packages**; ZYNTH margin comes from the package sell over
  the delivered cost.

**Ticketed** when:
- There's genuine public/professional demand to attend (conference, masterclass,
  entertainment).
- → Price tiers (early-bird / standard / VIP); model break-even attendance.

**Hybrid** (common for summits): title/anchor sponsor + supporting sponsors +
delegate tickets. Layer the models; make sure the combined revenue clears cost +
floor before committing.

## Sponsorship packages (build these when sponsor-funded)
Anchor tiers to the event's total budget: the sponsorship target should cover
direct cost + ZYNTH fee + margin. Typical structure:

| Tier | Indicative price | What they get | Sponsor's ROI story |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Title / Presenting** | 30–40% of target, 1 slot | Naming ("X presents…"), main-stage logo & lockup, speaking slot, premium booth, guest data, hero content | Category ownership + reach + qualified leads |
| **Gold** | 15–20% each, 2–3 slots | Logo on stage/collateral, activation booth, content integration, passes | Reach + sampling + leads |
| **Supporting** | 5–8% each, 4–6 slots | Logo, social mentions, passes, gift-bag insert | Awareness + association |
| **In-kind** | value-matched | Venue/F&B/AV/prizes in exchange for tier benefits | Reduces cash cost; sponsor gets exposure |

**Always show each sponsor's own ROI** — the number that makes them say yes:
`Sponsor ROI = (guests reached × relevance) vs. their normal CAC/CPM`, plus leads
captured and content/earned-media value. A sponsor buys audience access cheaper than
they could buy it in media — prove that.

## Ticketing model (build when ticketed)
- Tiers: early-bird / standard / VIP (VIP = better seating, networking, content access).
- **Break-even attendance** = fixed cost ÷ (ticket price − variable cost/pax).
- Model 3 scenarios (conservative / expected / optimistic sell-through). Never bank on
  optimistic for the floor check.
- Combine with sponsorship to de-risk (sponsors cover fixed cost; tickets are upside).

## ZYNTH profit model (show transparently, Section 12)
- **Direct cost** = venue + F&B + production/AV + talent + crew + logistics + permits
  + marketing + 10% contingency.
- **ZYNTH management fee** = 15–20% of total event budget.
- **Gross margin %** = (client price or total revenue − direct cost) ÷ (price/revenue).
  Must clear **35% floor**; target **40%**.
- If margin < floor: state it and show the lever — trim a costly production element,
  add/upgrade a sponsor tier, raise ticket price with justified value, or convert a
  cash cost to in-kind sponsorship.
- **Payment terms:** 50% deposit before work starts; balance on an agreed schedule
  (e.g. 30% pre-event, 20% post). Sponsors typically pay 50–100% before the event.

## ROI / value proof for the CLIENT (Section 13)
Translate the event into the client's money:
- **Lead-gen / B2B:** qualified guests/meetings × close rate × deal value = pipeline
  value; cost-per-qualified-guest vs. their normal CAC.
- **Launch:** reach + earned-media value + pre-orders/trials × conversion-to-paid.
- **Relationship / VIP:** target accounts in the room × relationship value; retention
  or upsell attributable to the event.
- **Awareness:** attendance + social reach + PR pickups valued vs. equivalent media spend.
- Always give a **break-even** ("the event pays for itself if it generates X") and a
  single headline benefit number the client can't miss.

## Sanity checks before delivering
- Funding model chosen and justified (not dodged)?
- If sponsor-funded: tiers priced to cover cost + fee + margin, each sponsor's ROI shown?
- If ticketed: break-even attendance modelled on a conservative sell-through?
- Margin ≥ 35% (aim 40%)? 10% contingency in? 50% deposit stated? FX = market, stated?
- Client's result/value = one clear, quantified number?
