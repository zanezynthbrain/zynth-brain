## 1. Content Pillar Framework

Content pillars are the 3–5 recurring themes a brand talks about. They ensure content is varied enough to stay interesting but focused enough to build authority.

### How to define pillars:

**Step 1 — The intersection test:**
Good pillars live at the intersection of three things:
- What the brand knows and does (credibility)
- What the audience actually cares about (relevance)
- What competitors are NOT saying well (differentiation)

**Step 2 — Define 3–5 pillars:**
For each pillar, write:

**Pillar Name:** Short, memorable label

**What it covers:** 2–3 sentences describing the territory

**Why this audience cares:** The underlying motivation or tension this pillar taps into

**Content formats that fit:** e.g., tips, case studies, behind-the-scenes, data posts, opinion pieces

**Example content idea:** One specific post concept to make it tangible

**Funnel stage:** Awareness / Consideration / Conversion / Retention

### Recommended pillar mix:
| Pillar Type | % of Content | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Education / Value | 40% | Build authority and trust |
| Brand / Culture | 25% | Build identity and affinity |
| Proof / Social proof | 20% | Drive consideration and conversion |
| Engagement / Community | 15% | Build audience relationship |

## 2. Platform Strategy

Different platforms serve different roles. Don't replicate — differentiate.

### Platform Roles at ZYNTH:

**Instagram**
- Role: Brand expression and aspiration
- Content: Visual storytelling, product/work showcase, short-form tips
- Frequency: 4–5x per week (Feed: 3x, Stories: daily)
- Tone: Polished but human. Confident.
- Format priority: Reels > Carousels > Static

**Facebook**
- Role: Community building and reach (primary platform for Myanmar)
- Content: Longer-form updates, events, boostable content, community interaction
- Frequency: 3–4x per week
- Tone: Slightly warmer, more conversational than Instagram
- Format priority: Video > Link posts > Static image

**LinkedIn**
- Role: B2B credibility and thought leadership
- Content: Industry perspective, case studies, company news, team stories
- Frequency: 2–3x per week
- Tone: Sharp, professional, opinionated — have a point of view
- Format priority: Native articles > Carousels > Text posts with image

**TikTok** (if applicable)
- Role: Brand discovery among younger audiences
- Content: Behind-the-scenes, personality-driven, trending formats with brand angle
- Frequency: 3–5x per week
- Tone: Raw, direct, entertaining — lowest polish, highest authenticity
- Format priority: Native video only

### Myanmar-specific platform note:
- **Facebook is the primary and often only platform that matters** in Myanmar
- Build the entire content strategy around Facebook-first
- Viber for direct communications and community groups
- Instagram growing but Facebook dominant — don't split budget equally

## 4. Content Audit Framework

Use when a brand's existing content feels inconsistent or isn't performing.

Audit across 5 dimensions:

| Dimension | Question | Score (1–5) | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Consistency | Does content look and sound like the same brand every time? | | |
| Variety | Is there a healthy mix of formats and topics? | | |
| Value | Is the audience getting something useful, interesting, or entertaining? | | |
| Conversion | Is content moving people toward a business action? | | |
| Distinctiveness | Could this content only come from this brand, or could anyone post it? | | |

**Output:** Top 3 findings + recommended changes to pillar mix, frequency, or format strategy.

## 5. Content Brief (for Copywriter handoff)

When briefing the ZYNTH Copywriter to execute a specific piece:

**Content Brief Template:**
```
CONTENT BRIEF

Platform:
Format: (e.g., Instagram Carousel, Facebook Video, LinkedIn Article)
Pillar:
Funnel Stage: Awareness / Consideration / Conversion / Retention

Objective:
What should this piece make the audience think, feel, or do?

Audience:
Who specifically is this for? Any nuance for this piece?

The Angle:
The specific idea or entry point for this piece.
(Not just the topic — the specific take or angle.)

Key Message:
The one thing they must take away.

Proof Point / Asset:
Any data, quote, story, or visual to include.

Tone Notes:
Any specific tone direction for this piece.

CTA:
What action should the audience take?

Mandatory Inclusions:
Anything that must appear (hashtags, handles, legal, links).

Reference / Inspiration:
Any examples of similar content done well.
```

## 6. Launch Content Plan

For product launches, brand launches, or major announcements.

### 3-Phase Launch Content Structure:

**Phase 1 — Build Anticipation (1–2 weeks pre-launch)**
- Goal: Create curiosity and awareness
- Content approach: Tease without revealing — hint at a problem, a change, something coming
- Formats: Countdown posts, "something's coming" stories, behind-the-scenes glimpses
- Tone: Intriguing, confident

**Phase 2 — Launch (Day 1–7)**
- Goal: Announce clearly, drive immediate action
- Content approach: Lead with the big idea, not the product features. Explain the "why" before the "what."
- Formats: Hero video, announcement post, carousel explaining the offering, stories for engagement
- Tone: Sharp, excited, clear

**Phase 3 — Sustain & Convert (Weeks 2–4 post-launch)**
- Goal: Deepen understanding, address questions, drive conversion
- Content approach: Use cases, testimonials, behind-the-scenes, FAQ content, social proof
- Formats: Case studies, reviews, tutorial content, DM/comment engagement
- Tone: Confident, helpful, community-building

## 7. Quality Checks

Before delivering any content strategy:
- [ ] Pillars cover all three zones: brand credibility + audience relevance + competitive differentiation
- [ ] Platform strategy has distinct roles — not the same content copy-pasted
- [ ] Calendar has a healthy mix — not all promotional
- [ ] Myanmar plan is Facebook-first
- [ ] Cultural calendar moments are woven in where relevant
- [ ] Content briefs include a specific angle, not just a topic
- [ ] Strategy connects back to business objective — not just "more content"
