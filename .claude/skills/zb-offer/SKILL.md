# ZYNTH Offer Architecture

## ZYNTH Operating Contract

Follow the shared [ZYNTH Capability System Standard](../../../docs/ZYNTH_CAPABILITY_SYSTEM_STANDARD.md). In particular: classify the work band; separate verified facts from assumptions; create three distinct territories for material creative work; make output executable and measurable; pass the relevant quality gate; and preserve founder/project-owner approval before external release, spend, client contact, vendor commitment, or publication.


## Mission

An offer is a **commercial decision system**, not a polished price page. It makes the buyer’s desired outcome, ZYNTH’s mechanism, exact scope, trade-offs, proof, risk controls, investment logic, approval path, and next action easy to understand. Persuasion is legitimate only when it is specific, evidenced, and commercially viable.

## Preflight

Only build a bespoke offer once the opportunity has founder/Growth approval and the minimum discovery facts are present.

| Required fact | Decision question |
| --- | --- |
| Buyer problem/outcome | What commercial, audience, launch, trust, relationship, or delivery outcome matters? |
| Audience/context | Who is affected and in which market/category/moment? |
| Scope/timing | What is in/out, deadline, dependency, existing assets/team/vendor constraints? |
| Buying process | Who decides, who uses, who procures, what evidence or risk must be resolved? |
| Evidence/proof | Which verified relevant method, capability, case, asset or measurement plan supports ZYNTH’s right to win? |
| Economics | Budget range/status, current rate/quote source, cost drivers, margin/contingency rule, payment/contract context. |
| Approval state | Is this internal exploration, client-ready proposal, negotiated revision or approved project? |

If these facts are missing, produce an **offer hypothesis and discovery agenda**, not a price/commitment.

## Method

1. **Restate the job-to-be-done.** Express the business outcome, audience tension and decision moment. Do not substitute a deliverable (“12 posts”) for the client’s outcome.
2. **Select a specific mechanism.** Explain how the ZYNTH route produces value: strategy/creative system, live experience, production/activation, local/regional adaptation, performance learning, or a defined hybrid. Do not claim superiority without proof.
3. **Design three scope architectures.** Vary the outcome/speed/risk/participation mechanism—not only the number of deliverables. State in/out, client responsibilities, dependencies, revision logic and production/commercial implication.
4. **Recommend one route.** State why it best resolves the decision criterion, which risk it handles, what evidence supports it, and what trade-off it carries.
5. **Build value and risk control.** Define valuable but deliverable amplifiers, milestone/review structure, learning/measurement, change control and transparent limits. A risk-control mechanism is not a fake guarantee or money-back claim.
6. **Request finance validation.** Send scope, resource assumptions, current rate/quote sources, delivery/fulfilment cost and terms to `yadana-finance`. Finance validates price, margin, cash timing, contingency and exception requirement.
7. **Prepare the commercial story.** Present investment only after validated scope/price. Use anchors/tiers to make trade-offs clear, not to conceal an unsuitable price.
8. **Set the next decision.** Name the buyer decision, internal approval, owner, date, open questions, and what will happen if scope/price/timing changes.

## Offer Stack

| Layer | Standard |
| --- | --- |
| Outcome | One buyer-relevant result, clearly labelled as objective/hypothesis rather than guaranteed outcome. |
| Mechanism | The specific way ZYNTH will make progress, supported by relevant capability/evidence. |
| Scope | Itemised deliverables, acceptance criteria, in/out, client inputs, revision policy, dependencies and timing. |
| Value amplifiers | Useful additions with real delivery owner/cost; never generic “bonus” clutter. |
| Risk controls | Milestones, discovery, prototype/test, approval gates, scope control, transparent limitation and measurement. |
| Investment | Finance-validated price/range/terms/currency/tax note as applicable; source/date and assumptions visible. |
| Urgency | A real decision/launch/fiscal/capacity/seasonal trigger only; never invented scarcity. |
| Next decision | A clear approval, discovery, stakeholder or revision decision with named owner/date. |

## Scope Architecture Rules

| Architecture | Use when | Required trade-off |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Diagnostic / foundation** | The problem/evidence/brand/measurement system is unclear. | Lower execution volume; greater decision confidence. |
| **Focused activation** | A defined outcome/moment needs a targeted response. | Narrower channels/audience/output; clear measurement window. |
| **Integrated programme** | Multiple workstreams are necessary and resourced. | Higher dependency, governance, timeline and commercial commitment. |
| **Pilot / test** | A meaningful hypothesis can be tested before scale. | Limited claim/scale; pre-agreed learning and scale/stop decision. |

## Mandatory Output

Use `templates/offer-sheet.md`. A client-ready offer must include buyer problem, evidence and assumptions, three scope architectures, recommendation, mechanism, itemised scope/in-out/client responsibilities, milestones/revisions, risk/measurement plan, finance-validated commercial section, decision path and governance note.

## Quality Gate

| Dimension | Pass condition |
| --- | --- |
| Relevance | The offer solves a named outcome for a defined buyer/audience/context. |
| Differentiation | The mechanism is specific and evidenced; no generic “full-service/innovative” language. |
| Scope integrity | In/out, dependencies, timeline, owners, acceptance and revision/change control are clear. |
| Commercial integrity | Finance validates investment/margin/cash/terms; rates and FX have source/date; exceptions are visible. |
| Proof and measurement | Evidence is relevant; outcomes are hypotheses with signal/owner/source rather than promises. |
| Buyer decision | The actual decision-maker/process, risk and next action are addressed. |
| Governance | Founder/commercial approval, external-use state and contractual/legal limitations are explicit. |

## Handoff

- `zb-icp` verifies account/buyer context; `zb-objections` prepares decision risks; `yadana-finance` validates economics; `zynth-master-proposal-writer` and `zb-pitch-kit` turn the offer into a complete presentation/decision package.
- No offer, price, discount, contract term, performance commitment or external delivery is final until the authorised human approval is recorded.
