## 4. Final QC

- [ ] Buyer problem, insight, proposition and recommendation are clear.
- [ ] All scope, deliverable, date, price, currency, term and outcome statements reconcile to the source spine.
- [ ] Every fact/case/metric/image/logo has evidence, permission and approval status.
- [ ] Assumptions, exclusions, dependencies, risks, change control and measurement are visible where relevant.
- [ ] Copy, language, brand, accessibility, visuals and legal/claims review are complete.
- [ ] The exact next decision, owner and deadline are stated.
- [ ] Founder/authorised commercial and client-release approvals are recorded.
