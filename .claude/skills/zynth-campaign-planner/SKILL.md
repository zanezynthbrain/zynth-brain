## 1. Campaign Brief (Core Output)

Always build a brief before any execution planning. This is ZYNTH's standard brief structure.

### Section 1 — Business Context
- What is the client's business situation right now?
- What triggered this campaign — opportunity or problem?
- What does success look like for the business (not just the campaign)?

### Section 2 — Campaign Objective
Write ONE primary objective using this format:
> **[Action verb]** + **[measurable outcome]** + **[audience]** + **[timeframe]**

Example: *"Generate 500 qualified leads from Singapore SME owners within 8 weeks."*

Rules:
- One objective. If the client wants two, plan two separate campaigns.
- Must be measurable — if it can't be tracked, it's not an objective.

### Section 3 — Target Audience
- Primary audience (who we most need to reach)
- Secondary audience (who influences the primary buyer)
- Audience insight: One sharp, true thing about this audience that will shape the campaign

### Section 4 — Campaign Message
- **Single-Minded Proposition (SMP):** One sentence. What do we want the audience to think, feel, or do after seeing this campaign?
- **Supporting messages:** 2–3 reasons to believe the SMP
- **Mandatories:** Anything that must be included (legal, brand, product)

### Section 5 — Channel Plan
For each recommended channel:
- **Channel name**
- **Role in campaign** (awareness / consideration / conversion / retention)
- **Format** (e.g., Meta Feed Video, Google Search, WhatsApp Broadcast)
- **Rationale** (why this channel for this audience)

### Section 6 — Timeline
Structure as campaign phases:
1. **Pre-launch / Teaser** (if applicable): Duration + activity
2. **Launch / Hero moment**: Duration + activity
3. **Sustain / Always-on**: Duration + activity
4. **Wind-down / Retargeting**: Duration + activity

### Section 7 — Budget Allocation
If budget is provided, allocate across:
- Media spend (paid channels)
- Production (creative assets)
- Agency/platform fees
- Contingency (recommend 10%)

If no budget is given, flag this as a required input before planning can be finalised.

### Section 8 — KPIs & Measurement
Define 1 primary KPI and 2–3 secondary KPIs:

| KPI | Type | Target | How Measured |
|---|---|---|---|
| Primary KPI | e.g., Leads | e.g., 500 | e.g., CRM form fills |
| Secondary KPI 1 | | | |
| Secondary KPI 2 | | | |

Always include: How will results be reported, and at what frequency?

## 2. Go-to-Market Launch Plan

For product launches or new business entries. Expand the brief with:

### Pre-Launch Checklist
- [ ] Brand assets ready (logo, colour, fonts, photography)
- [ ] Landing page / website live and tracked
- [ ] CRM or lead capture set up
- [ ] Social channels created and branded
- [ ] Paid media accounts set up with billing
- [ ] Influencer / PR outreach initiated (if applicable)
- [ ] Internal team briefed

### Launch Week Plan
Day-by-day activity plan for launch week across all channels.

### Post-Launch Review (2–4 weeks in)
- What do we measure?
- Decision criteria: when to scale up vs. pivot

## 3. Channel Selection Guide by Market

### Singapore
**High-performing channels:**
- Meta (Facebook + Instagram): Mass reach, strong for B2C and SMEs
- Google Search: High intent, strong for service businesses
- LinkedIn: B2B, corporate, startup ecosystem
- TikTok: Youth and lifestyle segments (under 35)
- WhatsApp Business: Direct CRM, event reminders, loyalty

**Market notes:**
- Singaporeans respond well to proof points — use reviews, stats, awards
- Bilingual copy (English + Mandarin) for CIMB/Heartland audiences
- Festival/seasonal hooks: CNY, Hari Raya, National Day, 11.11, 12.12

### Myanmar
**High-performing channels:**
- Facebook: #1 channel — treat it as the primary platform for all campaigns
- Facebook Messenger: Direct customer communication
- Viber: Secondary messaging channel (widely used)
- Influencer marketing: High trust, community-driven

**Market notes:**
- English + Burmese copy — Burmese script required for broad reach
- Mobile-first always — optimise all assets for mobile viewing
- Peak usage hours: Evening 7–10pm
- Avoid campaigns during politically sensitive periods

## 4. Campaign Sizing Guide

| Client Type | Typical Campaign Duration | Recommended Min. Budget (SGD) |
|---|---|---|
| SME / Local business | 4–8 weeks | $3,000–$10,000 |
| Startup launch | 8–12 weeks | $10,000–$30,000 |
| Regional / Enterprise | 12–24 weeks | $30,000+ |

Flag to client if their budget doesn't match their objectives — underfunding a campaign is a common failure point.

---

## 5. Output Format Standards

All ZYNTH campaign plans should:
- Start with a **1-paragraph Campaign Summary** (the elevator pitch of the plan)
- Use the standard brief structure above
- Highlight any **risks or dependencies** (e.g., "This plan assumes photography assets are ready by Week 2")
- End with **Questions for Client** — anything still needed to finalise the plan
- Be written to be **presented, not just read** — clear enough for a client-facing meeting

---

## 6. Quality Checks

Before delivering any campaign plan:
- [ ] One clear, measurable campaign objective
- [ ] Channel choices justified by audience behaviour, not preference
- [ ] Timeline is realistic — accounts for production and approval time
- [ ] KPIs are trackable with tools the client actually has
- [ ] Budget allocation adds up correctly
- [ ] Myanmar plans use Facebook as primary channel
- [ ] Document ends with open questions / next steps
