# ZYNTH Qualification Patterns

Read this when scoring a named account, incoming lead, sector, or existing-client growth opportunity.

## Signal Types

| Signal | What it may indicate | Validate in discovery |
| --- | --- | --- |
| New product/service/branch | Launch, awareness, demand-generation, experience or content need. | Business objective, launch date, target audience, ownership and budget process. |
| Active paid/social/content campaign | Existing marketing investment or performance concern. | Objective, agency/in-house setup, measurement maturity, creative/media challenge. |
| New senior marketing/brand/GM hire | Strategic reset, supplier review, new growth mandate. | Role mandate, planning calendar, buying authority and partner appetite. |
| Event, sponsorship, expo or partnership | Audience/relationship opportunity and operational demand. | Property ownership, funding, audience, rights, timeline and supplier status. |
| Expansion, funding, distribution/retail change | Growth/launch moment. | Priority market, commercial goal, timeline and decision makers. |
| Recruitment / employer-brand signal | Talent, culture, internal communication or employer proposition need. | Hiring priority, audience, internal sponsorship and success measure. |
| Competitor activity | Category pressure or learning opportunity. | Whether the target sees it as relevant and has a differentiated response need. |
| Referral / inbound inquiry | Existing trust and potential urgent intent. | Scope, problem, decision process, budget range and why now. |

A public signal is a starting hypothesis, never proof of a current project or budget.

## Discovery Questions by Need

### Campaign / Growth

1. What commercial behavior or decision needs to change, and for whom?
2. Why is this important in this period rather than later?
3. What has already been tried, what did the evidence show, and what must be different?
4. How will the organisation judge success; what data exists now?
5. Who owns the commercial objective, brand decision, budget and final approval?

### Event / Sponsorship

1. What must a live experience accomplish that other media cannot?
2. Who is the intended guest, what should they experience, and how will attendance/quality be validated?
3. Who owns the property, funding, venue, risk, contracts and delivery decision?
4. What rights/inventory exist today; what needs a partner/vendor/venue confirmation?
5. What are the commercial and operational non-negotiables: dates, budget, margin, deposit, safety, permits and plan B?

### Video / Creative Production

1. What is the single message and action the film must earn?
2. Where will it be watched, in what context, and how does that change format or hook?
3. Which brand/product/claim/talent/location facts are verified and approved for depiction?
4. What production route and rights boundaries are acceptable?
5. What delivery/review/measurement decision will the video support?

## Right-to-Win Map

A valuable ZYNTH account strategy connects a target need to a specific proof/method, not a generic statement that the agency is “full service.”

| Target need | Potential ZYNTH right-to-win | Proof required before pitching |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Complex launch / category education | Strategy + creative + content/video + measurement system. | Relevant case, sector understanding, outcome logic and delivery capacity. |
| Live experience / owned IP | Complete event/property concept, sponsor model, production/run-of-show and content amplification. | Event method, production feasibility, current vendor/venue evidence and commercial model. |
| Myanmar/Singapore adaptation | Local-language/cultural execution with regional operating discipline. | Verified language/culture reviewer, local partner/production plan and market evidence. |
| Performance creative problem | Creative testing system linked to media/measurement. | Tracking readiness, media account access/approval and test framework. |
| Brand/system inconsistency | Strategic proposition and scalable brand/content system. | Brand audit, governance model, stakeholder decision path and source assets. |

## Anti-Patterns

- Do not score a large company as high fit solely because it is large.
- Do not treat a publicly visible contact as the actual buyer or as consent for unsolicited engagement.
- Do not confuse a sophisticated website with capacity, active marketing need or agency dissatisfaction.
- Do not pitch every service. Recommend one discovery conversation and one high-confidence next step.
- Do not build a bespoke full proposal before a founder-approved Priority opportunity and minimum discovery evidence exist.
