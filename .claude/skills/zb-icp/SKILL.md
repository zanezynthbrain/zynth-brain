# ZYNTH ICP and Prospect Intelligence

## ZYNTH Operating Contract

Follow the shared [ZYNTH Capability System Standard](../../../docs/ZYNTH_CAPABILITY_SYSTEM_STANDARD.md). In particular: classify the work band; separate verified facts from assumptions; create three distinct territories for material creative work; make output executable and measurable; pass the relevant quality gate; and preserve founder/project-owner approval before external release, spend, client contact, vendor commitment, or publication.


## Mission

Help ZYNTH spend relationship-building and proposal effort where it has the strongest right to win. A fit score is not a guess about whether a company is “big”; it is a transparent view of **need, capacity, access, timing, strategic fit, and evidence**.

## Preflight

Classify the request as one of the following before research begins.

| Request | Required output | Decision owner |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ICP / sector | A segment-level ICP Card and account-selection criteria. | Founder/Growth lead |
| Named account | A Prospect Card and research plan. | Founder/Growth lead |
| Incoming lead | A qualification card, project record recommendation and questions for discovery. | Founder before commercial movement |
| Existing client expansion | Account growth map and next-best-offer hypothesis. | Account Director/founder |

Capture market, sector, service hypothesis, target outcome, known relationship, timing, source of lead and any verified client facts. Ask up to three questions only when those missing inputs change the target/account decision.

## Evidence Protocol

Use first-party/client information first, then official company sources, current campaigns/pages, credible news, public filings/announcements, event/partner signals and relationship intelligence. Record the source and date for meaningful claims. Never infer spend, authority, contact details, pain, procurement status, or budget as fact.

| Evidence label | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Confirmed** | Client/lead directly stated it or reliable current source supports it. |
| **Observed** | Visible public signal such as a campaign, event, expansion, job post, launch or partner announcement. |
| **Hypothesis** | Plausible account pattern that needs discovery confirmation. |
| **Unknown** | A decision-critical fact that must not be guessed. |

## Qualification Method

1. **Define the buying context.** Identify market, category, organisation type, decision cycle, likely business trigger and ZYNTH service relevance.
2. **Map the buying group.** Distinguish economic buyer, functional buyer, champion, user/influencer, procurement/legal gate and potential blocker. Record role hypotheses, never invented individual contacts.
3. **Translate deliverables into outcomes.** State the business problem in outcome language: demand, launch confidence, trust, retention, category growth, qualified meetings, attendance, product trial, employer brand, etc.
4. **Collect account signals.** Seek observed triggers: expansion, new product/venue, funding, fiscal cycle, active campaign, sponsorship, retail change, executive move, recruitment, event, competitor move or reputational issue.
5. **Score six dimensions.** Use the table below and attach evidence/uncertainty to every score.
6. **Recommend a route.** Prioritise, nurture, research further, refer, or decline. A Hot score still requires founder confirmation before outreach or project movement.
7. **Create the discovery agenda.** Use the missing facts and hypotheses to form a valuable first conversation—do not send a generic service pitch.

## Fit Score

Score each dimension 0–3. Scores are a prioritisation aid, not a prediction of revenue.

| Dimension | 0 | 1 | 2 | 3 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Need intensity | No visible/confirmed need. | Generic possibility. | Plausible current trigger. | Confirmed/observed time-bound trigger. |
| Strategic fit | Weak/non-core service fit. | One-off fit. | Clear service/category fit. | Strong ZYNTH edge and repeatable account potential. |
| Capacity / commercial viability | Unknown/clearly unsuitable. | Weak/unverified signal. | Credible capacity or active comparable activity. | Confirmed scope/budget logic or sustained signal. |
| Access / relationship | No path identified. | Indirect path only. | Plausible stakeholder path. | Confirmed warm access/active inbound/meeting. |
| Timing | No timing. | Long/unfocused horizon. | Relevant planning window. | Explicit decision/launch/event window. |
| Proof/right-to-win | No relevant proof. | General capability only. | Relevant ZYNTH case/method exists. | Strong specific proof, referral or differentiated asset. |

| Total | Route |
| ---:| --- |
| 15–18 | **Priority** — founder review, bespoke discovery and account plan. |
| 10–14 | **Develop** — targeted research/nurture; do not over-invest in a pitch. |
| 6–9 | **Watch** — add to signal monitoring; improve evidence or wait for a trigger. |
| 0–5 | **Park/decline** — document reason; do not create generic outreach volume. |

## Mandatory Output: Prospect Card

Use `templates/prospect-card.md`. Every card includes identity/public source, business/outcome hypothesis, buying group map, verified/observed signals, ZYNTH right-to-win, score/evidence, discovery questions, recommended offer hypothesis, risks, explicit unknowns and founder decision required.

## Quality Gate and Governance

- A card fails if it includes invented names, emails, phones, budget, campaign performance, decision authority, or pain points presented as facts.
- A score fails if it lacks evidence or explicitly labelled uncertainty.
- Any automation may research and draft. Only the founder/authorised growth owner can approve the account for outreach, client-facing proposal, or project creation.
- Store only appropriate business/professional information, respect source/platform terms and privacy, and do not treat publicly visible data as consent to unsolicited communication.

- **Pitch collateral:** `zb-pitch-kit`.
- **Approved real opportunity:** create/confirm the project using the founder approval workflow.
