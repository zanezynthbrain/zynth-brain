# ZYNTH Objection Diagnosis Reference

## Decision Tree

1. **Is the objection specific?** If not, ask a clarifying question. “We need to think” may mean price, risk, timing, authority, comparison, or no priority.
2. **Is it evidence, offer, commercial architecture, internal process, or fit?** Do not answer an evidence gap with a discount or a fit gap with a slide deck.
3. **Can ZYNTH address it honestly?** If yes, identify the appropriate proof/option. If no, state the limitation and avoid over-selling.
4. **Who must decide?** A champion may like the proposal but need finance/procurement/CEO/legal sign-off. Prepare the decision artifact for that role.
5. **What is the smallest legitimate next commitment?** A discovery meeting, stakeholder workshop, scoped diagnostic, revised option, evidence review, or respectful close.

## Response Sheet Prompts

| Field | Prompt |
| --- | --- |
| Exact buyer language | What did they actually say or write? |
| Context | Who said it, when, at which decision stage, after which proposal/meeting? |
| Underlying concern hypothesis | What risk or decision criterion may sit beneath it? Mark as hypothesis. |
| Evidence available | Which verified case, scope detail, measurement approach, quote, reference, or stakeholder can help? |
| Honest limitation | What cannot be promised, verified, discounted or delivered? |
| Option | What phased scope, discovery, proof, timeline or stakeholder route may address the legitimate concern? |
| Trade-off | What changes if scope/investment/timing/risk changes? |
| Next ask | What exact decision, meeting, input, or owner is requested? |

## Translation and Tone

For EN/MM material, transcreate the decision logic; do not translate slogans word-for-word. Keep the buyer’s dignity intact. Avoid pressure tactics, false urgency, “closing” language, or accusations that the buyer “does not understand value.”

## Pre-Emptive Proposal Use

Include an objection-pre-emption section only when it resolves a known decision risk. It should state: the concern, the evidence/approach, the limitation, and the next assurance—not a defensive FAQ. Example themes include implementation risk, scope clarity, approval route, measurement, and milestone control.
