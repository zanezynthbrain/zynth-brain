# ZYNTH Copy System

## Creative Route Patterns

Use a distinct mechanism before drafting. Do not label a tone as a strategy.

| Route | Useful when | Copy move | Risk to avoid |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Proof | Audience needs confidence or evidence. | Make the proof concrete, intelligible and relevant. | Unsupported metric/claim or dry feature list. |
| Transformation | The audience sees a before/after tension. | Show a meaningful shift in capability, identity or experience. | Vague “better future” language. |
| Contrast | A category assumption needs challenging. | Expose the old way, then replace it with a credible alternative. | Insulting competitors or false binary. |
| Utility | The audience wants a useful action/tool. | Lead with value they can use now. | Hiding the brand role or overloading the CTA. |
| Participation | The audience can contribute or make the idea theirs. | Make rules, reward, safety and invitation clear. | Empty “challenge” mechanics. |
| Ritual | The product belongs in a repeatable moment. | Name and dramatise the moment. | Cultural appropriation or forced habit claim. |
| Character | A human perspective makes the truth more relatable. | Give the speaker agency and a believable voice. | Invented testimonial, stereotype or forced mascot. |

## Claim Classification

| Claim type | Example | Required action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Verified product/service fact | Feature, availability, price, policy. | Cite approved source/owner/version. |
| Performance/outcome claim | “Increases sales”, “most trusted”, ROI. | Require substantiation and legal/brand approval; otherwise recast as objective or hypothesis. |
| Customer/third-party proof | Quote, testimonial, partnership. | Confirm permission, exact wording and source. |
| Comparative/superlative | “Best”, “first”, “only”, competitor comparison. | Verify basis, market/period and approval. |
| Urgency/offer | Deadline, limited stock, price. | Confirm live operational truth and expiry owner. |
| Cultural/community statement | Festival, community, heritage language. | Obtain local/qualified review; avoid generic symbolism. |

## EN/MM Adaptation Rule

Translate the **intended effect**, not the word order. Record: source text, intended reaction, audience, formality, platform, required approved terms, reviewer, and final version. Use native Unicode and typeset final copy in the composition tool.

## Variant/Test Matrix

| Variable | Example | Keep constant |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hook | Proof vs tension vs question. | Objective/audience/offer. |
| Message framing | Functional benefit vs emotional gain. | Claim accuracy and CTA destination. |
| CTA | Learn, register, book, shop. | Actual user journey/landing destination. |
| Format | Short video caption vs static post vs LinkedIn. | Proposition and brand voice. |

Test one meaningful variable at a time where performance measurement is available. A stylistic preference is not evidence of effectiveness.
