# ZYNTH Capability System Standard

**Version:** 1.0  
**Applies to:** Every ZYNTH skill, agent, SOP, role charter, project brief, proposal, creative package, knowledge note, and client-facing output.

## Purpose

ZYNTH does not compete by producing more generic content. It competes by converting a real commercial opportunity into a **distinctive, executable, measurable, and commercially sound solution**. This document is the operating contract that makes that standard repeatable for AI agents, human specialists, and hybrid teams.

A skill is not a paragraph describing what a role should do. It is a reusable decision system. It must tell the operator what to qualify, what evidence to use, which method to apply, what artefact to create, what quality bar must be met, what must be approved, and how the work advances to the next team.

> **ZYNTH standard:** Do not present an idea as a solution until it is strategically rooted, creatively distinctive, operationally feasible, commercially viable, and measurable.

## The ZYNTH Work Bands

Every request must be classified before work begins. The band determines how much freedom the agent has and where human approval is compulsory.

| Band | Purpose | Typical work | Required approval |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Explore** | Discover signals and create internal possibilities. | Market scans, trend signals, opportunity hypotheses, concept territories. | None to draft; founder review before a real lead/project is created. |
| **Propose** | Convert an approved opportunity into an internal or client-ready proposal. | Campaign/event/sponsorship proposal, plan, budget hypothesis, pitch pack. | Founder or designated commercial owner before sending externally. |
| **Execute** | Produce work within an approved scope, budget, brand kit, and schedule. | Design variations, production plan, media setup, vendor RFQ, reporting. | Project owner; founder for scope, risk, spend, or client-commitment changes. |
| **Release** | Publish, send, spend, contract, book, or represent the client. | Client email, social publishing, paid-media launch, vendor instruction, contract. | Explicit human approval only. |

No AI agent may silently move work from one band to another. An internal concept is not a client promise. A draft visual is not approved brand work. A model estimate is not a quoted price.

## The Universal Capability Contract

Every ZYNTH skill must include these nine elements. A compact `SKILL.md` may route details to `references/`, `templates/`, and `scripts/`; it must not omit the operating contract.

| Element | Required content | Test of adequacy |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **1. Trigger and scope** | When to use the skill, what it owns, what it does not own, and handoff skills. | An operator can select the right skill without guessing. |
| **2. Qualification / input contract** | Mandatory facts, useful context, assumptions, and the maximum number of clarification questions. | The skill does not invent client, market, brand, budget, legal, or performance facts. |
| **3. Method** | Ordered workflow with conditional branches and decision rules. | Another competent operator can repeat the work. |
| **4. Knowledge and evidence** | Source hierarchy, currentness requirements, named internal sources, and how to label uncertainty. | Facts are traceable; unverified inputs are visibly qualified. |
| **5. Creative and strategic standard** | How to reach insight, proposition, territories, craft, originality, and relevance. | The result is more than a formatted commodity output. |
| **6. Commercial and operating logic** | Budget/rate discipline, scope, margin, owner, dependencies, timing, and feasibility. | The idea can be produced without breaking the project economics. |
| **7. Output contract** | Exact artefacts, required sections, file formats, naming, and delivery location. | A reviewer knows what “done” means. |
| **8. Quality gate** | Scorecard, pass threshold, checks, revision loop, and escalation triggers. | Weak work is repaired or stopped before client release. |
| **9. Approval, handoff, and learning** | Decision owner, next role, state change, evidence to save, and post-project learning rule. | Work moves cleanly and the system improves from verified outcomes. |

## Universal Workflow

The following sequence is mandatory unless a specialist skill documents a justified exception.

### 0. Preflight: classify the work

Identify the work band, client/project status, market, industry, objective, delivery date, budget/funding status, named owner, risk level, and external-release status. If two or more critical inputs are absent, ask at most three high-value questions or proceed only as an explicitly labelled **internal hypothesis**.

### 1. Define the commercial problem

Write one sentence in this format:

> **For [specific audience], who experience [real tension/barrier], help [brand/organisation] achieve [measurable business outcome] by making [single desired action/belief] feel possible.**

Do not use a deliverable (“make a video” or “run an event”) as the objective. The method must first establish why that deliverable is the right solution.

### 2. Build the evidence frame

Separate facts, supplied claims, research findings, assumptions, and open questions. Use this hierarchy:

| Evidence type | Permitted use | Labelling rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Client-validated data, signed scope, first-party analytics, confirmed quote | Decision and client output. | Cite source/date internally. |
| Credible current external source or verified partner/vendor response | Direction and indicative planning. | Cite source/date; state limitations. |
| Internal historical result with comparable context | Benchmark or hypothesis. | State comparable conditions; do not promise repeat performance. |
| AI inference, trend pattern, or incomplete desk research | Internal exploration only. | Label **hypothesis** or **validate before external use**. |

### 3. Create three differentiated territories

For material creative, campaign, event, video, brand, or sponsorship work, produce three territories before choosing one. Each territory needs a human tension, single-minded proposition, organising idea, proof of relevance, signature expression, possible production system, risks, and reason not to choose it.

Do not create three cosmetic variations of the same idea. Deliberately vary the strategic mechanism: for example utility, participation, status, ritual, access, community, transformation, or proof.

### 4. Select and make the idea executable

Choose a territory with a written rationale. Translate it into a deliverable system: channel roles, experience journey, production approach, source files, copy/design requirements, measurements, dependencies, schedule, owner, and commercial implication. If a production element cannot be briefed, quoted, built, operated, and measured, it is still a concept—not an execution plan.

### 5. Package the decision

Prepare the correct package for the work type. The package must clearly distinguish **confirmed** information from **assumptions** and never fabricate numbers, permissions, market prices, partners, contacts, performance results, or legal clearance.

### 6. Score, revise, and release only through the correct gate

Use the ZYNTH Quality Gate. Any material output under 4/5 in a mandatory category requires a revision; any safety, legal, cultural, data, brand-rights, or commercial breach blocks release regardless of score.

## ZYNTH Quality Gate

Score each category from 1–5. A client-facing proposal, concept, asset, or plan must achieve **at least 4 in every applicable mandatory category** and an average of 4.2 or higher.

| Category | Question | Mandatory for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Business and audience truth | Does it address a specific business objective and audience tension? | All material work |
| Strategic clarity | Is there one defensible proposition and a clear reason this mechanism should work? | Campaigns, proposals, creative, events |
| Distinctiveness | Is it recognisable, culturally relevant, and not a category cliché or a generic AI aesthetic? | Creative, events, video, social, brand |
| Craft and brand fidelity | Are visual, copy, language, tone, accessibility, typography, and brand-system decisions controlled? | All client-facing creative |
| Operational feasibility | Can it be produced, procured, scheduled, staffed, and safely delivered? | Events, video, 3D, experiences, production |
| Commercial viability | Does scope, budget logic, margin, rights, resource need, and funding model hold? | Proposals and execution plans |
| Measurement integrity | Are KPIs tied to a decision, signal, baseline/target where available, owner, source, and review date? | Campaigns, paid media, events, social, sponsorship |
| Risk and governance | Are cultural, legal, privacy, safety, platform, claims, brand-rights, and client-approval risks addressed? | All work leaving Explore band |

## Core Package Standards

### Campaign / Omnichannel Package

A campaign package includes the opportunity, audience, insight, objective, proposition, three territories, selected platform, message hierarchy, channel roles, customer journey, content/activation system, media hypothesis, measurement plan, test plan, budget assumptions, production scope, timeline, risk register, and recommendation.

### Event / Experiential Package

An event package includes objective, guest profile, strategic reason for live, three creative territories, selected theme, experience journey, programming, spatial/production design, run-of-show, crew/RACI, venue/vendor assumptions, safety/permits, itemised indicative budget, funding model, margin logic, sponsor/ticket/client value, measurement, risk/contingency, and commercial terms.

### Sponsorship Package

A sponsorship package includes property/audience truth, sponsor category map, sponsor objectives, inventory and rights, exclusivity, tier architecture, value narrative, activation concepts, measurement plan, rate-card logic, fulfilment plan, lead-generation/data permissions, and post-event proof-of-delivery. Never equate impressions with sponsor ROI without a stated measurement method.

### Video / Motion Package

A video package includes communications objective, platform/audience behavior, narrative mechanism, script, shot/storyboard, treatment, visual and sound references, make-versus-generate decision per shot, talent/location/rights assumptions, production plan, edit rhythm, deliverables/cutdowns/subtitles, QC, cost assumptions, and release checklist. No generated footage is considered client-ready without a rights, likeness, brand, and craft review.

### 3D / Spatial Package

A 3D package includes the brief, dimensions/scale assumptions, venue/site constraints, circulation/guest capacity, brand kit, style references, model hierarchy, materials, lighting/camera plan, rendering specification, buildability notes, bill-of-quantities inputs, technical/risk checklist, `.blend` source file, preview render, export requirements, and revision log. A beautiful render without scale, buildability, and a controlled source file is concept art, not an event design.

### Social / Creator / Challenge Package

A social or TikTok challenge package includes the cultural observation, participation mechanic, creator role, rules, accessibility, soundtrack/rights status, moderation plan, safety/brand-suitability checks, content sequence, channel adaptation, production templates, community response plan, measurement, and escalation rule.

## Commercial and Brand Safeguards

- Treat all budgets, rates, vendor details, performance claims, and legal/regulatory statements as **indicative** until verified against current source evidence, a quote, or client confirmation.
- Use ZYNTH’s stated 35% minimum gross-margin rule for project planning; require written founder approval for an exception. The rate source and rate date must travel with a quotation.
- Require client/founder approval before external client communication, publishing, paid-media changes, vendor commitment, purchase, contract, or sensitive/culturally significant content.
- Do not generate readable Burmese/Myanmar typography, legal copy, logos, trademarked product details, sensitive cultural/religious imagery, or public-figure likenesses inside image/video models. Compose verified elements after visual review.
- Do not claim generated content, research, outcome, benchmark, vendor capability, or media result has been verified when it has not.

| --- | --- | --- |

| Creative Director | Generate options, articulate creative logic, flag clichés and production constraints. | Select territory, protect originality, taste, client confidence, and ethics. |
| Producer / Operations | Draft scopes, schedules, runbooks, risk prompts, vendor comparison structure. | Confirm feasibility, rates, compliance, resourcing, vendor and event-day decisions. |
| Account / Growth | Prepare account plan, proposal story, meeting notes, opportunity signals. | Manage relationship, expectation, negotiation, commitments, and escalation. |

| Founder | Receive opportunity intelligence and drafts. | Approve projects, commercial exceptions, strategic shifts, releases, and commitments. |

## The Knowledge Architecture

The current repository should use four distinct knowledge classes. Do not mix them in one generic file.

| Class | Content | Owner | Review cadence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Evergreen operating IP** | Methods, templates, rubrics, roles, naming standards, decision rules. | COO / capability owner | Quarterly or after material learning. |

| **Learning library** | Reusable evidence from completed, measured work. | Capability owner | Promote only after verification. |

The Git repository is the source of truth for executable skill definitions. The Vault/Drive is a reader-friendly mirror. Mirror documents must provide a concise summary **and** link to the full source skill, relevant method, template, SOP, and active version; a 27-line role card must never be positioned as the full operating manual.

## Role Charter Standard

Every human role and agent role must be defined with the following sections: mission, decision rights, accountabilities, recurring cadence, input/output contracts, KPIs with definitions and source, required competencies/tools, quality thresholds, escalation triggers, interfaces/RACI, and a 30/60/90-day success plan. A task list is not a role charter.

## Review and Improvement Cycle

Every Friday, the capability owner reviews: work produced, work accepted/rejected, revision causes, QC scores, client feedback, scope/margin variance, production incidents, and outcome evidence. Promote a lesson into a skill only when it is specific, repeated or materially consequential, evidence-backed, and assigned to an owner. Remove stale rates, unsupported market claims, and unused references.
