## The commercial finding — this is the decision
**At full scale the event does not clear the 35% floor until 76% of inventory sells.**
That is an unsafe bar for a first edition.

| | Cost | Clears 35% floor at | Hits 40% target at |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Option A — full scale | 113.3M MMK | 76% sell-through | 83% |
| **Option B — lean** ✅ | **70.4M MMK** | **43%** | **47%** |

**Recommendation: launch lean, upgrade with money in the bank.** Sponsor-facing
prices are identical either way — only ZYNTH's cost base changes.

**Biggest single lever: the venue.** A hotel taking Gold in-kind removes 6–16M of
cost *and* fills a tier. Hospitality is already on the sponsor target list
(Lotte, Sedona, Novotel, Melia). **Do this first.**
