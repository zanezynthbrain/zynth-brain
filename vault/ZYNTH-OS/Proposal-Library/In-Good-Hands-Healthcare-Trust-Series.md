# In Good Hands — Healthcare Trust Series

**Sector.** Healthcare & Pharma

**Target audience.** Families, 28–55

**Type.** Campaign

**The big idea.** A trust-building content series: real doctors, real patient stories, calm authority — turning a hospital into the name families recommend.

**Key deliverables.**
- Content strategy
- 6–8 patient/doctor films
- Health-education carousels
- Community management
- Monthly report

**KPIs.** Reach · sentiment · enquiry/appointment lift

**Indicative budget.** MMK 20–40M/mo (~S$6–11k) · market FX, RFQ vendors, 50% deposit

**ROI angle.** Enquiries attributed to campaign content vs baseline.

**ZYNTH edge.** Cinematic trust films, not stock.
