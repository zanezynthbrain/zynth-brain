# Data That Moves You — Youth Telecom Campaign

**Sector.** Telecom & Technology

**Target audience.** Gen-Z & students, 16–26

**Type.** Campaign

**The big idea.** A 6-week social-first campaign built on a single insight: youth don't buy data, they buy the moments data unlocks. UGC creators + a challenge mechanic.

**Key deliverables.**
- Content pillars + calendar
- 8–12 reels/mo
- Creator partnerships (tiered)
- Meta/TikTok paid
- Weekly performance report

**KPIs.** CPM & reach vs benchmark · challenge entries · SIM/plan activations

**Indicative budget.** MMK 25–45M/mo (~S$7–13k) · market FX, RFQ vendors, 50% deposit

**ROI angle.** Cost-per-activation tracked end-to-end; scale winners, cut losers weekly.

**ZYNTH edge.** Performance-creative testing framework.
