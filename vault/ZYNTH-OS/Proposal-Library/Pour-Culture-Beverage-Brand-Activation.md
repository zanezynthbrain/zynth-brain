# Pour Culture — Beverage Brand Activation

**Sector.** FMCG & Consumer Goods

**Target audience.** Urban 18–35 social crowd

**Type.** Campaign

**The big idea.** Turn a beverage into a scene: a shareable ritual + city activations + creator seeding, engineered for muted-feed stopping power.

**Key deliverables.**
- Big idea + key visual
- Activation kit (3 sites)
- Reels + stills batch
- Creator seeding
- Sampling logistics

**KPIs.** Reach & engagement rate · sampling redemptions · UGC volume

**Indicative budget.** MMK 30–60M (~S$9–17k) · market FX, RFQ vendors, 50% deposit

**ROI angle.** Footfall → sampling → social loop measured per site.

**ZYNTH edge.** Producer-led shoots at boutique cost.
