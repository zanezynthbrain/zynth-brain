# Your Future, Enrolled — Education Funnel

**Sector.** Education & EdTech

**Target audience.** Students & parents (intake season)

**Type.** Campaign

**The big idea.** An enrolment engine: top-of-funnel stories + mid-funnel proof + a retargeted lead form — built to hit a cost-per-enrolment target.

**Key deliverables.**
- Funnel design
- Story + testimonial content
- Landing page
- Paid search/social
- Weekly funnel report

**KPIs.** Cost-per-lead · applications · enrolments

**Indicative budget.** MMK 20–45M/mo (~S$6–13k) · market FX, RFQ vendors, 50% deposit

**ROI angle.** Cost-per-enrolment is the whole scoreboard.

**ZYNTH edge.** Measurement-led, not vanity reach.
