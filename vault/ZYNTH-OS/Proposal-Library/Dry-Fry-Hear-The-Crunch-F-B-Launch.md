# Dry Fry — Hear the Crunch ★ full client-ready

**Sector.** F&B / QSR (fried chicken)

**Market.** Myanmar (Yangon)

**Type.** Brand launch — campaign + opening event

**The big idea.** In a market of soft, greasy, same-tasting fried chicken, Dry Fry owns the one thing you can actually hear — the crunch. Dry-fried, boldly spiced, never greasy. **Hear the Crunch.**

**Key deliverables.**
- Hero "Crunch" film (sound-first / ASMR)
- #HearTheCrunch UGC challenge
- "First Crunch" opening event + crunch wall
- 6 food KOLs incl. 1 top-tier
- Delivery "crunch-box" + promo · 6-week paid

**KPIs.** 2,500–4,000 opening-week covers · 3,000–5,000 delivery orders (month 1) · 25–35% repeat-visit · cost/trial ≤ MMK 1,200

**Investment (3 tiers).** Essential 24M · **Signature 46M (recommended)** · Flagship 82M MMK · 50% deposit · market FX.

**ZYNTH edge.** Owns a sensory territory (crunch/sound) no competitor claims — the food itself becomes the ad, built for sound-first platforms.
