# Season of Belonging — Mall Festival

**Sector.** Retail & Shopping Malls

**Target audience.** Families & young shoppers

**Type.** Event

**The big idea.** A tentpole seasonal festival that fills the mall: hero installation, stage programming, tenant tie-ins, and a social wall driving dwell time.

**Key deliverables.**
- Festival concept + zoning
- Hero installation
- Stage + performances
- Tenant campaign
- Daily content

**KPIs.** Footfall uplift · tenant sales lift · social mentions

**Indicative budget.** MMK 60–120M (~S$17–34k) · market FX, RFQ vendors, 50% deposit

**ROI angle.** Footfall + tenant sales measured vs baseline weeks.

**ZYNTH edge.** End-to-end: concept → build → content.
