# Shwe Pay — The New Gold Standard ★ full client-ready

**Sector.** Banking & Finance / Fintech (digital wallet)

**Market.** Myanmar (Yangon)

**Type.** Integrated launch — campaign + flagship event

**The big idea.** In a market of wallets that belong to the banks, Shwe Pay becomes the one that belongs to *you* — money that moves as fast as your ambition. **The New Gold Standard** (Shwe = gold), carried from positioning → creative → the Launch Night → the KPI line.

**Key deliverables.**
- Hero brand film "The New Gold Standard"
- #MyGoldStandard UGC challenge
- "Launch Night" flagship event (150–200 + creators)
- Gold Moves reels · 6 KOLs incl. 1 top-tier
- OOH (Yangon) · university/mall roadshow · 8-week paid

**KPIs.** 120k–180k installs · 45–55% first-transaction activation · CPA ≤ MMK 650 · 2,000–3,500 merchants

**Investment (3 tiers).** Essential 48M · **Signature 98M (recommended)** · Flagship 168M MMK · 50% deposit · market FX.

**ZYNTH edge.** One idea carried the whole way through; competes on meaning, not a cashback war scale would win.
