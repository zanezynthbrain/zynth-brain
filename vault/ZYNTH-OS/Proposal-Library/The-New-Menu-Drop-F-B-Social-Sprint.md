# The New Menu Drop — F&B Social Sprint

**Sector.** Food & Beverage / Restaurant chains

**Target audience.** Urban foodies 18–34

**Type.** Campaign

**The big idea.** Launch a menu like a sneaker drop: countdown, creator tastings, limited-time hooks, and reels built for the first 3 seconds.

**Key deliverables.**
- Drop concept
- Food-styling shoot
- 10–15 reels
- Creator tastings
- Store POS tie-in

**KPIs.** Reach · footfall on drop week · new-item mix %

**Indicative budget.** MMK 15–35M (~S$4–10k) · market FX, RFQ vendors, 50% deposit

**ROI angle.** Drop-week sales vs normal week = clean ROI read.

**ZYNTH edge.** Batch shoot → a month of content.
