# ZYNTH-MM-PUB-001 — City-Pulse-Pavilion — City Pulse Pavilion
## Internal Proposal Seed V01

**Status:** Internal Concept; not approved for external release  
**Market:** Myanmar
**Event family:** Public activation, mall activation, roadshow, city installation and brand experience

## Executive Summary

A modular public pavilion that converts participation into a visible collective light signal, making the activation understandable in seconds, rewarding to dwell in and naturally shareable without becoming only a selfie object.

## Strategic Opportunity

The design is intended to create a clear journey from first recognition to meaningful participation or qualified conversation. It is an internal ZYNTH portfolio concept and must be adapted to a named client, venue, audience and verified objective before any external proposal is sent.

## Experience Concept

The proposed journey is **Open threshold → participation object → story wall → collective pulse moment → social/share or community handoff**. The spatial system should make the event purpose legible within seconds while reserving a deeper layer for people who choose to dwell. The concept is not a promise of outcomes; it is a designed mechanism whose results depend on content, operations, audience fit and execution quality.

## Spatial Programme

| Zone | Role | Initial design requirement |
|---|---|---|
| Arrival / orientation | Establish purpose and reduce hesitation | Clear threshold and sightline |
| Core experience | Deliver the signature interaction or story | One focal mechanism, not competing objects |
| Content / proof | Make the message understandable | Verified, editable content only |
| Conversion / support | Route the audience to the next action | Staffed and measurable handoff |
| Operations | Protect production and staff movement | Service, storage, power and safety separation |

## Required Deliverables

The internal package will include five visual views, a plan/zone diagram, material and lighting direction, camera/sightline record, feasibility sheet, source-file specification, export matrix, run-of-show logic and a full client proposal after discovery. The current visual anchor remains an **Internal Concept** until source geometry, assumptions and review status are documented.

## Commercial Planning Band

**Indicative planning band:** MMK 45–125M excluding site, fabrication, permits, staffing and media. This is not a quotation and excludes the items stated above. A final budget requires a brief, venue, quantities, technical scope, supplier inputs, tax treatment, schedule and founder approval.

## Content, People and Operations

The delivery team is expected to include a creative lead, spatial/3D lead, producer, technical director or vendor, content lead and client-side decision owner. Staffing, accessibility support, security, registration, moderation, cleaning, power, contingency and measurement must be planned as part of the event system.

## Measurement Logic

Measurement should be selected after the objective is named. Possible measures include qualified conversations, dwell time, participation completion, route completion, registrations, content interactions, event satisfaction, remote participation quality, lead quality and follow-up conversion. No KPI is guaranteed by the render alone.

## Risks and Assumptions

Crowd density, weather, permits, power, staffing, safeguarding, privacy of participant data, content moderation and accessibility must be validated.
