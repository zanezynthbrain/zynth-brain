# The Next Tier — Premium Fintech Launch

**Sector.** Banking & Fintech

**Target audience.** Young professionals, SME owners, HNWIs (Yangon)

**Type.** Event

**The big idea.** An invitation-only 'initiation' night that turns a premium banking tier into a membership people want to be seen holding — LED arrival, cinematic reveal, SME success panel.

**Key deliverables.**
- 250-guest launch
- Stage + LED + projection
- QR sign-up wall
- Hype film + cutdowns
- Press & KOL kit

**KPIs.** ≥250 premium sign-ups in 30 days · 5M+ earned impressions · ≥10 tier-1 features

**Indicative budget.** MMK 110–130M (~S$31–37k) · market FX, RFQ vendors, 50% deposit

**ROI angle.** Every guest has a unique QR → sign-ups attributed to the room, not guessed.

**ZYNTH edge.** Attribution + market-FX pricing + IGNITE-standard production.
