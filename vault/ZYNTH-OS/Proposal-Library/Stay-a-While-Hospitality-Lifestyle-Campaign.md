# Stay a While — Hospitality Lifestyle Campaign

**Sector.** Hospitality, Hotels & Travel

**Target audience.** Local staycationers & regional travellers

**Type.** Campaign

**The big idea.** Sell the feeling of arrival: a lifestyle content engine + influencer stays + a direct-booking offer that beats the OTAs.

**Key deliverables.**
- Content strategy
- Property film + reels
- Influencer stays
- Direct-booking offer
- Monthly report

**KPIs.** Direct bookings · social reach · RevPAR lift on offer

**Indicative budget.** MMK 20–40M/mo (~S$6–11k) · market FX, RFQ vendors, 50% deposit

**ROI angle.** Direct bookings from campaign codes = attributable revenue.

**ZYNTH edge.** Travel storytelling that converts.
