### INTEGRATED DIGITAL MARKETING CAMPAIGN PLAN (OCTOBER 2026)

#### 1. CAMPAIGN OVERVIEW

The KPay Thadingyut Shwe-Yaung-Lin (Golden Light) campaign is a high-impact, integrated digital marketing initiative designed to position KPay as the primary facilitator of tradition, gratitude, and commerce during Myanmar’s most significant festival of lights. As the nation transitions into the 2026 festive season, this campaign leverages the deep-rooted cultural practice of "Kan-Taw" (paying respects to elders) and the peak consumer spending period to drive aggressive user acquisition and transaction growth.

#### 2. TARGET AUDIENCE PROFILE

To achieve the ambitious target of 50,000 premium sign-ups, the campaign focuses on three high-value segments that drive the digital economy in Myanmar.

#### Segment A: The Urban "Gratitude" Gen Z & Millennials

Demographics: Age 22-35; Male/Female; Single or newly married; Income 800,000+ MMK/month.
Location: Yangon (Hlaing, Bahan, Kamayut), Mandalay.
Psychographics: Value modern convenience but are deeply traditional. They feel a strong obligation to "Kan-Taw" their parents and teachers but are often time-poor or physically distant from them. They aspire to be seen as successful and generous.
Behaviors: Heavy TikTok and Facebook users. Early adopters of social commerce. Use mobile wallets for food delivery, mobile top-ups, and streaming subscriptions.
Pain Points: The hassle of finding clean, new banknotes for Thadingyut gifting; the risk of carrying large amounts of cash during festivals; the "guilt" of not being able to visit home.
Media Consumption: TikTok (evening peaks 8 PM - 11 PM), Facebook (all-day browsing), Telegram (news/community groups).

#### Segment B: The "Thriving" SME Owners

Demographics: Age 28-45; Business owners (fashion, electronics, food, beauty); Income 2,000,000+ MMK/month.
Location: Major commercial hubs in Yangon (Downtown, Mingalar Taung Nyunt) and Mandalay.
Psychographics: Focused on efficiency, business growth, and financial security. They want to capitalize on the Thadingyut spending surge and reduce operational friction.
Behaviors: Use Facebook for business promotion. They prefer digital payments to avoid the security risks of cash handling and the chronic difficulty of getting small change in Myanmar.
Pain Points: High transaction fees on other platforms; lack of integrated marketing support; slow settlement times.
Media Consumption: Facebook Business Suite, Viber communities, local business news portals (e.g., Myanmar Business Today).

#### Segment C: The "Modern Elders" (Premium Referrals)

Demographics: Age 50+; Parents of Segment A; High net worth or retired professionals.
Location: Urban residential areas (Golden Valley, North Dagon).
Psychographics: Increasingly tech-curious but need trust and security. They enjoy receiving gifts and "pocket money" from their children and want to feel "included" in the digital age.
Behaviors: Use Facebook to stay connected with family. Watch long-form video content (Facebook Watch). Use Viber for messaging.
Pain Points: Fear of digital fraud; complex UI/UX in financial apps; dependence on children for tech support.
Media Consumption: Facebook (Morning 7 AM - 9 AM), Viber, traditional TV (occasionally).

#### 3. COMPETITIVE LANDSCAPE

The Myanmar mobile payment market in 2026 is a duopoly under pressure, with KPay and WavePay leading, followed by niche bank-led wallets.

| Competitor | Thadingyut Strategy | Gaps & Opportunities for KPay |
|---|---|---|
| WavePay | Heavy focus on rural agent networks and cash-out promotions. Usually runs "Spin the Wheel" games with low-value prizes. | Gap: Their UI feels "utility-heavy" and lacks emotional resonance. KPay Opportunity: Focus on the "premium" and "lifestyle" aspects of gifting. |
| OnePay | Partnerships with high-end malls (Junction City, City Mall) for flat cashback. | Gap: Limited reach outside Tier-1 retail. KPay Opportunity: Dominate the "Digital Kan-Taw" P2P space which is higher in volume. |
| AYA Pay / CB Pay | Focus on interest rates and fixed deposit integrations. | Gap: Perceived as "too corporate" and slow for the festive mood. KPay Opportunity: Be the "fast, social, and celebratory" wallet. |
| MPT Money | Bundled data and top-up offers for MPT users. | Gap: Lack of a strong merchant ecosystem and social features. KPay Opportunity: Leverage the KBZ ecosystem (merchants + banking trust). |

Strategic Advantage: KPay’s integration with the KBZ Bank ecosystem provides a "Premium" perception that WavePay lacks. This campaign will exploit that by positioning KPay as the only way to send "Golden Blessings" (Shwe-Yaung-Su) that carry more prestige than simple cash.

#### 4. CAMPAIGN STRATEGY

#### The Big Idea: "Shwe-Yaung-Lin" (The Golden Light of Gratitude)

Thadingyut is the "Festival of Lights," symbolizing the Buddha's descent from heaven. Traditionally, it is a time of illumination and showing gratitude. Our strategy is to digitize the light. We will position every KPay transaction—whether a gift to a parent or a payment to a merchant—as a "spark of light" that contributes to a brighter, more prosperous Myanmar.

#### Brand Positioning

KPay is the "Enabler of Modern Traditions." We aren't replacing the traditional Thadingyut; we are making it more beautiful, secure, and rewarding through technology. We are the bridge between the physical candle and the digital gift.

#### Key Messages

"Send Gratitude Instantly:" No need for new banknotes. Send "Digital Kan-Taw" with customized Thadingyut "Golden Envelopes" in the KPay app.
"Illuminate Your Savings:" Get exclusive 10-20% cashback when you shop at KPay partner merchants for Thadingyut gifts.
"The Gift that Gives Back:" Every new premium sign-up during the festival enters a draw to win "Shwe-Yaung-Su" (Golden Prizes) totaling 20,000,000 MMK.
"Empowering the Spark:" Supporting local SMEs by driving traffic to their stores through the KPay merchant network.

#### Tone of Voice

Warm & Respectful: For the "Kan-Taw" and emotional aspects.
Vibrant & Innovative: For the tech features and TikTok challenges.
High-Energy: For the lucky draws and merchant promotions.
Language: Modern Burmese (colloquial for social) and Professional Burmese (for SME/Business).

#### Campaign Hashtags

Primary: #KPayShweThadingyut
Secondary: #DigitalKanTaw #KPayLightUp2026 #ShweYaungLin
