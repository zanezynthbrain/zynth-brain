**Status date:** 15 August 2026  
**Purpose:** Give the Managing Director a truthful view of what ZYNTH can prepare now, what is already operating continuously, and what must happen before work becomes a real client commitment.

> **Operating principle:** ZYNTH can autonomously research, analyse, plan, draft, improve and prepare internal creative work. It cannot contact a client, publish, spend money, book a vendor, release paid media, or begin client production until the founder approves a named project and the relevant release decision.

ZYNTH is now capable of acting as an internal strategy, proposal, creative-development and operating-support workforce for Myanmar and Singapore. The system has a live inventory of **23 runtime agent entries**, **36 versioned skills**, **12 standing founder knowledge documents**, **37 proposal-pool records**, **two full client-grade proposal exemplars**, and **seven existing creative-output files**. This is most valuable when a client needs to clarify, launch, grow, create cultural relevance, produce a brand experience, create content/motion work, or bridge Myanmar and Singapore.

## 6. Founder decision sequence

The path from an internal idea to professional client work should remain simple and controlled.

| Stage | System creates | Founder decides | External action permitted? |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Explore** | Research, account card, opportunity memo, concept package or generic proposal territory. | Whether the opportunity deserves deeper work. | No. |
| **Shape** | Full internal proposal draft, creative territory, implementation and budget assumptions. | Whether to create/approve a named project and commercial direction. | No. |
| **Validate** | Cost/RFQ checklist, risk/rights questions, client-input list and final proposal version. | Scope, commercial terms, release readiness and proposal owner. | Only after explicit approval. |
| **Mobilise** | Production brief, task plan, creative queue preparation and delivery governance. | Contract/deposit, production route and each material commitment. | Only under the approved project controls. |
| **Learn** | Results review, lessons, case-study evidence and improvement tasks. | What to repeat, improve or retire. | Case study/publication still requires approval. |
