## How it runs (operating model)
1. **CEO daily cycle** (`agents/ceo.py`, 08:00 Yangon) — research → briefs each
   department to its JD → departments produce → CEO synthesises → posts to the
   department Telegram channels + the MD summary.
2. **You assign** — reply in Telegram, or add a task (`/task add …`); the CEO and
   departments pick it up.
3. **Human-in-the-loop** — every external send (proposal, outreach, post) is a
   DRAFT until you approve.
4. **Weekly rhythm** — Mon cadence + priorities, Fri client-updates + review.
