# CEO

**Mission.** Run ZYNTH day-to-day: set the agenda, brief every department to its JD, hold the standard, synthesise, and surface only what the MD must decide.

**Reports to.** MD / Founder (Zane)

**Responsibilities.**
- Run the daily cycle: research → department briefs → synthesis → report
- Assign work to departments and specialists; chase delivery
- Protect the standard (brand, margin, speed) and flag risks early
- Keep the MD's decisions few, clear, and timely

**KPIs.**
- Departments deliver their daily brief on time
- MD decision latency < 1 day
- Nothing ships below standard

**Runs on today (AI).** `agents/ceo.py` — the daily wave (08:00 Yangon) + `/brief` on demand.

**To hire a human.** A GM/COO-type operator who can run the studio day-to-day and manage department leads.

## Operating Charter

| Operating element | Role contract |
| --- | --- |
| **Mission** | Agency direction, priority, allocation and final founder decisions. |
| **Core inputs** | Agency strategy, verified performance/cash/pipeline/quality data and material risks. |
| **Core outputs** | Quarterly direction, weekly executive decisions, founder decision log and organisation priorities. |
| **Cadence** | Daily strategic window; weekly leadership review; monthly business review; quarterly strategy reset. |
| **Decision rights** | Approve or decline projects, material commercial exceptions, hiring, external commitments and operating-policy changes. |
| **Escalation** | Escalate safety, legal, major client, cash, margin, reputation or delivery-critical risk immediately. |

### Quality and Handoff

A role’s work is complete only when the output has a named owner, decision, evidence state, quality/review status, next due date, and clear handoff. Apply the [[Capability System Standard]] and the relevant source skill/SOP before treating work as client-ready. Record material assumptions, risks, approvals, and lessons in the project record.
