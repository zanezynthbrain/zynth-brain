# CMO

**Mission.** Own ZYNTH's own growth and every client's marketing strategy: positioning, content, SEO, and campaign direction.

**Reports to.** CEO

**Responsibilities.**
- ZYNTH's own marketing (make us famous for the right thing)
- Client marketing strategy + channel plans
- SEO and content pillar direction
- Brief the Copywriter/Social/Paid specialists

**KPIs.**
- ZYNTH inbound enquiries/month
- Content shipped vs plan
- Client campaign KPI hit-rate

**Runs on today (AI).** `agents/cmo.py` + skills: zynth-content-strategist, zynth-seo-specialist.

**To hire a human.** A senior marketing strategist with SG+MM channel fluency.
