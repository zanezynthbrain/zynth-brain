# Creative Director

**Mission.** Own the ideas and the craft: distinctive concepts, art direction, and the standard for all creative output.

**Reports to.** CEO

**Responsibilities.**
- Big creative ideas / campaign platforms
- Art direction and creative QC
- Direct Copywriter, Art Director, Video Producer
- Guard 'distinctive, not generic'

**KPIs.**
- Concept approval rate
- Creative that beats category benchmark
- Portfolio pieces produced

**Runs on today (AI).** creative pipeline + skills: zynth-creative-director, zynth-art-director, zynth-creative-video-director.

**To hire a human.** A creative director with a strong regional portfolio.

## Operating Charter

| Operating element | Role contract |
| --- | --- |
| **Mission** | Creative quality, distinctive ideas, brand coherence and the bridge from strategy to craft. |
| **Core inputs** | Approved strategic brief, brand/context truth, audience insight, market references, scope, feasibility and approvals. |
| **Core outputs** | Creative territories, selected direction, rationale, quality review, production implication and presentation story. |
| **Cadence** | Concept boards weekly; pre-client quality gate; production review; portfolio/learning review. |
| **Decision rights** | Select internal creative route; founder/client approves major direction, scope, rights-sensitive work and release. |
| **Escalation** | Escalate strategic ambiguity, generic work, brand/cultural/rights risk, budget/buildability conflict or client misalignment. |

### Quality and Handoff

A role’s work is complete only when the output has a named owner, decision, evidence state, quality/review status, next due date, and clear handoff. Apply the [[Capability System Standard]] and the relevant source skill/SOP before treating work as client-ready. Record material assumptions, risks, approvals, and lessons in the project record.
