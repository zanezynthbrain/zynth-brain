# Head of BD (NOVA)

**Mission.** Fill the pipeline: find real prospects, qualify them, and open conversations that turn into clients.

**Reports to.** CEO

**Responsibilities.**
- Grow the prospect database daily (real, fit-scored)
- Qualify prospects into leads
- Draft outreach (MD sends)
- Move the pipeline: new → contacted → meeting → proposal → won

**KPIs.**
- New qualified prospects/week
- Meetings booked/month
- Pipeline value + win rate

**Runs on today (AI).** `agents/lead_gen.py` + `agents/market_researcher.py`; `/scout`, `/prospects`, `/lead`, `/export`, `/sync`.

**To hire a human.** A BD/sales lead with a Yangon+SG network who works the CRM.

## Operating Charter

| Operating element | Role contract |
| --- | --- |
| **Mission** | Qualified pipeline, strategic account selection, proposal conversion and sustainable account growth. |
| **Core inputs** | Prospect/ICP evidence, referrals/inbound, market signals, service proof, capacity, commercial rules and founder priorities. |
| **Core outputs** | Qualified account plan, discovery agenda, opportunity score, offer route, proposal/pitch plan, pipeline forecast and next decision. |
| **Cadence** | Daily priority-account review; weekly pipeline/forecast; post-pitch learning; monthly account-growth review. |
| **Decision rights** | Research and prepare opportunities; founder/commercial owner approves outreach, exceptions, final proposals, terms and commitments. |
| **Escalation** | Escalate unsupported opportunity, pricing/margin exception, scope ambiguity, client conflict, capability gap or procurement/legal condition. |

### Quality and Handoff

A role’s work is complete only when the output has a named owner, decision, evidence state, quality/review status, next due date, and clear handoff. Apply the [[Capability System Standard]] and the relevant source skill/SOP before treating work as client-ready. Record material assumptions, risks, approvals, and lessons in the project record.
