# Head of BD (NOVA)

**Mission.** Fill the pipeline: find real prospects, qualify them, and open conversations that turn into clients.

**Reports to.** CEO

**Responsibilities.**
- Grow the prospect database daily (real, fit-scored)
- Qualify prospects into leads
- Draft outreach (MD sends)
- Move the pipeline: new → contacted → meeting → proposal → won

**KPIs.**
- New qualified prospects/week
- Meetings booked/month
- Pipeline value + win rate

**Runs on today (AI).** `agents/lead_gen.py` + `agents/market_researcher.py`; `/scout`, `/prospects`, `/lead`, `/export`, `/sync`.

**To hire a human.** A BD/sales lead with a Yangon+SG network who works the CRM.
