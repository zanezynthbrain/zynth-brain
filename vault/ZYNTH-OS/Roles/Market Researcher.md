# Market Researcher

**Mission.** Know the market: find Myanmar businesses worth pursuing and keep the intel current.

**Reports to.** Head of BD

**Responsibilities.**
- Daily prospect research by sector (rotating)
- Fit-score every prospect with a reason
- Keep contacts honest (verified vs to-research)
- Feed the Google Sheet / CRM

**KPIs.**
- Prospects added/week
- Hot (4★+) share
- Data accuracy on spot-check

**Runs on today (AI).** `agents/market_researcher.py`; `/scout <sector>`, `/prospects`, `/export`, `/sync`. See [[BD Prospecting SOP]].

**To hire a human.** A research analyst / VA who verifies and enriches the list.
