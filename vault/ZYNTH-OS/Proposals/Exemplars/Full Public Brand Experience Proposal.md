# Full Proposal Exemplar — **Money, Made Human**

**Prepared for:** Illustrative client, ARC Financial  
**Market:** Yangon, Myanmar  
**Proposal status:** Internal exemplar; concept and costs are indicative pending client discovery, venue, permit, compliance and RFQ confirmation  
**Decision requested:** Approve a six-week discovery-and-design phase for a public financial-confidence experience.

## 1. Executive Decision Summary

ARC engages ZYNTH to create **Money, Made Human**, a one-day public brand experience that makes everyday financial confidence feel useful, personal and approachable—without using an event as a disguised product-sale exercise. The recommended experience turns real life aspirations into voluntary, interactive conversations and approved tools, then gives ARC a consent-based, measurable relationship path.

The headline idea is a “life decisions festival,” not a corporate fair. Visitors choose the future they are building—starting a small business, supporting family, moving home, learning a skill, or building a buffer—and move through practical experience zones. The signature moment, the **Future Postcard Wall**, turns anonymous guest promises into a live kinetic visual, making collective ambition visible without displaying personal financial data.

The indicative standard-scale programme is MMK 320–520 million before tax. It requires ARC compliance approval, an accessible venue, public-safety/permit route, partner confirmation, verified supplier RFQs, and client-owned data/consent controls.

## 2. Client Challenge and Objective

Financial brands often communicate through abstract numbers or overly formal products. ARC needs to create awareness and trust among people who do not yet see financial planning as relevant to their own life. The business objective is to build qualified brand consideration and permission-based next steps; the social objective is to make useful financial capability more accessible.

The experience must not provide unqualified individual advice, make return promises, collect unnecessary personal data, or imply that product participation is required to access value. Every product statement, tool, consent flow and financial-education claim requires ARC compliance approval.

## 5. Concept Explanation

Money, Made Human is a public experience where money becomes a tool for life rather than a source of anxiety. Guests choose an aspiration at arrival and receive a simple journey card. They then explore four short zones: **Start Small**, **Make Space**, **Build Your Buffer**, and **Pass It Forward**. Each zone features an approved practical interaction, a partner or staff conversation route, and a next step that does not require a purchase.

The visual world uses warm, contemporary materials and visible “future threads” connecting individual choices across the site. The signature Future Postcard Wall collects voluntary, moderated messages. Guests can write a future promise, select a visual symbol, or opt out. The final projection is anonymous by default and subject to clear consent, moderation and accessibility design.

| Participate | Future Postcard Wall, short talks, creative activity | Shareable but privacy-safe signature moment |

The stage programme uses 15-minute micro-sessions rather than long panels: “Starting a side income responsibly,” “A family conversation about the future,” “What to ask before a major financial choice,” and “How to build a practical buffer.” Speakers, scripts, slides and Q&A moderation require compliance and content approval.

## 11. Commercial Model and Terms

This is client-funded awareness and brand-trust work. A sponsor-funded extension is possible only if sponsor rights, relevance, category conflict, fulfilment, data, guest value, and sponsor return are separately designed and approved. ZYNTH proposes a 50% commencement deposit, 30% after final design/RFQ approval, and 20% on final delivery; all supplier bookings require client written approval and appropriate pre-funding.

The scope includes two consolidated review rounds per agreed output. New zones, late creative changes, increased capacity, new partner requirements, new event days, or unapproved production changes require a written change order. ARC owns approved final client assets according to agreed rights; vendor, music, talent, visual, source-file and personal-data rights are recorded separately.
