# Myanmar Pharmaceutical Marketing Campaign Research

**Prepared for:** ZYNTH Myanmar pharmaceutical campaign proposal  
**Status:** Directional research for strategy and proposal development; legal and medical review remains mandatory.

## Regulatory sources

The Myanmar Food and Drug Administration publishes the National Drug Law issued on 30 October 1992 and links the official document from its website: https://www.fda.gov.mm/?p=176. The extracted English translation indicates that a person intending to manufacture, import, export, store, distribute or sell pharmaceutical raw material or drugs must register the relevant drug with the Board of Authority in the prescribed manner. It also states that parties granted registration or a licence must follow the authority’s orders, directives and conditions regarding labelling and advertising.

The International Bar Association Healthcare and Life Sciences Law Committee’s 2024 Myanmar survey identifies the National Drug Law No. 7 of 1992, Notification No. 7/1993 on Drug Labelling, and the Myanmar Medical Council’s Medical Ethics Guideline as relevant sources for pharmaceutical promotion. It identifies FDA Myanmar under the Ministry of Health as the primary regulatory and supervisory authority. It describes advertising broadly and reports that OTC drugs can be advertised to the public only after obtaining a Drug Registration Certificate from FDA Myanmar. Source: https://www.ibanet.org/document?id=Healthcare-Survey-Responses-2024-Myanmar

## Local consumer-health evidence

Yee Yee Swe’s 2025 Yangon study, *Factors Affecting Consumer Buying Decision Towards OTC Drugs in Yangon, Myanmar*, examines physician/pharmacist recommendations, digital marketing, price, country of origin and previous experience as variables associated with OTC purchase decisions. The study text describes brand reputation, price, availability, safety, effectiveness, family or pharmacy advice and prior experience as relevant considerations. It also discusses varying health literacy and access to expert counsel, including the risk of incorrect information from unregulated online sources. Source: https://meral.edu.mm/record/11971/file_preview/Yee%20Yee%20Swe%20MMM%20II%20-%2045%20MMM%203rd%20Batch.pdf?allow_aggs=True

The study is directional local evidence focused on Yangon and is not treated as a national market census, clinical evidence or a substitute for client research. Its use in the proposal is to inform hypotheses about clarity, trust, pharmacy influence, availability, price sensitivity and responsible digital information.

## Strategic implications for ZYNTH

A safe portfolio campaign should use a registered OTC product placeholder and avoid invented product facts. The campaign should make approved information easier to understand, connect digital content to qualified pharmacy or professional guidance, protect consumers from diagnosis-by-content and misinformation, and establish a measurement system that distinguishes attention from qualified consideration.

All live executions require the client’s current registration status, approved label, approved claims, warnings, dosage, contraindications, distribution permissions, medical/regulatory review, professional translation, legal review where required and founder/client release approval.
