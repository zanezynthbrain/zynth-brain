# ZYNTH NORTH STAR — read this when you feel lost

This one page reconciles the **business** (what ZYNTH sells) with the
**system** (the AI tools we built). If you're overwhelmed, start here.

---

## The core truth (from your own playbook)
> An agency sells **billable brain-hours, at a margin.** You are not selling
> design, video, or events — you sell time, judgment, and the *reliability*
> of a repeatable system.

The AI system is **not the business.** It is a *tool inside* the business —
it makes you faster and more consistent at a few stages. The business is
still: win clients → deliver → prove results → keep them.

## The ZYNTH Value Chain — and where the AI helps
```
1. ATTRACT   → leads            [AI helps: /bd finds prospects]
2. QUALIFY   → is this a fit?    [YOU decide]
3. SCOPE     → what/what not     [AI drafts; YOU set boundaries]
4. PRICE     → the number        [AI: /proposal /event with real pricing]  ← strong
5. DELIVER   → make the work     [MOSTLY YOU + your team + vendors]         ← AI weak
6. PROVE     → results in data   [not built yet — this is where agencies die]
7. EXPAND    → retainer/referral [not built yet]
```
**The AI is strongest at stages 1, 3, 4** (finding, scoping, pricing, proposing).
**Stages 5–7 are the actual business** and are mostly YOUR job. Don't confuse a
great proposal engine with a running agency.
