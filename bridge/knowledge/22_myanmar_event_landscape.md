## Cultural calendar → campaign windows
- **Thingyan (Myanmar New Year, mid-April):** the biggest festival. The holiday
  week itself is a *business-quiet* period (offices shut); the **4–6 weeks before**
  are a peak campaign window (New-Year brand pushes, retail, beverage).
- **Buddhist Lent / Waso (≈Jul–Oct):** traditionally quiet — no weddings, reduced
  festivities. Good for B2B, corporate, education (school intake), CSR; weak for
  celebratory consumer activations.
- **Thadingyut (Festival of Lights, Oct):** gifting + retail + family season —
  strong for FMCG, retail, malls, telco.
- **Tazaungdaing (Nov, incl. Taunggyi balloon festival):** lights + festivals —
  experiential, beverage, telco sponsorship season.
- **Christmas / New Year (Dec–Jan):** urban, retail, hospitality, youth.
- **School intake (≈Jun):** education enrolment campaigns peak.

## Event types by industry
- **Banking & fintech:** product/app launches, CSR events, agent/partner events,
  sponsorships, financial-literacy roadshows.
- **Telecom:** roadshows, concerts, campus activations, retail-store events, esports.
- **FMCG / beverage:** concerts, sampling activations, trade events, festival tie-ins.
- **Real estate:** launch events, show-unit experiences, investor functions.
- **Automotive:** showroom launches, test-drive experiences, auto shows.
- **Retail / malls:** seasonal festivals, store openings, tenant activations.

## Decision-makers & procurement (per sector)
- **Banking:** Head of Marketing & Corporate Communications; wallet/app Marketing Lead.
- **Telecom:** Head of Brand / Marketing Communications; Digital & Content Lead.
- **FMCG / beverage:** Brand Manager / Trade Marketing Manager (per brand).
- **Real estate:** Head of Marketing / Launch & Brand Lead.
- **Automotive:** Marketing Manager / Head of Sales & Marketing (per franchise).
- **Procurement style:** relationship-first, personal networks matter; larger
  budgets go through RFQ / multi-vendor pitch. Warm intro > cold pitch.
