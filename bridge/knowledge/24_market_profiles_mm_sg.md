## MYANMAR
- **Population:** ~55M, median age 28. **Internet:** ~40%, mobile-first. **FX:** 1 USD ≈ 4,400 MMK.
- **Platforms:** Facebook (#1 by far — "essentially the internet"), TikTok (growing fast), Viber
  (messaging), YouTube, Instagram (urban youth), LinkedIn (minimal).
- **Media rates (MMK) `[UNVERIFIED — Manus]`:**
  - Facebook/IG CPM 2,000–8,000 ($0.45–1.80) · TikTok CPM 3,000–10,000 · Google Ads CPC 500–2,000 ($0.11–0.45)
  - Billboard (Yangon prime) 3–8M/month · Radio 30s 100,000–500,000 · Newspaper full page 2–5M
  - Influencer: micro (10–50K) 200,000–500,000/post · mid (50–200K) 500,000–2,000,000/post · top (200K+) 2M–10M/post
- **Cultural calendar:** see `22_myanmar_event_landscape.md`. Buddhist Lent (Jul–Oct) = conservative, CSR-focused.
- **Regulations:** Myanmar Cyber Security Law; **no political content**; 2025 Motion Picture Law (video);
  Unicode (Pyidaungsu) fonts only.
- **Competitors (digital) `[UNVERIFIED — Manus]`:** Mango Digital, Jenius, Bindez, Amara Digital.
  Event competitors: Blink, MEMC.
- **Tips:** mobile-first everything; prioritise Facebook; Viber for direct/service; Myanmar language for
  reach, English for business/expat; avoid political content.

## SINGAPORE
- **Population:** ~5.9M, highly affluent. **Internet:** 96%, multi-device. **FX:** 1 USD ≈ 1.35 SGD.
- **Platforms:** Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, LinkedIn (B2B), YouTube, WhatsApp, Telegram.
- **Media rates (SGD) `[UNVERIFIED — Manus]`:**
  - FB/IG CPM 5–15 · TikTok CPM 8–20 · LinkedIn CPM 15–40 · Google Ads CPC 1–8 (competitive)
  - Billboard (Orchard) 15,000–50,000/mo · MRT 5,000–30,000/mo · Straits Times full page 20,000–40,000 · Radio 30s 500–2,000
  - Influencer: micro 200–800 · mid 1,000–5,000 · macro 5,000–30,000+ per post
- **Cultural calendar:** CNY (Jan/Feb), Hari Raya, Deepavali (Oct/Nov), National Day (Aug 9), Christmas,
  Great Singapore Sale (Jun–Aug).
- **Regulations:** PDPA (data), IMDA guidelines, ASAS advertising standards.
- **Competitors:** Ogilvy, BBDO, Dentsu, Publicis + local boutiques. High competition → higher CPC/CPM;
  quality + targeting crucial. Target high-value segments with premium messaging.
