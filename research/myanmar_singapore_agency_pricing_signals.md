# Myanmar and Singapore Agency Pricing Signals

**Research date:** 15 August 2026  
**Use:** External planning signals for ZYNTH package architecture. These are published estimates or offers, not a substitute for ZYNTH’s own cost build, partner RFQ, client discovery, tax review, or commercial approval.

## Myanmar Signals

A Myanmar pricing guide published by Marketing Myanmar presents 2024–2025 ranges for social management, SEO, branding, website, video, strategy, and advisory work. It describes full-service social management at MMK 2–3 million+ monthly, a full brand system/strategy at MMK 8–15 million+, and consulting workshops at MMK 1.5–6 million, while explicitly separating paid-media spend from agency fees. It recommends cost-plus pricing, a target gross margin, scope/revision control, deposits, and currency-risk provisions. [1]

Kabar Kyaw Marketing’s 2026 guide presents a broader USD-denominated Myanmar digital market, estimating established-agency social management at USD 400–1,000 monthly and full-service retainers at USD 1,000–3,000+ monthly; it also distinguishes management fees from paid spend and emphasises conversion infrastructure, account ownership, and realistic evaluation windows. [2]

These two sources are not fully consistent. This is expected because they address different agency tiers, currencies, client types, and scopes. ZYNTH should therefore use a **three-layer price architecture**: a costed local-MMK baseline, a USD or SGD regional/MNC basis, and a bespoke project/event RFQ basis.

## Singapore Signals

Aii Develop publicly advertises a paid-social starter package beginning at SGD 1,500 per month with a three-month minimum, while reserving enterprise packages for custom scoping. [3] Brew Interactive’s 2026 pricing guide recommends objective-led scoping rather than channel-first buying and lists broad monthly service ranges in USD, with social activity ranging from USD 1,000 to 20,000+ depending on scope and video; it separately notes management fee, ad spend, and conversion/measurement factors. [4]

A Singapore influencer-agency founder interview added a practitioner signal rather than a verified market benchmark: the guest described a SGD 3,000–5,000 initial 2–3-month creator test and wide creator-rate variance. This is one operator’s view and must never be represented as a standard Singapore rate. [5]

## ZYNTH Pricing Principles Derived from the Evidence

1. Price **strategy and diagnosis** separately from execution. Do not give senior thinking away as an unpriced “pitch.”
2. Separate agency fee, paid media, production, creators/talent, venue, technology, travel, printing, permits, taxes, and contingency in every client proposal.
3. Use standardised packages only for repeatable outcomes; use discovery and a bottom-up costed scope for launches, events, 3D, video, sponsorship, and multi-market work.
4. Publish a starting investment where it filters unqualified enquiries; retain a custom route for larger or regulated work.
5. Protect contribution margin through revision limits, a change-order process, deposits, payment milestones, and a defined currency/FX rule.
6. Never promise ROI, reach, sales, or event attendance without the relevant baseline, controls, spend, tracking, and client-operation dependencies.
