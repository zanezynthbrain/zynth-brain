## 1. The headline finding

**At full scale, IGNITE does not clear the 35% floor until 76% of sponsorship
inventory is sold.** For a first-edition event with no prior audience data, that
is an unsafe bar to plan against.

**At lean scale, the same inventory clears the floor at 43% and the 40% target at
47%** — a bar a first edition can realistically hit.

**Recommendation: launch lean, upgrade with money in the bank.** The inventory and
the sponsor-facing price list stay identical in both cases; only ZYNTH's cost base
changes. Every tier sold above 47% converts almost entirely to margin.

## 7. Cash & terms
- **R2 applies to sponsors:** 50% on signature, 50% by 31 October. No logo goes to
  print before the first 50% clears.
- Vendor deposits (venue, AV, catering) are typically 30–50% — these land **before**
  sponsor second instalments. Sequence G1 money against venue deposit specifically.
- **R3:** IGNITE must not draw ZYNTH below 3 months runway. At lean scale the maximum
  exposure before G2 is the venue + AV deposits (~9M / USD 2,050) — acceptable.

## 8. What must be verified before any of this goes out

1. One real quote each: venue, catering per-pax, AV package, staffing.
2. Whether a hotel will take Gold in-kind for the venue — **do this first**.
3. Delegate ticket price against comparable Yangon summits (120k is an assumption).
4. Whether 300 is the right room size for the venue actually booked.
5. Sponsor tier pricing tested against **two** real conversations before publishing.
